# ELEVATE introduction video: narration script (DRAFT for approval)

Length about 3 minutes (about 430 words). One voice. Screens are fake-data pictures of the real app, animated in Remotion
(see `tools/recap-video/`). Rule: the narration says "ELITE Resource Services", never "ERS". Acronyms are spelled the way they
are said (the build script checks for a standalone "ERS" and fails).

| # | Screen | Narration |
|---|--------|-----------|
| 1 | Logo, then the dashboard | Welcome to ELEVATE, the home for everyone at ELITE Resource Services. Your schedule, your time, your documents, your training and your questions all live in one place. This short tour shows you what is here. |
| 2 | Sign-in with the security code | First, signing in. ELEVATE asks for a security code from your authenticator app each time. It takes a few seconds, and it keeps your information, and our clients' trust, safe. |
| 3 | The time clock | Next, the time clock. Clock in when you start, take your breaks, and clock out when you finish. ELEVATE keeps your hours, so there is one clear record. Forgot to clock out? Ask for a time correction, and your lead will review it. |
| 4 | Schedules, time off, extra hours | Schedules show your shifts in your client's time zone and in Manila time. You can ask for time off, and ask for extra hours when a client needs more of you, all with a clear answer from your lead. |
| 5 | Documents and signing | Documents keeps your files in one private place, in folders you choose. When something needs your signature, ELEVATE Sign walks you through it. |
| 6 | Announcements, a short video playing | Announcements bring company news, policies and now videos right to you. When something needs your acknowledgment, you will see a reminder until it is done. |
| 7 | Profile and My data | Your profile holds your details. My data shows exactly what we keep about you, and lets you ask for a correction. |
| 8 | ELEVATE Learn | Training happens in ELEVATE Learn: Heepa (HIPAA) and Cybersecurity basics, with short videos, an exam and a certificate you can download. |
| 9 | Safe Voice, privacy incident, Contact support | Need to speak up? Safe Voice lets you raise a concern completely anonymously. If you spot a privacy mistake, report it in privacy incidents. And if something in ELEVATE is not working, Contact support is one click away. |
| 10 | Help center, What's new | The Help center has step by step guides with pictures and videos for everything you just saw. And the What's new page tells you each time ELEVATE gets better. |
| 11 | Logo | That is ELEVATE. Built for you, by ELITE Resource Services. Welcome aboard. |

## Notes for the reviewer
- Pay, hours-to-pay and contractor wording are deliberately not mentioned.
- Safe Voice appears only as a menu item, never with numbers or screens of cases.
- Nothing shows real people, clients or patients: all screens come from the fake demo data.
- Section 6 shows a harmless demo clip, not a real announcement.
