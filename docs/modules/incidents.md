## Privacy incident reports (Release 2 of ELEVATE Learn)

- `src/modules/incidents/`, tables `ops.privacy_incidents` and `ops.privacy_incident_messages` (migration 0057, RLS on). Page `/incidents` ("Privacy incidents" in the Learning group of the menu, open to everyone) and `/incidents/[id]`. **Free text written by staff; the form forbids patient details and says so** (a warning above the form, general words only). Kinds: wrong recipient, lost device, suspicious link, exposed password, unauthorized access, left visible, other. Fields: kind, the day it happened, an "ongoing" flag, what happened, how it was found, optional client (from the client list), who else knows, what was already done.
- **Who reads:** the reporter (their own, with status and the thread), HR Admin, Super Admin and the **Privacy Officer**, a per-person flag (`core.users.is_privacy_officer`). **HR or Super Admin names the officer** (`incidents.designate`, decided with the owner; a card on the incidents page, by sign-in email, audited `user.privacy_officer.update`). The flag is an `alsoFlag` on a permission rule (`PermissionRule.alsoFlag`, `AuthzUser.isPrivacyOfficer`): it **adds** to roles and widens nothing else (matrix test "Privacy Officer flag"). A Team Lead or Executive does not see other people's reports. Anyone else who asks for a report id gets "not found", the same as a missing one.
- **Handling:** status new, in review, closed (closing needs a resolution note that only the privacy team reads). **Nobody handles their own report** (another person does), a closed report takes no messages until reopened, and the thread is append-only. A handler opening someone else's report is audited (`incident.view`).
- **Notices:** in-app to HR, Super Admin and officers (kind and "ongoing" only, never the text) plus **one email each straight away** (`emailHandlers`, no details, a link to the inbox; a missing email service never loses the report). The reporter is told in the app when the status changes or a handler replies. Audit entries hold kind, ongoing flag and ids, **never text** (a test searches for it).
- **Evidence:** a trigger refuses rewriting a report's text, kind, date, flag, client or reporter, and deleting it (only the handling fields move); messages cannot be changed or deleted. Rate limit `incident` (10 an hour per person).
- Not built: client breach-notice timelines (needs the client agreements), a dashboard count for the privacy team, an incident checklist or evidence upload, reporting from inside the Learn app (the HIPAA lessons say to tell the privacy team; a link can be added once the address is final). Tests: `src/modules/incidents/incidents.test.ts`, `tests/authz/incidents-actions.test.ts`, `tests/integration/incidents.test.ts`, matrix rows `incidents.report`, `incidents.handle`, `incidents.designate`.
