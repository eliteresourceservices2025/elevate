## The Jibble link (Phase 2.4)

- **Jibble is only for screenshots.** ELEVATE is the time clock and the only source of hours. Never store Jibble screenshots, GPS or activity; the only thing read back is tracked-minute totals for the nightly comparison (`time.jibble_daily`, kept 35 days, never used for hours or exports).
- **Credentials:** a personal access token (`JIBBLE_ACCESS_TOKEN`, works on Jibble's free plan and acts as the person who made it) or a Client ID + Secret. Local tests use the git-ignored `apps/elevate/.env.jibble.local`; production uses the host's environment. No token = nothing is sent and the clock works as usual. A rejected token (401/403) alerts HR once a day. Never log tokens; failures store only a short code and the HTTP status (`describeFailure`).
- **Code:** `src/modules/jibble/`. `http-client.ts` is plain fetch (no server-only, so `pnpm jibble:probe` can use it): Workspace `/v1/People`, TimeTracking `POST /v1/TimeEntries` (type In, Out or StartBreak) and `/v1/TimeEntries/EndBreak`, TimeAttendance `/v2/TimesheetsSummary`. `client.ts` holds the configured client and `setJibbleClient()` for tests (use a fake in every test; never call the real API from tests). `mirror-rules.ts` is the pure part.
- **Mirror (outbox):** `enqueueMirror()` writes `time.jibble_link_log` rows (one per clock event) in the clock transaction when ALL are true: a client is configured, `JIBBLE_MIRROR_ENABLED` is not `false`, the monitoring policy is published, and the person's team has `clock_rules.jibble_mirror` on (HR sets it in the Rules tab; turning it on needs the published policy). `processMirrorQueue()` sends them (Inngest event `jibble/mirror.requested` plus a 15-minute sweep), one person at a time in click order; network trouble, 429 and 5xx retry 6 times over about an hour, a refusal is final, 409 counts as sent, a retrying call holds later calls for that person. A person with no Jibble match gets a `skipped` row. Jibble being down never blocks or slows the ELEVATE clock.
- **Breaks:** `JIBBLE_BREAK_MODE` = `native` (the default: Jibble's own breaks), `clock` (break start sends Out, break end sends In) or `off`. Native mode needs **breaks set up in Jibble** (Settings > Breaks, available through each person's schedule; make them Unpaid, since ELEVATE's breaks are deducted). Jibble lists breaks **per person** (`GET /v1/GetBreaks(personId,time)`; the plain `/v1/Break` list is something else), so a break start reads the person's breaks (cached 10 minutes) and `pickBreak` chooses: unpaid first, exact length, else a flexible one (length 0 = "staggered", fits any length), else the shortest long enough, else the longest, and sends its `breakId`; a break end (`/v1/TimeEntries/EndBreak`) points at the entry that started it. With no break available to the person, StartBreak fails (not retried) and HR sees it in the Jibble page; "Test connection" warns. A clock-out on a break sends only Out in clock mode and EndBreak then Out in native mode. Corrections are never mirrored.
- **Send-now and late sending:** a click tells the job service at once (`kickMirror`, Inngest event `jibble/mirror.requested`, needs `INNGEST_EVENT_KEY` from the **Production** Inngest environment); without it every call waits for the 15-minute sweep and Jibble starts or stops screenshots up to 15 minutes late (Jibble stamps the time when it receives the call). `isSendingSlow` (calls that went through first time and waited over 3 minutes, at least two and half of them, over 6 hours) shows an amber notice on the Jibble page and tells HR through the health check. A missing key or a failed send is written to the log.
- **Self-test** (`JIBBLE_SELF_TEST_EMAILS`, a deployment setting, comma-separated sign-in emails): a listed person is mirrored to Jibble **without** the published policy and the team switch, so the owner can test the link on their own account. Off when empty (the normal state). It is an environment setting on purpose, never a screen, so HR cannot use it to monitor anyone; the Jibble page shows an amber "Self-test is on" notice while it is set. Remove it when testing is done. Still needs `JIBBLE_ACCESS_TOKEN`, `ELEVATE_ENV=production`, a matched Jibble person and a configured client. The repair job covers listed people too; the nightly comparison does not.
- **Fallback mode** (`JIBBLE_MIRROR_ENABLED=false`): ELEVATE sends nothing, people clock into both apps, and the nightly comparison finds gaps.
- **People:** `time.jibble_people` (own table, not a column on employees) matched by work email nightly (`syncJibblePeople`) or by hand in the Jibble page; a hand-made match is never overwritten and one Jibble account is never given to two people.
- **Nightly comparison** (`runJibbleComparison`, 3:10 AM Phoenix, after the 2:30 rebuild): for people on teams with the switch on, yesterday in the person's own zone; ELEVATE minutes = worked minutes (plus break minutes when breaks are not sent); more than `JIBBLE_MISMATCH_MINUTES` (default 15) apart sets the `jibble_mismatch` day flag (also on a day with no ELEVATE record). The nightly rebuild keeps the flag.
- **HR tab:** Attendance > Jibble (`jibble.manage`, HR and Super Admin only): connection test, match people, recent calls with Retry.
- `pnpm jibble:probe [email] [--clock] [--native-break]` checks the token and tries one TEST person; use it before turning a team on to see whether an API clock-in really starts the desktop app's screenshots.

## Hardening before the pilot (Phase 2.6, part 1)

- **Safety lock** (`sendsAllowed()` in `jibble/client.ts`): ELEVATE sends to Jibble only when `ELEVATE_ENV=production`. Anywhere else (laptops, staging, tests) it sends only for Jibble person ids in `JIBBLE_TEST_PERSON_IDS`; other calls are logged as skipped ("not sent: not the production environment"). The live Jibble organization is real people: **set `ELEVATE_ENV=production` only on the production deployment**, and tests that need sending set it themselves.
- **Repair job** (`runJibbleRepair`, every 30 minutes; the backstop also runs it every 15): for people on teams that use Jibble who are working or clocked out in the last 30 minutes, compare ELEVATE's state with Jibble's latest time entry (`latestState`) and send the call that fixes the difference (`repairAction`: ELEVATE is the source of truth). It skips people with a waiting call, a click in the last 3 minutes, a repair in the last 9 minutes, and does nothing while paused; at most 150 people a run. Repair calls are `jibble_link_log` rows with `source = 'repair'` and no event. A **retry first checks Jibble's state** so a call that went through is not sent twice.
- **Break fallback:** a native break start that Jibble refuses (no break for the person, already used; not auth, not a retryable error) sends a clock-out instead (`fallback = true` on the row) so screenshots still stop; its break end then sends a clock-in.
- **Pause switch** (`time.jibble_settings`, `setJibblePaused`, HR): while paused nothing is sent and calls wait. On resume, calls that waited more than 10 minutes are dropped (replaying a backlog would put Jibble in a wrong state) and the repair job sets Jibble right.
- **Alerts:** auth failures alert HR at most every 2 hours (`jibble.auth`); 3 or more failed calls in 2 hours alert at most every 6 hours (`jibble.failing`); the Jibble page shows a banner when the oldest waiting call is 15 minutes old. A 429 waits at least its `Retry-After`. One token request at a time per server. **Unmatched people** (`runJibbleUnmatchedReport`, daily): HR gets one list of who clocked in with no Jibble account yesterday; the day gets the `no_screenshots` flag (not for the safety-lock skip).
- **The clock never depends on the rate limiter:** `clock` and `presence` fail open when Upstash is missing or unreachable (`allowWhenLimiterUnavailable`); every other policy still fails closed in production. The header says "Cannot reach ELEVATE: refresh, or sign in again" when the browser is online but requests fail.
- **Payroll completeness:** every Monday 8 AM Manila each lead is told how many of their people have unapproved hours from last week (HR for people with no lead); Wednesdays HR gets who is holding it up. The Hours export tab shows approved versus not approved days by team for the latest periods, and an export tells HR how many finished days it left out. "Absent" is labelled "Absent (review)": never an automatic deduction.
