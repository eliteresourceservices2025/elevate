## The Jibble link (Phase 2.4)

**Implementation status (October 27, 2026):** The tabbed page, shift summary, retry restrictions, viewer-timezone display and server-side history filtering are implemented. Per-person coordination, stale-queue recovery and the planned mismatch notifications and corrections are not implemented.

- **Jibble is only for screenshots.** ELEVATE is the time clock and the only source of hours. Never store Jibble screenshots, GPS or activity. The integration reads Jibble's latest clock state and daily tracked-minute totals for the nightly comparison (`time.jibble_daily`, kept 35 days, never used for hours or exports).
- **Credentials:** a personal access token (`JIBBLE_ACCESS_TOKEN`, works on Jibble's free plan and acts as the person who made it) or a Client ID + Secret. Local tests use the git-ignored `apps/elevate/.env.jibble.local`; production uses the host's environment. No token = nothing is sent and the clock works as usual. A rejected token (401/403) alerts HR at most once in two hours. Never log tokens; failures store only a short code and the HTTP status (`describeFailure`).
- **Code:** `src/modules/jibble/`. `http-client.ts` is plain fetch (no server-only, so `pnpm jibble:probe` can use it): Workspace `/v1/People`, TimeTracking `POST /v1/TimeEntries` (type In, Out or StartBreak) and `/v1/TimeEntries/EndBreak`, TimeAttendance `/v2/TimesheetsSummary`. `client.ts` holds the configured client and `setJibbleClient()` for tests (use a fake in every test; never call the real API from tests). `mirror-rules.ts` is the pure part.
- **Mirror (outbox):** `enqueueMirror()` writes `time.jibble_link_log` rows (one per clock event) in the clock transaction when a client is configured, mirroring is enabled, the monitoring policy is published, and the person's team has `clock_rules.jibble_mirror` on (with the documented self-test exception). `processMirrorQueue()` orders each person's queued calls within one run; separate workers are not yet serialized and can overlap. Transient errors are retried with backoff, and a retry waits if Jibble's state cannot be confirmed. A 409 is marked sent only after a state check confirms the target. Unmatched people get a `skipped` row. Jibble being down never blocks the ELEVATE clock.
- **Breaks:** `JIBBLE_BREAK_MODE` = `native` (the default: Jibble's own breaks), `clock` (break start sends Out, break end sends In) or `off`. Native mode needs **breaks set up in Jibble** (Settings > Breaks, available through each person's schedule; make them Unpaid, since ELEVATE's breaks are deducted). Jibble lists breaks **per person** (`GET /v1/GetBreaks(personId,time)`); `pickBreak` prefers unpaid, exact length, then a flexible break, the shortest long enough, or the longest. If a native break start is refused for a non-retryable, non-auth reason, the integration attempts a clock-out instead; the call can still fail if that fallback is rejected. A clock-out on a break sends only Out in clock mode and EndBreak then Out in native mode. Corrections are never mirrored.
- **Send-now and late sending:** after the clock transaction commits, `kickMirror` schedules an immediate send through Next.js `after`; outside request scope it asks Inngest when configured, with scheduled sweeps as a backstop. Delays have multiple possible causes. `isSendingSlow` flags an aggregate of recent first-attempt sends over three minutes. A sent call or a Jibble clock-in does not verify that the desktop app captured screenshots.
- **Self-test** (`JIBBLE_SELF_TEST_EMAILS`, a deployment setting, comma-separated sign-in emails): a listed person is mirrored to Jibble **without** the published policy and the team switch, so the owner can test the link on their own account. Off when empty (the normal state). It is an environment setting on purpose, never a screen, so HR cannot use it to monitor anyone; the Jibble page shows an amber "Self-test is on" notice while it is set. Remove it when testing is done. Still needs `JIBBLE_ACCESS_TOKEN`, `ELEVATE_ENV=production`, a matched Jibble person and a configured client. The repair job covers listed people too; the nightly comparison does not.
- **Fallback mode** (`JIBBLE_MIRROR_ENABLED=false`): ELEVATE sends nothing, people clock into both apps, and the nightly comparison finds gaps.
- **People:** `time.jibble_people` (own table, not a column on employees) matched by work email nightly (`syncJibblePeople`) or by hand in the Jibble page; a hand-made match is never overwritten and one Jibble account is never given to two people. **Matching by hand** (Attendance > Jibble, `setJibblePerson`): HR types the email the person uses in Jibble, their Jibble member code (as in Jibble's exports), or Jibble's person id; the server looks it up in Jibble's people list (`match-rules.ts`, whole value, any case) and stores the id it found. No match, or more than one, is refused with a message; the browser never supplies an id the server did not look up (except a pasted id, as before). The member code is read through `$select=...,code`; if Jibble refuses that field (400) the client carries on without codes and email matching still works.
- **Nightly comparison** (`runJibbleComparison`, 3:10 AM Phoenix, after the 2:30 rebuild): for people on teams with the switch on, yesterday in the person's own zone; ELEVATE minutes = worked minutes (plus break minutes when breaks are not sent); more than `JIBBLE_MISMATCH_MINUTES` (default 15) apart sets the `jibble_mismatch` day flag (also on a day with no ELEVATE record). The nightly rebuild keeps the flag.
- **Jibble page:** `jibble.manage` is for HR and Super Admin. Connection controls sit above tabs for ELEVATE shift times, individual call history and people matching. The local page shows the viewer's attendance timezone (company default when absent) and offers server-side name/status filtering and 25-row pagination over retained calls. Nonqueued calls are purged after 90 days; queued calls are retained. Manual retry is limited to recent failed event calls with no other call at the same or a later creation time; it checks current Jibble state and reports the result. This restriction does not resolve the concurrent-worker or stale-backlog risks.
- `pnpm jibble:probe [email] [--clock] [--native-break]` checks the token and tries one TEST person; use it before turning a team on to see whether an API clock-in really starts the desktop app's screenshots.

## Hardening before the pilot (Phase 2.6, part 1)

- **Safety lock** (`sendsAllowed()` in `jibble/client.ts`): ELEVATE sends to Jibble only when `ELEVATE_ENV=production`. Anywhere else (laptops, staging, tests) it sends only for Jibble person ids in `JIBBLE_TEST_PERSON_IDS`; other calls are logged as skipped ("not sent: not the production environment"). The live Jibble organization is real people: **set `ELEVATE_ENV=production` only on the production deployment**, and tests that need sending set it themselves.
- **Repair job** (`runJibbleRepair`, every 30 minutes; the backstop also runs it every 15): for eligible people who are working or recently clocked out, compare ELEVATE's state with Jibble's latest time entry and send a repair if needed. It skips people with a waiting call, a click in the last 3 minutes, or a repair in the last 9 minutes, and does nothing while paused; at most 150 people a run. Repairs have `source = 'repair'` and no event. A retry checks Jibble's state, but the current implementation does not guarantee against concurrent or uncertain duplicate sends.
- **Break fallback:** a native break start that Jibble refuses (no break for the person, already used; not auth, not a retryable error) sends a clock-out instead (`fallback = true` on the row) so screenshots still stop; its break end then sends a clock-in.
- **Pause switch** (`time.jibble_settings`, `setJibblePaused`, HR): while paused nothing is sent and calls wait. On resume, calls that waited more than 10 minutes are dropped (replaying a backlog would put Jibble in a wrong state) and the repair job sets Jibble right.
- **Alerts:** auth failures alert HR at most every 2 hours (`jibble.auth`); 3 or more failed calls in 2 hours alert at most every 6 hours (`jibble.failing`); the Jibble page shows a banner when the oldest waiting call is 15 minutes old. A 429 waits at least its `Retry-After`. One token request at a time per server. **Unmatched people** (`runJibbleUnmatchedReport`, daily): HR gets one list of who clocked in with no Jibble account yesterday; the day gets the `no_screenshots` flag (not for the safety-lock skip).
- **The clock never depends on the rate limiter:** `clock` and `presence` fail open when Upstash is missing or unreachable (`allowWhenLimiterUnavailable`); every other policy still fails closed in production. The header says "Cannot reach ELEVATE: refresh, or sign in again" when the browser is online but requests fail.
- **Payroll completeness:** every Monday 8 AM Manila each lead is told how many of their people have unapproved hours from last week (HR for people with no lead); Wednesdays HR gets who is holding it up. The Hours export tab shows approved versus not approved days by team for the latest periods, and an export tells HR how many finished days it left out. "Absent" is labelled "Absent (review)": never an automatic deduction.
