# The time clock

> Moved out of CLAUDE.md to keep that file short. These are the full working notes for this area: read them before changing it. The hard rules are repeated in CLAUDE.md's "Module notes" index.

## The time clock (Phase 2.3)

- `time.clock_events` (`src/modules/attendance/`) is append-only (trigger). Web events get the **database clock** (`clock_timestamp()`) and the IP from `x-forwarded-for`; nothing the browser sends can change either. `performClock()` (service.ts) does everything inside `lockEmployeeClock()`: state machine from `clock.ts` (out -> working <-> break -> out; a clock-out on a break writes `break_end` first), no clock-in on approved full-day leave, IP range flag, location, selfie.
- **Breaks are unpaid** (deducted from worked time). The **calendar day** of a session is the day it STARTED in the person's own zone (`time.clock_prefs.time_zone`, default company zone), so a night shift is one day. `attendance_days` is rebuilt nightly from events (`rebuildAttendanceDays`), never hand-edited; the My time page computes live from events.
- **Allowed IP ranges are per team** (`time.clock_rules`): outside = flagged for the lead, never blocked; no row = no restriction. IPv4 and CIDR only.
- **Location and selfie are monitoring**: both work only while a policy with `kind = 'monitoring'` is published (`monitoringPolicyPublished()`), location also needs the person's opt-in (stored rounded to 2 decimals, about 1 km), selfie needs the team rule. Selfies live in `employee-docs/selfies/<employeeId>/`, are checked by their bytes (JPEG, 1.5 MB), and are deleted after 30 days (`purgeSelfies`); the row stays.
- **Idle prompt** only flags (`time.idle_prompts`), it never clocks anyone out. **Missed clock-out**: a session open over 12 hours notifies the person and their lead once (schedule-based grace comes with Phase 2.5).
- **Corrections** are requests (`time.clock_corrections`) approved by the lead (HR for anyone); approval writes NEW `admin_correction` rows and re-checks that the proposed events fit and break nothing that was valid. Nobody decides their own.
- **Timed breaks:** a break can be 15, 30 or 60 minutes or open-ended (`planned_break_minutes` on the `break_start` event; only those lengths are accepted). A timed break past its length plus a one-minute grace is an **overbreak**: the person sees a "Your break is up" pop-up in the header, the minutes show on the timesheet (`overbreak` flag and `attendance_days.overbreak_minutes`), and the lead (HR when nobody is above) is notified once per break (`time.overbreak_notices`), either when the break ends or by the 10-minute `runOverbreakAlerts` job while it is still running. An overbreak on a finished break counts even while the session is open.
- **Every clock-in is its own record**: `buildDays()` keeps `sessionList` for each day and the My time table shows each session under its day; days are only summed, never merged away.
- **Internal staff use the clock too**: it needs a people record, so HR and Super Admin accounts without one see "Set up your time clock" in the header and can create or link their own with `createMyProfile` (links an unlinked record with the same email first; audited). Anyone else asks HR to add them.
- `performClock()` hands each clock event to `enqueueMirror()` (Jibble link, Phase 2.4) inside the same transaction.
- Tests that need a published monitoring policy use `withMonitoring()` in `tests/integration/attendance.test.ts` (adds a published version, then archives the policy), so the seeded placeholder (version 1) stays intact for the announcements tests.

## Clock reliability, time claims and end-of-day notes (Phase 2.3.1)

- **Offline:** the clock is online-only on purpose (rule 9: the server owns the time). The header widget shows "Offline: clock paused" and disables the buttons; a failed request says nothing was recorded. There is no offline queue: a missed moment is fixed with a time claim.
- **Heartbeat:** a clocked-in page calls `pingPresence` every 10 minutes (and at once when the tab becomes visible, so a sleeping laptop reports its gap on return; people are working, not watching the clock, so the beat is deliberately rare). It stores only the latest time per person in `time.clock_presence` (no history), set at clock-in too. The reply carries the PREVIOUS time seen, so a gap over 30 minutes shows "Welcome back": "Yes" or "I stopped at ..." (a clock-out correction, kind connection_problem, device_problem or forgot). Never discard a ping reply: the ping itself counts as seen. Leads see "Last seen" and "Possibly offline" (25 minutes without a ping; display only, nobody is clocked out). `runQuietSessionAlerts` (every 30 minutes) tells the lead once when someone working has not been seen for 2 hours (`time.quiet_notices`). The 12-hour missed clock-out notice stays.
- **A waiting clock-out request** (`pendingClockOut`) blocks other clock actions for that person until it is decided or cancelled.
- **Time claims = corrections with proof** (`time.clock_corrections` has `kind` and `original_proposed`; screenshots in `time.correction_evidence`, files in `employee-docs/time-evidence/<employeeId>/`). A claim that adds a clock-in needs 1-3 JPG/PNG screenshots (5 MB, bytes checked, SHA-256 stored); upload goes through `requestEvidenceUpload` then the claim attaches them. Screenshots support the reviewer; they are never hours. Only the person, their chain of leads and HR open them (60-second signed link, audited as `clock.evidence_view`). Files are deleted 90 days after the decision, unattached ones after a day (`purgeEvidence`). Upload screens warn: never include client or patient information.
- **Who decides:** the lead (HR when nobody is above). HR only when it was filed for the person by someone else or reaches back more than 7 days from when it was filed (`needsHrDecision`). Nobody decides their own or one they filed. The reviewer may change the times when approving (kept in `original_proposed`, the person is told).
- **File for others** (`fileCorrectionForOthers`, `attendance.file_for_others`: HR anyone, lead their downline): notifies the person and HR; HR decides.
- **End-of-day notes** (`time.shift_notes`, one per session keyed by its clock-in event id, plain text up to 5,000 characters): optional box after clock-out (`ClockResult.sessionId`), editable by the author for 24 hours after clock-out (`edited` is set), visible to the author, the chain of leads and HR (`attendance.notes_view`). A team rule `eod_expected` only adds a `no_eod` flag to the nightly rebuild; it never blocks clock-out.
- E2E: after any clock-out the "Wrap up your day" box opens; click "Skip" before the next step.

## Technical issue reports

A contractor can report a problem with the clock, Jibble, their connection or a device from Attendance ("Report a technical issue" on the My time tab next to the time correction, and the Technical issues tab; any clock state, no clock events needed). **A screenshot is required** (1 to 3 JPG or PNG, up to 5 MB, stored like correction proof in `time.correction_evidence.issue_id`, migration 0086; deleted 90 days after the report is handled; never client or patient information). `time.tech_issue_reports` (migration 0085) holds the report: kind, when it started, free text (no client or patient information), open or handled. It is only a report: it never adds, changes or removes a clock event or hours (missing time is a correction request, and the person can send both). Permissions: `attendance.report_issue` (own, everyone) and `attendance.handle_issue` (HR all, a lead their people through `timeChainUserIds`; nobody handles their own). The lead and HR are notified (the notice names the kind, never the text); at most 5 open reports per person; reports must be within 31 days.

## Jibble send on clock-out

After a clock event is saved, `kickMirror(employeeId)` (`jibble/enqueue.ts`) sends that person's queued Jibble calls straight away using Next's `after()` (after the response, so Jibble being slow or down never slows the ELEVATE clock). A call that fails stays queued for the 15-minute sweep. Where there is no request (scripts, tests) it falls back to the Inngest event.

## Extra hours and the scheduled break

Extra hours are worked time beyond the scheduled hours. The scheduled hours have the scheduled unpaid break taken out, and worked time only has a break taken out when the person presses Break. So the part of the scheduled break that was NOT taken is time inside the shift window and is **not** extra: the allowance is scheduled minutes + (scheduled break - break actually taken). Example (reported 2026-10-16): a 4-hour shift with a 30-minute unpaid break (3h 30m scheduled), worked 4h 02m with no break pressed, showed "Extra +32m"; it is now no extra (allowance 4h, 2 minutes over, under the 15-minute threshold). `extraMinutes()` takes `breakMinutes` (taken); left out it assumes the scheduled break was taken. The hours review still shows "Worked X against Y scheduled; break taken Z" next to each extra.

## A time correction appeal always carries a screenshot

`requestCorrection` refuses an appeal with no screenshot, whatever it adds (before, only one that added a clock-in needed it). Corrections a lead or HR file for someone else and HR's bulk corrections are unchanged. The daily evidence purge now keeps screenshots attached to extra hours requests and issue reports (it used to treat anything not attached to a correction as an orphan after a day).

**"I forgot to clock out" after the person already clocked out late** (reported 2026-10-21): a clock-out proposed at a time that falls inside a session that already ends with a later clock-out used to be refused ("do not fit": in, out, out). `lateClockOutsReplacedBy` (service.ts) finds that later clock-out; `requestCorrection` and `fileCorrectionForOthers` store it in `void_event_ids`, so the reviewer sees "Remove clock out at ..." and approving replaces it (removed by a void row, never deleted). The lead or HR may approve it (decision 2026-10-21); one HR files for someone else or older than a week is HR only, as before. The bulk import does the same. The closing clock-out is found through `Session.endEventId` from `replayClock`, not by comparing times. Only the single-clock-out case does this; any other proposal that does not fit is still refused.

## Changing someone's recorded time (HR, Super Admin, Team Lead)

`attendance.edit_hours` (Super Admin and HR all, Team Lead team; migration 0088). On Team attendance, "Change someone's time" shows a person's events for a day: tick the ones recorded wrongly to **remove** them and add the right ones, with a reason (`edit-actions.ts`, `editAttendance`).

- **Append-only is kept.** A removed event is never edited or deleted: a row in `time.clock_event_voids` (insert-only, a trigger refuses update/delete/truncate) marks it, and **everything that builds hours or the clock's state reads the view `time.v_clock_events`** (the events without a void), never the raw table: `loadRecentEvents`/`loadEventsBetween`, `checkProposal`, the nightly rebuild and its checks, Team attendance, the separation check, the Jibble people picker. New reads must use the view. The audit entry holds before (removed) and after (added).
- **HR and Super Admin apply it at once**, for anyone except themselves, any day up to a year back (not the future): one transaction under the person's clock lock (a `clock_corrections` row marked approved, the voids, the new `admin_correction` events), then the person's days are rebuilt from the events that remain (`rebuildAfterChange`). **A Team Lead's change for their team is a proposal** in the ordinary correction queue (`void_event_ids` on the row) that HR or Super Admin approves; a lead can never decide it. Nobody changes their own time; HR's own needs another HR or Super Admin.
- Checks (`checkProposal` with `voidIds`): the events to remove must be on the person's record and not already removed; the result must replay (nothing that fitted may stop fitting, for example a clock-out left without its clock-in); no session over 16 hours; nothing in the future. `decideCorrection` now also applies voids and rebuilds the person's days at once (it used to leave that to the nightly run); HR and Super Admin may decide changes up to a year old, a lead up to 31 days.
- Jibble: edited or added events are not sent to Jibble; the nightly comparison sees the changed ELEVATE totals against Jibble's and may raise `jibble_mismatch`, which is the honest result. Hours still come only from ELEVATE.
