## Performance reviews and goals (Phase 4.1)

- `src/modules/reviews/`, tables in schema `talent`: `review_templates`, `review_cycles`, `reviews` (one per person per cycle), `review_responses` (self and lead), `review_calibrations`, `review_acknowledgments`, `goals`, `goal_notes`, `review_settings` (one row). Menu item **Reviews** (`/reviews`) with its own pages: `/reviews/[id]`, `/reviews/cycles/new`, `/reviews/cycles/[id]`, `/reviews/templates`, `/reviews/goals`.
- **Wording:** the plan's "probation (regularization)" review is an **early-engagement review** at month 3 and month 5 (1099 contractors, no regularization status). Counsel to confirm. Ratings are 1 to 5 with labels (needs improvement to outstanding). **No pay, raise or bonus field exists anywhere**; ratings are never converted into pay. No review content in emails or notifications (a link only).
- **Flow:** HR launches a cycle (quarterly, annual or early) from a template for everyone, some teams or chosen people (`launchReviewCycle`). The cycle **keeps its own copy of the questions**, so editing a template never changes a running cycle. Each review has the person's lead at launch (`lead_user_id`, HR can hand it to someone else with `reassignReviewer` before it is written). Steps: self review (optional, reminded) -> lead review (does not wait for the self review) -> HR calibration -> HR shares -> the person acknowledges ("I have read this", not "I agree", with an optional comment). `stageOf()` in `constants.ts` derives the stage from the timestamps.
- **Visibility** (`visibility()`, pure and tested): HR sees everything; the person sees their own self review, and the lead's feedback and final rating **only after HR shares**; the lead sees the self review **only after submitting their own** and the calibration only after sharing. If HR changed the lead's overall rating, the person is shown the final rating and the lead's written feedback but **not the lead's original rating** (`leadRatingHidden`); HR's reason for the change stays with HR. A changed rating needs a written reason. **Nobody writes the lead step for, calibrates or shares their own review** (another HR admin does). The Executive sees counts and averages only (`reviews.summary`), and an average is hidden below 5 reviews (`MIN_GROUP`).
- **Evidence:** `review_responses`, `review_calibrations` (newest row is current, only until shared) and `review_acknowledgments` are append-only (triggers reuse `talent.reject_change()`); a `reviews` row only moves forward (`reviews_guard`: a finished step cannot change, identity never changes, no delete). Audit rows never hold answers, notes or goal text.
- **Early-engagement reviews** (`runEarlyReviews`, daily 1:45 AM Phoenix, job `early-reviews`): a person with a start date gets a month 3 and a month 5 review on `startDate + 3 or 5 months` (month arithmetic clamps to the month end, `addMonths`). A milestone is opened for up to 31 days after its date (a job outage is made up) and **never back-filled** for people who started long ago; a unique index (`employee_id, milestone`) makes it idempotent. Each milestone and calendar month gets its own cycle ("Early-engagement review, month 3, 2026-10") using HR's chosen early template or the built-in short one; HR can switch it off (`setEarlyReviews`, Templates page). Due dates are +7, +14 and +21 days.
- **Reminders** (`runReviewReminders`, daily 8:45 Manila, job `review-reminders`): whoever has the next step of an open cycle's review gets one nudge a day once its due date is reached (`last_reminder_on`); HR gets one summary count. Both jobs are in the health registry.
- **Goals** (`goals`, `goal_notes`, `/reviews/goals`): simple goals (title, details, target date, status, progress notes), added and updated by the person, their chain of leads and HR (`goals.manage`); archived, never deleted. No weighting or scoring.
- Permissions: `reviews.view/summary/manage_templates/manage_cycles/write_self/write_lead/calibrate/share/acknowledge`, `goals.view/manage` (matrix rows in `tests/authz/matrix.test.ts`).
- Tests: `src/modules/reviews/reviews.test.ts` (pure), `tests/authz/reviews-actions.test.ts`, `tests/integration/reviews.test.ts`, `tests/e2e/reviews.spec.ts`.
- **Waiving a review** (migration 0081; `waiveReview`, `restoreReview`, permission `reviews.waive`: HR and Super Admin for anyone, a **team lead for their own team**): a review that is not needed this time (someone on leave, an early review already covered) is waived with a **written reason** (3 to 500 characters, required by a check constraint and the form). Stage `waived` comes first in `stageOf()`. A waived review drops out of reminders, the overdue flags and the Executive's totals (the summary shows a separate waived count), and nothing can be written, calibrated or shared on it. **Only before it is shared** with the person (also enforced in `reviews_guard`, which also stops a waived row moving forward), while the cycle is open; **nobody waives their own review** (another HR admin or the lead does). It can be brought back (`restoreReview`: HR and Super Admin any, a lead only one they waived themselves), which clears the waiver; a waived review cannot be handed to another lead; the audit log keeps the history. The reason lives on the review row for HR and the leads, is **never shown to the person** (they see only "waived, nothing to do") and **never written to the audit log** (only its length), and the other side is notified without the reason.
