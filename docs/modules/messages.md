# Messages and calls (chat first, calls second)

Working notes for `apps/elevate/src/modules/messages/`. Design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-17-messages-design.md`. Plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-17-messages-phase-1-chat-core.md`. Prototype: https://claude.ai/artifact/SaDLNSUTBLuwHPJBZUEEQn

## Purpose

One company-controlled place for internal chat (direct messages, private groups, later team and company channels, client groups) and calls, replacing scattered free tools. Chat is the main feature; calls are secondary.

## Decisions (owner, 2026-11-17)

- Free and open-source tools first; new monthly cost expected $0 (ceiling about $20).
- Calls: Cloudflare Realtime SFU and TURN (free up to 1,000 GB a month), no server to run; fallback self-hosted LiveKit behind `calls/transport.ts`.
- **Cloudflare usage on Settings > Calls:** besides our own estimate, the page shows Cloudflare's real month-to-date **TURN** traffic (`calls/cloudflare-usage.ts`, GraphQL node `callsTurnUsageAdaptiveGroups`). It needs `CLOUDFLARE_ACCOUNT_ID` and `CLOUDFLARE_ANALYTICS_TOKEN` (a token with only the Account Analytics read permission); without them the page says "Not connected". Cloudflare documents no SFU usage figure, so the SFU part stays an estimate until one is confirmed in the GraphQL schema explorer.
- Clients (outsiders) join as guests in client groups for attendance matters, under a written "never share patient details in ELEVATE" rule, with no vendor BAAs.
- HR reads a chat only through a logged request an HR Admin makes and a different Super Admin approves (read-only, requested dates only, 72 hours).
- Recording only by people a Super Admin picks, with consent from everyone in the call, never in calls that include a client.
- GIFs come from KLIPY through our server. No phone-number calling.

## Who manages groups (owner decision 2026-11-17)

Only **HR Admins and Super Admins** create, rename, change the people of, and delete group chats (`messages.create_group`, `messages.manage_group`, scope all). Everyone else chats in the groups they were added to and in direct messages, and cannot leave a group on their own (HR removes people). Deleting is the usual ELEVATE soft delete: the group is archived for everyone and its encrypted messages stay until the retention period ends.

- HR manages groups from the **Manage groups** page (`/messages/manage`: names, counts, people; never message text) and from the People panel of a group they are in. Everything is audited with ids and counts only (`chat.group.create|rename|delete`, `chat.members.add|remove`; an add lists the ids of who was added, a remove the id of who was removed).
- **Managing is not reading.** An admin who is not in a group cannot read it and cannot add themself to it (that would be a way around the two-person access request, Phase 6). A group they create includes them as a member until they remove themself.
- The server checks `messages.manage_group` on every action; the buttons are only hidden for convenience.
- **Owner accepted (2026-11-17):** one HR Admin or Super Admin can add any other person to a group without a second approval; that person then reads new messages from that moment. The add is audited (ids of who was added), and everyone in the group sees a system line that people were added.

## Hard rules

1. Message text is encrypted (`fieldCrypto`, context `chat_message:<conversationId>:<messageId>`). It never appears in Realtime payloads, notifications, email, audit rows, logs or Sentry. Audit rows carry ids and counts; individual sends are not audited.
2. Membership is checked on every read, write, channel join and call join. A permission never replaces membership. A person who was removed from a group loses all access at once (no list, no history, no sending, not even what came before), and a person added later (or added back) sees only what is sent from the moment they were added: thread, list preview and unread counts all use that window (tested). A deleted group (archived) cannot be read or written by anyone.
3. Realtime carries ids only (`ChatSignal`, no text field). The browser subscribes to its own private channel `user:<id>`, authorized by one policy on `realtime.messages` calling `chat.can_join(topic, uid)`; every read still goes through server actions.
4. HR access to a conversation is only through an approved two-person request (Phase 6). Nobody else can read a chat.
5. No patient details: warnings above the composer and on file attachments, a banner and guest notice in client groups. Free text cannot be technically blocked.
6. Messages are insert-only (database trigger): a delete sets `deleted_at`, an edit adds a `message_revisions` row. Deleted text is never returned; the encrypted row stays until the retention period (counsel) ends.

## Architecture (Phase 1)

```
Browser ── server actions ──► Postgres chat.* (encrypted text)
   ▲                              │ after commit
   └── Realtime private channel user:<id> ◄── server broadcast of {type, conversationId, messageId}
       (browser then calls loadChatMessages / loadConversationList)
Fallback: poll every 20 s while the socket is not subscribed.
```

## Tables (schema `chat`, RLS on, no policies except `realtime.messages`)

`conversations` (kind `dm`|`group`, `dm_key` unique), `conversation_members` (`left_at`, `last_read_seq`), `messages` (`seq` identity for ordering and read position, `body_enc`, `deleted_at`), `message_revisions`. Later phases add attachments, reactions, pins, polls, search tokens, guests, access requests, calls.

## Limits

Body 1 to 4,000 characters; groups 3 to 200 members (owner decision 2026-11-17; up to 50 people added per step; Realtime signals go out in batches of 100);  30 messages per sender per 10 seconds (counted in Postgres, not Upstash, to protect the free tier); page size 50 (max 100).

## What is built (state at 2026-11-17)

- **Phase 1 chat core, group management (200 cap, HR/Super Admin only).**
- **Phase 2 rich chat:** threads (one level), reactions (fixed emoji set), pins, @mentions (a notice saying where, never what), automatic team, department and company channels (daily sync; the company channel is HR-post-only), polls, pictures/files/voice notes (3-step upload, bytes-based type check, location data removed from pictures, served only through the authenticated route `/api/messages/attachments/[id]`), message search (a blind index of HMAC word tokens, never plain text), muting a chat, personal settings (appear offline, daily email), a daily unread email (`chat_unread`, lowest priority, count and link only; job `chat-digest`, 8:00 AM Phoenix).
- **Phase 3 client guests** (below).
- **@ suggestions:** typing `@` opens the list of people in the chat at once (names starting with what is typed first, then words, then contains), `@everyone` first in chats of three or more; arrows, Enter or Tab pick, Escape closes (`mention-suggest.ts`).
- **Emoji picker:** categories, a search box (English names plus Filipino ones such as kamote for the sweet potato), a Popular row until the person has used some emoji, then Recently used (kept in this browser only, `localStorage`, up to 16). Reactions accept any emoji in the catalog (`emoji.ts`, the server checks it).
- **Reactions:** hover (or focus) a reaction to see who reacted (first 12 names, you as "You").
- **Quote reply:** the Quote button on a message puts it above the box; the reply carries `quoted_message_id` (same chat only, never a system line, immutable by trigger). The quote shows the sender and a short snippet (latest edit, or Photo/Voice note/GIF/Poll); a removed original shows "The quoted message was removed", and a message from before the viewer joined shows "A message from before you joined" without any of its text.
- **Channels made by hand (kind `channel`):** HR Admin and Super Admin add them (Manage groups > New channel), rename them, choose who can post (everyone, or only HR; replies and reactions stay open), change the people and remove (archive) them. - **The automatic team, department and company channels** follow the organization, but HR Admin and Super Admin can also manage them from Manage groups (or the chat's People panel): rename, change the people, set who posts, add a picture, or remove. Renaming or changing the people sets `conversations.hr_managed`; from then on the daily sync, the "Update channels" button and the team-change sync leave that channel's name and people alone (new team members are no longer added automatically; HR adds them). A removed channel is archived and the sync never brings it back or makes a duplicate (a removed company channel is not recreated; HR can make a new channel). Changing who posts or the picture does not take a channel over.
- **Pictures for groups, channels and client groups:** HR Admin and Super Admin add, change or remove one (they need not be in the group): an uploaded chat image (checked by its bytes, location data removed), kept in `conversations.photo_attachment_id` and shown through `/api/messages/conversation-photo/<id>` to members and group managers only.
- **Photos in the chat:** every message shows the sender's profile photo (yours too) through the existing profile-photo route.
- **Unread:** reading a chat marks it read up to the newest message of any kind, including replies inside threads; the server never accepts a read position beyond the newest real message.
- **Notification sound:** a new message in a chat that is not muted plays the person's chosen sound (Chime, Pop, Bell, Ding or Off; saved in `chat.user_settings.sound`, picked in Messages > Settings with a Play button). The tones are synthesized in the browser (`components/play-sound.ts`, Web Audio), so there are no sound files. It plays when the unread count rises while the person is NOT looking at Messages (the count already leaves out their own messages and muted chats), at most once every 1.5 seconds, and only after the person has clicked or typed on the page (a browser rule). Calls can reuse the module for a ringtone later.
- **Presence (online dot):** Supabase Presence on the private `conv:<id>` channel (already authorized by `chat.can_join`): only the person's id is shared, nothing is stored, shown as "N online" in the chat header and a green dot in the People list of the open chat. "Appear offline" (chat settings) watches without announcing (takes effect on reload). It says nothing about activity or time and is never a monitoring tool.
- **GIFs (built; verified against KLIPY with a TEST key and against GIPHY):** two services, picked by `GIF_PROVIDER` (`klipy`, `giphy` or `off`; with it unset the first service that has a key is used). KLIPY's production key needs their approval (and a screen recording of the integration with their attribution); GIPHY's free beta key works now but allows only 100 calls an hour for the whole company, so searches are cached for 10 minutes, limited per person (30 per 10 minutes) and capped company-wide at 90 an hour (`gifSearch`, `gifSearchCompany` in `rate-limit.ts`). Each picker shows its service's credit ("Powered by GIPHY" or "Powered by KLIPY"); if the service supplies an official logo, add it next to that text. Only exact media hosts are ever fetched (`static.klipy.com`, `media.giphy.com`, `media0` to `media4.giphy.com`, `i.giphy.com`; never a whole domain). Every address our search returns is signed (`gif-sign.ts`, HMAC from the newest field-encryption key); sending a GIF and the picker's preview route (`/api/messages/gif-preview?u=...&s=...`, limited per person) accept only signed addresses, so nobody can post or fetch an arbitrary address on those hosts. Downloads are read as a stream and stopped at 5 MB. `KLIPY_API_KEY` (server env only) turns them on; without it the picker says GIFs aren't set up. Search and previews go through our server (`searchChatGifs`, `/api/messages/gif-preview`), a sent GIF stores only an https KLIPY address (encrypted like any message body, kind `gif`) and is shown through `/api/messages/gif/[messageId]` (current members only), so KLIPY never sees who is looking. Only https `klipy.com`/`klipy.co` hosts are ever fetched (`gif-rules.ts`: no redirects, 5 MB cap, gif/webp only). Real response shape confirmed: `data.data[]` items with `file.{hd,md,sm,xs}.{gif,webp,jpg,mp4,webm}.url`, hosted on `static.klipy.com`. `parseGifResults` stays tolerant and fails closed. The e2e test "a GIF" runs only when `KLIPY_API_KEY` is set and also checks the browser never contacts KLIPY directly. Check KLIPY's attribution rules before going live.
- **Built (5a): all-hands (migration none; `calls.mode = 'townhall'`).** HR, Executive and Super Admin (`calls.start_townhall`) start one from the chat bar ("Start an all-hands") in a company, department, team, channel or group chat, never in a direct message or a client group, and only while Settings > Calls allows it. Up to **150** people (`MAX_TOWNHALL_PARTICIPANTS`) and **8 speakers** including the host (`MAX_TOWNHALL_SPEAKERS`). The audience only watches: the server refuses their `publishTracks` and only pulls tracks of the host and speakers (`mayPublish`, `chooseSubscriptions`). The host makes someone a speaker or puts them back (`setSpeaker`, audited, never a guest; putting back closes what they were sending at Cloudflare). The browser shows no microphone, camera or screen buttons to the audience, only a raised hand and five fixed reactions (👍 👏 ❤️ 😂 🎉, sent as a tiny hint on the call channel's presence, one every 2 seconds; audience members stay silent on the channel until they raise a hand or react, so 125 people do not announce themselves). **Questions go in the chat:** the call page links to the group's chat in a new tab (the Q&A is ordinary chat messages; no separate Q&A tool was built). **Not rehearsed with real people yet:** presence traffic from ~125 browsers and the real Cloudflare egress of 125 viewers are unmeasured; rehearse before announcing.
- **Built (5b): recording (migration 0161).** Tables `chat.call_recordings` (one live per call, ends with a reason, `expires_at` 30 days after the end, `deleted_at` when the files are gone), `call_recording_parts` (about 8 MB WebM pieces, 24 MB at most, status pending/ready, SHA-256) and `recording_consents` (insert-only history by trigger: a changed mind is a new row, the latest answer counts); private bucket `call-recordings` (run `pnpm storage:setup`). **Who:** only people a Super Admin picks (`core.users.can_record_calls`, Settings > Calls > People who can record; `calls.record` is flag-only, `calls.manage_recorders` is Super Admin). **Refused when:** the company switch is off (default), the chat is a client group, a guest is in the call, or a recording is already live (`recording-rules.ts`, shared by server and browser). **Consent:** the recorder agrees by starting; everyone else sees a red notice with Yes/No; nothing of a person is recorded before a yes, and they can take it back at any time (the recorder's browser is only told the people who currently agreed: `allowedUserIds`, filled in for the recorder only). A system line "Recording started. Nobody is recorded until they say yes." goes into the chat. **How:** the recorder's own browser mixes the agreed people's audio and draws one picture (shared screen, else pinned or speaking person) onto a canvas (`browser/recorder.ts`, MediaRecorder, about 0.8 Mbps), and uploads pieces with the 3-step pattern (`requestRecordingUpload` gives a one-time token for a server-picked path, the browser uploads, `finishRecordingUpload` reads the file, checks size and, for the first piece, the WebM header, hashes it and keeps it). **Ends** when the recorder or the host stops it, the recorder leaves or is removed, the call ends, a guest joins, or 2 GB is reached; the last piece may arrive for 10 minutes after. **Watching:** `/messages/recordings` lists recordings of calls you were in; `/api/calls/recordings/[id]` streams the pieces in order to people who had a seat in that call (never a guest), before it expires; every play is audited (`call.recording.play`). **Deleting:** the daily job `calls-daily` (3:50 AM Phoenix; also ends calls nobody is in) deletes files after 30 days and never marks a recording deleted while its files may remain. Audit rows (`call.recording.start/stop/consent/play/expire`, `call.recorder.set`, `call.speaker`) carry ids, counts and reasons only. **Limits of this version:** the recorder must keep their tab open and visible (a background tab draws slowly, the sound is unaffected); a closed tab loses at most the last few minutes not yet uploaded; playback starts from the beginning (no seeking); the server cannot stop a modified recorder's browser from including someone who said no (the recorder is a trusted person chosen by a Super Admin; the policy says so); screen shares may show other people's windows. **Needs before real use:** the updated Monitoring policy and privacy notice published (`docs/policies/`), and counsel's answer on consent wording. HR access to a recording they were not in is Phase 6 (not built).

- **Microphone, camera and speaker picker (2026-11-19):** `browser/devices.ts` (pure helpers, choice kept in this browser's localStorage under `elevate.call.devices`, ids only, never sent to the server; an unplugged device falls back to the default), `call-devices.tsx` (lists in the lobby with a test that shows your picture and a microphone level meter, and a Devices dialog in the call from the More menu), transport `startSending(kind, {deviceId})` and `switchDevice` (`replaceTrack` on the same sender: no new offer, a muted microphone stays muted). Speakers use `setSinkId` where the browser has it (Chrome, Edge). **Checked against real Cloudflare:** after switching the microphone mid-call the other person still hears it (`calls-real.spec.ts`). The rehearsal with real people is in `docs/CALLS-REHEARSAL.md`.
- **Built (Phase 6, 2026-11-19): HR access requests (migration 0162, module `src/modules/chat-access`).** Owner decisions: an HR Admin (or Super Admin) asks, a DIFFERENT Super Admin approves; access is read-only, for the requested dates only (up to 90 days) or for one recording, and lasts 72 hours; **direct messages cannot be requested** (nor recordings of direct-message calls); the people in the chat are told **after it ends, and only if the access was actually opened** (one plain line from the daily job `chat-access-notices` at 4:00 AM Phoenix: the dates and that two administrators approved it; no name, no reason). Table `chat.access_requests`: the reason and the decision note are encrypted (`chat_access_reason:<id>`, `chat_access_note:<id>`); the two-person rule is a database check as well as a service check; a trigger makes a request unchangeable once made (only the decision, once, from pending, and the usage counters move) and forbids deletes. A pending request lapses after 7 days; at most 5 wait per person. **Pages:** `/messages/access` (HR: ask, cancel, open what was approved; Super Admin: decide, with a confirmation; the reason is shown only to the person who asked and to Super Admins) and `/messages/access/[requestId]` (read-only viewer: text, edited/removed marks, attachment NAMES only, no composer, 100 messages a page; or the recording player through `/api/chat-access/[requestId]/recording`). **Only the person who asked can read what was granted** (not the approver, not another HR Admin; everyone else gets a 404). Every opening re-checks approval, requester, the 72 hours and the dates, counts a read, and writes `chat.access.read` (ids and counts only); recording plays also write `call.recording.play` with the request id. Removed messages stay removed (their text is never returned). `messages/queries.ts` `loadWindowForAccess` and `calls/recording.ts` `openRecordingForAccess` skip the membership check and must only be called by `chat-access` after it has checked the grant.
- **Not built yet:** seeking in recordings, rollout (Phase 7). A real 25-person meeting, simulcast and audio-only pulls are still unconfirmed against real Cloudflare (see the spike notes above).

## Client guests (Phase 3, owner decision 2026-11-17)

- A guest is `core.users.is_guest = true` with **no roles at all** (not even Employee). A database trigger (`core.guest_roles_guard`) refuses to give a guest a role, and `is_guest` cannot change once set.
- **Default deny everywhere:** `requireUser()` sends a guest to `/messages` unless the caller passes `{ allowGuest: true }`. Only Messages (actions, queries, the attachment route, the `(app)` layout) opts in. The permission registry grants guests `messages.use` alone, through `alsoFlag.guest`; `tests/authz/guest-access.test.ts` checks every registered action.
- **Invite:** HR Admin or Super Admin (`messages.invite_guests`) on `/messages/guests`. The invitation carries `guest_client_id`; first sign-in set-up (`provisionCoreUser`) then makes the account a guest and a `chat.guests` row (no Employee role, no Super Admin bootstrap, no people record). An expired guest invitation still never produces staff. An email already invited as staff, or already holding an account, is refused, and staff invitations refuse an email that has a guest invitation.
- **Notice first:** nothing opens until the guest accepts the short guest notice (`GUEST_NOTICE_BODY` in `guests.ts`; counsel to finalize). Enforced on the server (`assertGuestReady` in `run()` and `actorWithAccess`), not only by the screen.
- **What a guest can do:** read and write in the client groups they were added to, vote, react, upload, search their own chats, mute. They cannot start chats, create groups, look people up, or be added to a plain group or a direct message (`activeUserIds` excludes guests). A client group (`kind = 'client'`, `client_id`) takes staff and only guests of **that** client (`membersFit`). HR/Super Admin create and manage them from Manage groups ("New client group").
- **Every client chat shows** "Attendance matters only. Never share patient details."
- **Ending access:** HR archives the guest (`archiveGuest`): the account is archived (requireUser signs them out), they leave every chat, nothing is deleted.
- **Known limit (key rotation):** the search index (`chat.search_tokens`) is keyed from the FIRST key in `FIELD_ENCRYPTION_KEYS`. Rotating keys makes older messages stop matching in search until the index is rebuilt; there is no rebuild job yet. Do not rotate until one exists (or accept search gaps).
- **Not yet:** adding a guest to an existing client group later from the Manage screen (create a new client group, or add through the action), Team Lead invites (HR and Super Admin only for now), guest sign-up wording in the invitation email (the generic ELEVATE invitation is reused).

## Calls (Phases 4 and 5, in progress; plan approved 2026-11-18)

- **Tools (decided 2026-11-18):** Cloudflare Realtime SFU + TURN (free up to 1,000 GB a month, shared by SFU and TURN), media straight from browser to Cloudflare; **signaling over our private Supabase Realtime channels** (no Socket.IO server). Not a peer-to-peer mesh: a mesh can't do 13 to 25 on camera or a 125-viewer all-hands. Only `modules/calls/sfu.ts` talks to Cloudflare (swappable; self-hosted LiveKit is the fallback). Calls are OFF until the four `CLOUDFLARE_*` variables are set (`.env.example`).
- **Built (4a foundation, migration 0159):** tables `chat.calls` (one live call per chat), `call_participants` (one open seat per person), `call_tracks` (directory of published tracks), `call_settings` (recording switch OFF by default), `call_usage`; `core.users.can_record_calls` (a Super Admin picks recorders; never a guest; no role grants it); `chat.can_join` now allows `call:<id>` to current members while the call is live; on a call channel a browser may only **track presence** (the `chat_send` policy), every real signal comes from the server; permissions `calls.start/join/start_townhall/record/manage_recorders/settings` (guests have none: a guest joins a client-group call through Messages access and can never start, host or record); `sfu.ts` (sessions, push/pull tracks, renegotiate, close, TURN credentials; ids checked before they reach a URL; errors carry only a status), `track-selection.ts` (everyone's audio, video for one page of about 9 tiles, screens always, all-hands audience sees only hosts/speakers), `usage.ts` (GB estimate).
- **Built (4b, migration 0160): calls on a fake media layer.** `calls/service.ts` (start, join, leave, end, heartbeat, publish, subscribe, renegotiate; one live call per chat; 25 in a meeting; a seat silent for 90 seconds is treated as left; a call nobody joins in 60 seconds is cleaned up; the server decides which tracks anyone may pull), `calls/actions.ts` (every action checks the permission, Zod, rate limit and chat membership; a guest can join but never start or end), ringing (a `call` signal on the shared `user:<id>` connection, pop-up with ringtone, muted chats don't ring, only chats of 25 or fewer ring), the call page `/messages/call/[callId]`, a lobby (choose microphone and camera), tiles, mute, camera, screen share, raise hand (Presence on the call channel), the mini window that keeps the call and its sound running on every page, a "Start a call / Join call" strip under each chat's header, and system lines in the chat ("Call started", "Call ended after N min", "Missed call" + a notice for a missed call). The guest frame has the same call support. `CALLS_FAKE=1` (server) and `NEXT_PUBLIC_CALLS_FAKE=1` (browser) switch to the fake media layer, never in production; the real browser transport is `browser/cloudflare-transport.ts` (written from Cloudflare's docs, to be confirmed in the spike). Deviation from the plan: call lines in the chat use the existing `system` message kind (no new kind).
- **Built (4c): meetings of up to 25.** Everyone's audio is pulled, but video only for one page of tiles (`track-selection.ts`); pinned and the active speaker (`active-speaker.ts`, from real audio levels with a pause before switching) come first; video that goes off screen is closed at Cloudflare (`unsubscribeCallTracks`), so we don't pay for pictures nobody sees. Beside your own tile there are up to 11 others (video first, then name circles) and a People list for everyone. **Host controls** (server-enforced: `muteParticipant` closes their audio at Cloudflare, `removeParticipant` ends their seat and tracks; both audited with ids only; a person muted can unmute, a removed person is sent back to the chat): the host is whoever started the call; if the host leaves, the person who has been there longest becomes host, and **never a client guest** (a call with only guests left has no host). Only the host can end it for everyone. **Settings > Calls** (Super Admin, `calls.settings`): this month's estimated media against the free 1,000 GB, a warning level, the all-hands switch (for 5a), and the last 10 calls (when, how long, how many; never who or what). The estimate is ours; Cloudflare's dashboard has the real figure.
- **Spike against real Cloudflare (2026-11-18, with the owner's SFU app and TURN key): it works.**
  - **Confirmed:** TURN credentials mint (several stun/turn/turns addresses, including port 443). The SFU creates sessions (201), 130 at once all succeed, and a wrong secret is refused (401). Real media flows: with Chrome's fake camera and microphone, two people see each other with frames advancing and hear each other, and a shared screen arrives; a group of 4 and a group of 6 (each person in their own Chrome process) all received every picture and voice.
  - **Found and fixed in the spike:** (1) Cloudflare's session ids are **64** hex characters, not 32 (our safety check refused them). (2) **A session is one connection:** we had used one session for both a sending and a receiving connection, and Cloudflare's answers then had m-lines in the wrong order. Now each person has **two sessions** (sending `cf_session_id`, receiving `cf_pull_session_id`, in migration 0160). (3) Pulling a video before the publisher's packets flow fails after about 8 seconds with `empty_track_error` ("No track data from remote peer"); the pull is now retried (and the 30-second heartbeat asks again for anything missing).
  - **Not confirmed:** a real 25-person call (this laptop with 8 cores handled 6 people but was slow to deliver every picture, about 2 minutes for all 30 pictures, mostly retries on an overloaded machine; with 8 people it did not finish in 2 minutes; separate real computers will do far better); simulcast / layer choice; audio-only pulls; Cloudflare's own usage numbers vs our estimate. **Plan:** a real rehearsal with the team (10 to 25 people on their own computers) before rolling out, and consider marking a track "ready" only after its first packets leave the publisher so nobody pulls a video that has not started (a follow-up).
  - **Test setup lessons:** Chrome's fake camera dies within seconds on this machine unless Chrome runs with `--disable-gpu`; and each person needs their own Chrome process for a fake camera to stay alive.

- **Independent security review of calls (2026-11-18) and what changed:** (1) the server now remembers every track a person pulled (`pulled_mids`) and, when they leave, are removed, are taken out of the chat, their chat is deleted or their guest access ends, closes BOTH what they sent and what they received at Cloudflare, so a modified browser can't keep listening (an ordinary browser also leaves when the server says "not found"); (2) a person a host removed cannot rejoin that call (`removed_by`), and host is one held role (a starter who returns after the role passed on is an attendee, never a second host; removal hands the role on); (3) only people who may manage channels can start a call in a channel only HR posts in; (4) rejoining closes the old seat's media; (5) the number of tracks per person is enforced under a lock and the kind a browser claims for a track (audio or video) must match its offer, so a microphone can't be labelled video to dodge a host mute; (6) other people's Cloudflare session ids are no longer sent to browsers; error logs carry Cloudflare error codes only. All call tables are in migration 0160. **Left as is, on purpose:** Presence on `call:<id>` is only a hint for the little mute and hand icons (a chat member could fake another person's icon; it carries no authority); sessions created for a join that then fails are not closed (they cost nothing and expire).
- **Not built yet:** a microphone/camera picker (the browser's default is used), all-hands (5a), recording (5b). The Cloudflare calls in `sfu.ts` are written from the documentation and must be confirmed in the Phase 4 spike with a real Cloudflare app (simulcast, audio-only pulls and 25 participants are unconfirmed).
- **Rules to keep:** the server is the authority (removing or muting someone closes their tracks at Cloudflare); nobody records in a call with a guest or in a client group; everyone consents to a recording; call audit rows carry ids and counts only; no call content or speaking-time statistics are stored.
