# Invitations, certificates, deactivation, support, anniversaries

> Moved out of CLAUDE.md to keep that file short. These are the full working notes for this area: read them before changing it. The hard rules are repeated in CLAUDE.md's "Module notes" index.

## Invitations with roles (Settings > Invitations)

- A **Super Admin** can choose roles (all but Employee) and the **Safe Voice handler** flag when inviting (`core.invitations.roles` and `is_safevoice_handler`, migration 0044; a check constraint allows only the five grantable roles). HR can still invite but only as Employee: asking for roles or the flag needs `settings.manage_roles` / `settings.set_safevoice_handler` (the same Super-Admin-only rights as the Roles page), checked in `createInvitation`. A re-invite by HR keeps what a Super Admin chose; a Super Admin's new choice (even an empty one) replaces it.
- **Nothing is granted when inviting.** `provisionCoreUser` (first sign-in) reads the invitation in the same statement that marks it accepted, so the choices are applied **exactly once**, and **only if the invitation had not expired**. It writes the roles (`granted_by` = the inviter), sets the handler flag and audits `invitation.grants_applied` (roles and flag, `invitedBy` in the metadata).
- Only someone who may give roles sees what was chosen (`listInvitations` blanks roles and the flag for HR), so who is a Safe Voice handler is not read off the invitation list. The invitation email says nothing about roles.
- Tests: `src/modules/settings/invitation-grants.test.ts`, `tests/authz/settings-actions.test.ts`, `tests/integration/invitations.test.ts` (apply once, expired grants nothing, HR refused, re-invite rules, database constraint), `tests/e2e/invitations.spec.ts`.

## Certificates (training that expires, e.g. HIPAA)

- `src/modules/credentials/`, tables `talent.credentials` and `credential_reminders` (migration 0045, RLS on). Page `/credentials` ("Certificates" in the menu). A certificate is only a **name and dates** (issued optional, ends required) for a person: no file, number, score or price. Removing archives (`archivedAt`), never erases. The same person, name and end date is recorded once (partial unique index), so a repeat import adds nothing. A renewal is a NEW row with a later end date; the older one is shown as "Renewed" and is left out of the expired and expiring lists and the reminders (`isRenewed()` in `service.ts`).
- **Permissions:** `credentials.view` (HR and Super Admin all, Team Lead their downline, Employee their own; recruiters and executives nothing) and `credentials.manage` (HR, Super Admin: add, remove, import).
- **Reminders** (`runCredentialReminders`, daily 1:05 AM Phoenix, job `credential-reminders`, in the health registry): 30 days, 7 days and the end date, once each (reuses `documents/expiry.ts`: `dueReminder`, `describeExpiry`), to the person (in-app) and one summary to HR. A certificate **added or imported inside a window starts silent** (`silenceDueReminders` marks the passed thresholds as handled), so loading a file never causes a burst of notices. Nothing is sent for someone who has left. The dashboard's needs-attention list shows HR counts of expired and expiring certificates.
- **Load from TalentHR's assets file** (the card on `/credentials`, action `importCredentials`, pure part `planCredentialImport` in `rules.ts`): preview first, then confirm. Only rows whose name contains the text HR types (e.g. HIPAA) are looked at; a row becomes a certificate when it has an **Assignment Email** that matches a current person (by work email) and a valid **Warranty end date** (month/day/year); Purchase date becomes the issue date; prices and everything else are ignored. Skips are counted by reason (never listing values). The audit entry holds counts only. The instruction row (`GUIDE ROW = yes`) is dropped by the CSV reader.
- Tests: `src/modules/credentials/credentials.test.ts` (pure), `tests/authz/credentials-actions.test.ts`, `tests/integration/credentials.test.ts`, `tests/e2e/credentials.spec.ts`.

## Deactivating an account (Settings > Roles and access)

- `deactivateAccount` / `reactivateAccount` (`settings.deactivate_account`, Super Admin only; rules in `settings/deactivate.ts`): bans the sign-in at the auth service (`onboarding/accounts.ts` `disableLogin` / `enableLogin`), archives the `core.users` row, ends open sessions, audits `user.deactivate` / `user.reactivate`. Nothing is deleted. For test accounts and mistakes: **a person with an active people record is refused** (use Offboarding, which also ends schedule, leave and equipment), you cannot deactivate yourself, and the last active Super Admin stays. An accepted invitation stays as history.
- Tests: `src/modules/settings/deactivate.test.ts`, `tests/authz/settings-actions.test.ts`, `tests/integration/account-deactivation.test.ts`.

## Contact support and work anniversary reminders

- **Contact support** (`src/modules/support/`, table `ops.support_requests`, migration 0051, page `/support`, permissions `support.create` for everyone and `support.manage` for HR and Super Admin): a short form (topic, title, what happened, optionally the page the person was on, which only a path inside the app is accepted for). It is **saved first**, HR and Super Admin are notified in the app (`support.new`), and it is **emailed straight away** to the support address (`DEFAULT_SUPPORT_EMAIL`, overridable with `SUPPORT_EMAIL`) with the person as reply-to (`OutgoingEmail.replyTo`). A missing or failing email service never loses the request (`emailed` records it). Five requests an hour per person (`support` policy). HR works an inbox on the same page and marks requests handled or reopens them (audited). The form warns against passwords, client names and patient information. The login screen and the public careers page show the address as a plain mailto link. Reached from the account menu ("Contact support") and Settings.
- **Work anniversary reminders** (`runAnniversaryReminders`, `people/anniversary.ts` pure, daily 1:25 AM Phoenix, job `anniversary-reminders`, in the health registry; table `core.anniversary_notices`, one row per person and year): seven days before a start-date anniversary of **one year or more** (a missed day is made up until the day before, never on or after the day), **Super Admin, HR, every recruiter and the person's leads who hold the Team Lead role** get one in-app notice listing who and when. Executives in the chain are not notified. People who have left, people with no start date and first-year starters are skipped. A 29 February start falls on 28 February in a non-leap year.

## People records retention (Settings > People records retention)

Design and open decisions: `docs/modules/people-retention-design.md`. **Built, off by default.**

- `runPeopleRetention` (`src/modules/people/retention.ts`, pure stages in `retention-rules.ts`, daily 3:35 AM Phoenix, job `people-retention`, in the health registry). Settings row `core.people_retention_settings` (migration 0070; `enabled` false, 60 and 72 months from `employees.end_date`). HR and Super Admin only (`people.manage_retention`).
- Only people with status `separated` and an end date are touched; a person with a legal hold (`employees.legal_hold_at`, `setLegalHold`, reason required, audited) is skipped.
- **Archive stage:** `employees.retention_archived_at`; the directory and `getProfile` then open the record for HR only. Nothing erased.
- **Anonymize stage:** names, birth date, contact and address replaced, `work_email` set to `removed-<id>@removed.invalid`, `employee_sensitive`, emergency contacts and custom values deleted, document rows blanked (`documents/retention.ts`) and their files removed through `DocumentStorage`. A trigger refuses later updates to an anonymized record unless the job's `core.retention` flag is set. Audit entries (`retention.archive`, `retention.anonymize`, `retention.hold_set`, `retention.hold_lifted`, `retention.settings_change`) hold ids and counts only.
- **Not covered yet:** `core.change_requests` payloads and the before/after JSON in `core.employment_history` (append-only); counsel decides whether those need scrubbing. Time records follow their own 3-year rule.
- The settings page shows counts only (to archive, to anonymize, on hold).
- Tests: `src/modules/people/retention-rules.test.ts`, `tests/authz/people-retention-actions.test.ts`, `tests/integration/people-retention.test.ts`.
