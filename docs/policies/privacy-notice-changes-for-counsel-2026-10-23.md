# Privacy notice: what changed (for counsel), 23 October 2026

The notice is `privacy-notice.md` in this folder; the short public version is the Privacy policy page of ELEVATE. Both were updated. Neither is published in
the app yet: publishing is permanent and makes everyone accept the notice again, so it waits for your review. Everything below is written for the people who
work with ERS (contractors, 1099) and for people who apply to work with ERS.

## Changes, with the old and new wording

### 1. Training records (ELEVATE Learn)
- **Before:** the courses assigned, exam answers, results and certificates.
- **Now adds:** the customer service course; when a course was assigned; which modules were finished and about how long was spent reading; **the replies chosen in
  a simulated practice call and the result**; the statement accepted at the end of a course; the certificate, with **the network address recorded when the person
  accepts**. States that **no voice or screen is recorded**, that the voices in lessons are pre-made audio, and that ELEVATE does **not track which videos or audio
  clips a person plays**.
- **Facts behind it:** the practice call is text only: the person picks a reply from choices; nothing they say is captured. The network address at acceptance
  already appeared on certificate copies. Video and audio plays are not logged by the app (the hosting provider's ordinary request logs are outside our control).

### 2. Why we use it (training)
- **Now adds:** grading of practice calls, and the sentence "Scores are calculated by the system, and people review any result that affects you."
- **Please check:** the notice also says "we do not make decisions about you by computer alone". Pass or fail on the exam and the practice call is computed by the
  system. Is the added sentence enough, or should the notice say plainly that pass or fail is automatic and that HR can review it on request?

### 3. Who can see it (new line)
- "Training results: you, HR and the Privacy Officer see your scores and answers. Your lead and clients see only whether you passed."
- This is how the system works today (leads and clients see completion only, never scores).

### 4. How long we keep it (training)
- **Before:** results and certificates five years after completion, then one year in an HR-only archive; answers to individual exam questions removed after 12 months.
- **Now adds:** the replies chosen in a practice call are removed after 12 months too; scores and pass or fail results stay for the period above.
- **Known gap (unchanged from the earlier notes):** Learn keeps results and certificates for the full six years in one place; there is no separate restricted archive
  stage yet. Per-answer data is purged on schedule. Counsel may prefer that the notice not promise a separate archive until it exists.

### 5. Job applications
- **Now adds:** the hiring team may add labels (tags) to an application and may combine two applications into one record when they are clearly from the same person.
  Reports about applications use counts only, with no names, so they are not mentioned.

### 6. Typo
- "Brevo and Brevo and Resend (email)" is now "Brevo and Resend (email)".

### The public Privacy policy page
- Gains a short Training records point, a longer Job applications point (source, tags, keyword counting as a reading aid with no stored resume text, the 12-month
  "keep my details" choice) and the training retention periods.

## Questions for counsel

2. Is it acceptable to record the network address at acceptance of a training statement, on the basis already stated for training records?
3. **Voices:** the lesson videos use a voice cloned from an ERS staff member's own recording (with their agreement), and the practice-call callers and exam reader are
   synthetic voices. Should learners be told that the voices are generated?
4. Retention: should the notice avoid promising the restricted archive until it is built (item 4)?
