## Why this policy exists

This policy tells you exactly what ERS watches while you work, why, who can see it and how long it is kept. It applies to people who clock in and out with ELEVATE. It is part of how we show clients that the time we report is time actually worked. We keep it to the minimum we need. Please read it. You will be asked to confirm that you have.

## What is recorded

**1. Your time clock (ELEVATE).** When you clock in, take a break or clock out, ELEVATE records the time (from our server, not your device), the type of event and the network (IP) address of the request. It also notes if you were offline for a long time while clocked in, and may ask "Are you still working?" if there has been no activity. The question only records your answer. It never clocks you out.

**2. Work screenshots (Jibble).** While you are clocked in and not on a break, the Jibble desktop app takes screenshots of your screen at intervals set by ERS. When you clock in or out, ELEVATE passes that clock event to Jibble so that screenshots start and stop with your working time. Screenshots show whatever is on your screen at that moment.

**3. Approximate location (optional).** You may choose, in your clock settings, to let ELEVATE record your approximate location (rounded to about 1 km) when you clock in. This is your choice. It is off unless you switch it on, and you can switch it off at any time.

## What is not recorded

- No photos or selfies, no webcam or microphone.
- No keystroke logging and no reading of your messages, email or files.
- No tracking of which apps or websites you use, and no activity scores. ERS uses Jibble for screenshots only.
- Nothing while you are clocked out or on a break.
- Screenshots are not copied into ELEVATE.

## Why

- To confirm that reported hours match work done, for accurate billing of our clients and for your pay.
- To protect clients' data and our systems.
- To resolve questions about a time record quickly and fairly.

It is not used to score you automatically. People review any screenshot or record before anything is done about it.

## Who can see it

- **Time records:** you, your team leads and HR.
- **Screenshots:** only the few people ERS authorizes in Jibble (HR and your team lead). They are not shared with clients unless a client has a genuine concern about work done and ERS decides to share the relevant part.
- Every look at a monitoring record in ELEVATE is logged.

## Client and patient information

Some of our clients are healthcare organizations. A screenshot may show their information. ERS treats any such screenshot as confidential, does not share it outside the people named above, and deletes it as soon as it is no longer needed. If you work with sensitive client information, close or minimize windows you do not need on screen while you work.

## How long it is kept

- **Screenshots:** kept in Jibble for **90 days**, then deleted.
- **Time clock records:** the engagement and three years after, as in the Privacy notice.
- **Location (if you choose it):** kept with the clock event for the same period as the time record.

## Your rights and choices

- You may ask HR to show you the monitoring records held about you, and to correct a wrong time record using a correction request in ELEVATE.
- If a screenshot captured something private by mistake, tell HR and we will review it and delete it if it is not needed.
- You may switch your location setting on or off.
- Your other rights are in the Privacy notice, including your right to complain to the National Privacy Commission.

## Changes

If this policy changes, you will be asked to read the new version the next time you sign in, and ERS will not start collecting anything new before that. Questions: the ERS HR team.
