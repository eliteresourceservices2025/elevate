## Why this policy exists

This policy tells you exactly what ERS watches while you work, why, who can see it and how long it is kept. It applies to people who clock in and out with ELEVATE or the ELEVATE Tracker App. It is part of how we show clients that the time we report is time actually worked. We keep it to the minimum we need. Please read it. You will be asked to confirm that you have.

## What is recorded

**1. Your time clock (ELEVATE).** When you clock in, take a break or clock out, in ELEVATE or in the ELEVATE Tracker App, ELEVATE records the time (from our server, not your device), the type of event and the network (IP) address of the request. If you work for more than one client, it records which client and project you chose. It also notes if you were offline for a long time while clocked in, and may ask "Are you still working?" if there has been no activity. The question only records your answer. It never clocks you out.

**2. The ELEVATE Tracker App.** This small app runs on your work computer only while you are clocked in and not on a break. While it runs it records:

- the **name of the program** in front (for example "Excel"), and whether you were **active or idle**. A locked screen counts as idle. Idle means no keyboard or mouse use for a few minutes.
- about **one screenshot every 10 minutes**, of all your screens. The picture is **blurred on your computer before it is sent**, so text is not readable. It shows layout and colors only.

You can always see what the app recorded about you: in the app under "What was captured", and in ELEVATE on your Attendance page, where you can also see who looked at your screenshots. The app never clocks you in or out by itself. Only your click does.

**3. Work screenshots in Jibble.** If HR has set you to clock in with Jibble, the Jibble app takes screenshots at intervals set by ERS while you are clocked in and not on a break. Jibble is being replaced by the ELEVATE Tracker App team by team; you will be told when yours moves.

**4. Approximate location (optional).** You may choose, in your clock settings, to let ELEVATE record your approximate location (rounded to about 1 km) when you clock in. This is your choice. It is off unless you switch it on, and you can switch it off at any time.

## What is not recorded

- No photos or selfies, no webcam or microphone.
- No keystroke logging, no clipboard, no reading of your email or files. ERS does not read your ELEVATE chats either, except as described under "Messages" below.
- The Tracker App never records window titles, web addresses, page names or document names. Only the program name.
- No activity scores. The Tracker App is evidence for checking a time record. It does not change your hours, and it does not pay or rank you.
- Nothing while you are clocked out or on a break.
- Unblurred screenshots are never stored by ERS. The blur happens on your computer.

## Messages (ELEVATE chat)

Chats are private to the people in them. Message text is stored encrypted and is never shown to anyone outside the chat. HR can create, rename and delete group chats and choose who is in them, but that does not let HR read them. ERS may open a chat only through a request made by HR and approved by a different Super Admin, for a specific reason such as an investigation. It is read-only, limited to the dates requested and to 72 hours, and every look is logged. Chats are not used to score or rate you. ELEVATE shows an "online" dot to the people in a chat you have open (you can appear offline); it records nothing about when you were active. Clients invited as guests may only use the chats they were added to, for attendance matters, and never to share patient or client details. Please do not write patient or client details in any chat.

## Why

- To confirm that reported hours match work done, for accurate billing of our clients and for your pay.
- To protect clients' data and our systems.
- To resolve questions about a time record quickly and fairly.

It is not used to score you automatically. People review any screenshot or record before anything is done about it.

## Who can see it

- **Time records:** you, your team leads and HR.
- **Tracker records (programs, active and idle time, blurred screenshots):** you, your team lead (including a lead you report your time to), HR and Super Admins. Executives and recruiters cannot open another person's screenshots.
- Anyone other than you must pick a reason before opening a screenshot. **Every look is logged**, and you can see who looked and how many times on your Attendance page.
- Screenshots are not shared with clients unless a client has a genuine concern about work done and ERS decides to share the relevant part. Clients are never given pictures.

## Client and patient information

Some of our clients are healthcare organizations. Even blurred, a screenshot may hint at their information. ERS treats every screenshot as confidential, does not share it outside the people named above, and deletes it when the time below is up. If you work with sensitive client information, close or minimize windows you do not need on screen while you work.

## How long it is kept

- **Tracker screenshots:** deleted automatically after **30 days**.
- **Tracker program and active/idle records:** with the time record (the engagement and three years after, as in the Privacy notice).
- **Jibble screenshots (if you clock in Jibble):** kept in Jibble for **2 months**, then deleted.
- **Time clock records:** the engagement and three years after, as in the Privacy notice.
- **Messages and files:** one year after they are sent, as in the Privacy notice.
- **Location (if you choose it):** kept with the clock event for the same period as the time record.
- **On your computer:** the app keeps what it has not yet sent, and a list of what it recorded in the last day. Uninstalling the app removes your sign-in, and you can choose to delete its data.

## Your rights and choices

- You may ask HR to show you the monitoring records held about you, and to correct a wrong time record using a correction request in ELEVATE.
- If a screenshot captured something private by mistake, tell HR and we will review it and delete it if it is not needed.
- You may switch your location setting on or off.
- You may ask HR to sign out the computer the app is on, or stop using it when you are not working.
- Your other rights are in the Privacy notice, including your right to complain to the National Privacy Commission.

## Changes

If this policy changes, you will be asked to read the new version the next time you sign in, and ERS will not start collecting anything new before that. The Tracker App collects nothing on a computer until this policy is published. Questions: the ERS HR team.
