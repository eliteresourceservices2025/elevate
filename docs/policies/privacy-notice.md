## About this notice

Elite Resource Services ("ERS", "we") runs ELEVATE, the internal system we use to manage the people who work with us and to receive job applications. This notice explains what personal data ELEVATE holds about you, why, who can see it, how long we keep it and what rights you have under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173). You will be asked to confirm you have read it.

**Who is responsible:** ERS is the personal information controller. Our Data Protection Officer role is held by the **ERS HR team**. You can reach them through the contact details on the Privacy policy page at https://elevate.eliteresourceservices.com/privacy, or in ELEVATE under "My data".

## What we collect

- **Account details:** your name, work email, the sign-in method you use (password or Google) and your two-step sign-in. With Google sign-in we receive only your name, email address and profile picture.
- **Your profile and work records:** your position, team, who you report to, the clients you are assigned to, your start date and status, schedule, time off and prize days, equipment assigned to you, and the documents you or HR upload (for example your contract, ID copies and certificates).
- **Personal details:** date of birth, civil status, phone, home address and emergency contact, where you or HR provide them.
- **Sensitive details:** government ID numbers (TIN, SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG), bank or payout details and your pay rate. These are encrypted and visible only to you and the HR and administrator roles that need them for payroll and compliance.
- **Optional self-identification:** your gender, pronouns, sexual orientation and religion, only if you choose to share them in your profile, and, if you apply for a job, your gender, sexual orientation and religion in the optional part of the application form. Every question can be skipped (there is always a "prefer not to say"), and you can change or remove your answers at any time. Gender, sexual orientation and religion are stored encrypted. Your pronouns are shown next to your name for colleagues if you choose one. Your gender, sexual orientation and religion are visible to colleagues only if you switch on "Show on my profile to colleagues" for that answer (each is off unless you turn it on, so colleagues can address you properly and plan company events with everyone in mind); otherwise nobody can see them. Answers given on a job application are never shown to anyone by name. HR sees only combined counts of 5 or more people, never a name, to check that our workplace and hiring are fair. We do not use these answers to decide whether to hire, promote, pay or assess anyone. They are deleted when your record is removed.
- **Working time:** clock-in, break and clock-out records, the network address you clocked in from, requests to correct your time, and notes you add at the end of a shift. Our Monitoring policy explains the Jibble work screenshots and the optional location setting.
- **Performance and development:** goals, early-engagement and periodic reviews, ratings and feedback.
- **Training records:** the courses assigned to you in ELEVATE Learn (for example HIPAA, cybersecurity and customer service), the modules you finished and about how long you spent, your exam answers and scores, your replies and result in a practice call, the statement you accept at the end of a course, and your certificates (with the network address recorded when you accept). We do not record your voice or screen, and ELEVATE does not track which videos or audio clips you play.
- **Engagement and exit records:** onboarding and offboarding checklists, agreements you sign in ELEVATE, exit interview answers and certificates of engagement.
- **Job applications:** if you apply through our careers page, the details and resume you send, where you heard about the job (if you say), and our hiring team's notes. Our hiring team may tag your application and combine duplicate applications from the same person. We count how many of the job's keywords your resume contains, as a reading aid for the hiring team only: the resume text is not kept, nothing about you personally is used, and no decision is made by a computer. If you tick "keep my details for 12 months", we keep your application so the team can contact you about other roles; you can ask us to remove it at any time.
- **Security records:** a log of important actions (for example sign-ins and who opened a sensitive record), used to protect everyone's data.

We do not store client or patient information in ELEVATE. Please never upload it.

## Why we use it, and our basis

- **To manage your engagement with ERS:** onboarding, scheduling, time records, time off, equipment, reviews, agreements and ending the engagement. Basis: our contract with you and our legitimate interest in running the business.
- **To pay and report correctly:** hours records used for payment, and records we are required to keep for tax, social contributions and other laws. Basis: contract and legal obligation.
- **To train and show training was done:** assigning courses, grading exams and practice calls and issuing certificates, so ERS and its clients can show required training (for example HIPAA) was completed. Scores are calculated by the system, and people review any result that affects you. Basis: our contract with you, legal obligation and legitimate interest. Your leads and clients see whether you completed a course, never your scores.
- **To protect people, clients and systems:** security logs, sign-in checks and the monitoring described in our Monitoring policy. Basis: legitimate interest and, where the law requires it, your consent.
- **To hire:** to assess applications and make offers. Basis: your request to be considered and our legitimate interest.

We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising or make decisions about you by computer alone. People review everything that affects you.

## Who can see it

- **You** can see your own records under "My data".
- **Your team leads** see what they need to manage their team: schedule, time records, leave requests and reviews they take part in. They do not see your birth date, civil status, personal email, home address or sensitive details, and they see your optional gender, sexual orientation or religion answers only if you chose to show them on your profile.
- **HR** sees employee records and, with administrators, the sensitive details. Every time a sensitive detail is opened, the action is logged.
- **Training results:** you, HR and the Privacy Officer see your scores and answers. Your lead and clients see only whether you passed.
- **Clients** are told your name, role and working hours where needed to serve them. We do not share your sensitive details or personal records with clients.
- **Authorities and other parties** only where the law requires it, or with your consent.

## Companies that help us run ELEVATE

These providers process data for us under their own security and data-protection terms: Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage, hosted in Singapore), Vercel (hosting), Google (sign-in if you choose it), Brevo and Resend (email), Upstash (abuse protection), Inngest (scheduled tasks) and Jibble (work screenshots, as described in the Monitoring policy). Some of them store or handle data outside the Philippines. We use them only for the purposes above.

## How long we keep it

- **People records:** while you work with ERS and for **five years after the engagement ends**. After that they are kept for **one more year in a restricted archive** that only HR can open, then deleted or anonymized unless the law needs us to keep them longer.
- **Training results and certificates:** **five years** after you complete the course, then **one more year in a restricted archive** that only HR can open, then deleted or anonymized unless the law needs us to keep them longer. Your answers to individual exam and practice-call questions are removed after **12 months**; your scores and pass or fail results stay.
- **Time records and approved hours:** the engagement and **three years** after it ends.
- **Tax and payment records:** for the period the law requires.
- **Applications:** **12 months** after a rejection (or after we told you that you did not meet the requirements) and **6 months** after a withdrawal, then anonymized. If you ticked "keep my details for 12 months", we keep it until that agreement ends, then the periods above apply. If you are hired, your application becomes part of your people record.
- **Time-claim screenshots you upload:** deleted 90 days after the decision.
- **Security log:** at least three years.
- **Safe Voice reports** are anonymous and covered by their own page.

## Your rights

You may, at any time and free of charge:

- be told what personal data we hold and how it is used;
- see it and get a copy (use "My data" in ELEVATE);
- ask us to correct what is wrong or out of date;
- object to some uses, or withdraw consent you gave;
- ask us to block, remove or erase data that is no longer needed or was collected unlawfully, where the law allows;
- be indemnified for damages caused by a breach of your rights; and
- complain to the **National Privacy Commission** (privacy.gov.ph).

To use these rights, open "My data" in ELEVATE, or contact the ERS HR team. We answer within a reasonable time and will tell you if we cannot do what you ask and why.

## Security

Sign-in needs a password or Google and a second step. Sensitive details are encrypted, files are private, and access is limited by role and logged. If a breach affecting you occurs, we will tell you and the National Privacy Commission as the law requires.

## Changes

If we change this notice, you will be asked to read the new version the next time you sign in. Contact the ERS HR team with any question.
