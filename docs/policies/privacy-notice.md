## About this notice

Elite Resource Services ("ERS", "we") runs ELEVATE, the internal system we use to manage the people who work with us and to receive job applications. This notice explains what personal data ELEVATE holds about you, why, who can see it, how long we keep it and your rights under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173). You will be asked to confirm you have read it.

**Who is responsible:** ERS is the personal information controller. The **ERS HR team** holds our Data Protection Officer role; reach them through the Privacy policy page at https://elevate.eliteresourceservices.com/privacy or in ELEVATE under "My data".

## What we collect

- **Account details:** your name, work email, sign-in method (password or Google) and two-step sign-in. With Google we receive only your name, email address and profile picture.
- **Your profile and work records:** position, team, who you report to, assigned clients, start date and status, schedule, time off and prize days, equipment, and documents you or HR upload (for example your contract, ID copies and certificates).
- **Personal details:** date of birth, civil status, phone, home address and emergency contact, where provided.
- **Sensitive details:** government ID numbers (TIN, SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG), bank or payout details and your pay rate. These are encrypted. IDs and bank details are visible only to you and the HR and administrator roles that need them for payroll and compliance.
- **Optional self-identification:** your gender, pronouns, sexual orientation and religion, only if you choose to share them in your profile or, if you apply for a job, in the optional part of the application form. Every question can be skipped and you can change or remove answers at any time. Gender, sexual orientation and religion are stored encrypted. Pronouns are shown next to your name. The other three are visible to colleagues only if you switch on "Show on my profile to colleagues" for that answer (off unless you turn it on); otherwise nobody can see them. Job application answers are never shown by name. HR sees only combined counts of 5 or more people, to check that our workplace and hiring are fair. We never use these answers to decide whether to hire, promote, pay or assess anyone. They are deleted when your record is removed.
- **Working time:** clock-in, break and clock-out records, the network address you clocked in from, time correction requests and end-of-shift notes. See our Monitoring policy for Jibble screenshots and optional location.
- **Messages:** what you send in ELEVATE chats (text, files, pictures, voice notes, polls and GIFs), who is in each chat, when you read it, and an optional "online" dot (you can appear offline). Message text is encrypted. Only the people in a chat can read it; ERS staff cannot open a chat unless a second person approves a logged request. Chats are not used to score or rate you.
- **Performance:** goals, reviews, ratings and feedback.
- **Training records:** the ELEVATE Learn courses assigned to you (for example HIPAA, cybersecurity and customer service), modules finished and about how long you spent, exam answers and scores, your replies and result in a practice call, the statement you accept at the end of a course, and your certificates (with the network address recorded when you accept). We do not record your voice or screen or which videos you play.
- **Engagement and exit records:** onboarding and offboarding checklists, agreements you sign in ELEVATE, exit interview answers and certificates.
- **Job applications:** the details and resume you send through our careers page, where you heard about the job (if you say), and our hiring team's notes. The team may tag applications and combine duplicates. We count how many of the job's keywords your resume contains, as a reading aid only: the resume text is not kept, nothing about you personally is used and no computer makes a decision. If you tick "keep my details for 12 months", we keep your application to contact you about other roles; you can ask us to remove it at any time.
- **Security records:** a log of important actions (for example sign-ins and who opened a sensitive record).

We do not store client or patient information in ELEVATE. Please never upload it or write it in a chat.

## Why we use it, and our basis

- **To manage your engagement with ERS:** onboarding, scheduling, time records, time off, equipment, reviews, agreements and ending the engagement. Basis: our contract with you and our legitimate interest in running the business.
- **To let you talk with colleagues:** chat in ELEVATE. Basis: our contract with you and our legitimate interest in running the business.
- **To pay and report correctly:** hours records used for payment, and records the law requires for tax and social contributions. Basis: contract and legal obligation.
- **To train and show training was done:** assigning courses, grading exams and practice calls and issuing certificates, so ERS and clients can show required training (for example HIPAA) was done. Scores are calculated by the system and people review any result that affects you. Basis: contract, legal obligation and legitimate interest. Leads and clients see whether you completed a course, never your scores.
- **To protect people, clients and systems:** security logs, sign-in checks and the monitoring in our Monitoring policy. Basis: legitimate interest and, where the law requires, your consent.
- **To hire:** to assess applications and make offers. Basis: your request and our legitimate interest.

We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising or decide anything about you by computer alone. People review everything that affects you.

## Who can see it

- **You** can see your own records under "My data".
- **Your team leads** see what they need to manage their team: schedule, time records, leave requests and reviews they take part in. They do not see your birth date, civil status, personal email, home address or sensitive details, nor your optional gender, sexual orientation or religion unless you chose to show them.
- **HR** sees employee records and, with administrators, the sensitive details; each time one is opened, it is logged.
- **Pay:** your pay is visible to you and to staff individually chosen to run payroll. HR and administrator roles can also see the pay rate held on your record. Payroll figures are encrypted, and every change to them is kept in a permanent history.
- **Training results:** you, HR and the Privacy Officer see your scores and answers. Your lead and clients see only whether you passed.
- **Chats:** the people in a chat. HR can create, rename and delete group chats and choose who is in them, but cannot read them. Clients invited as guests see only the chats they were added to.
- **Clients** are told your name, role and working hours where needed to serve them, never your sensitive details or personal records.
- **Authorities and other parties** only where the law requires it, or with your consent.

## Companies that help us run ELEVATE

These providers process data for us under their own data-protection terms: Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage, hosted in Singapore), Vercel (hosting), Google (sign-in if you choose it), Brevo and Resend (email), Upstash (abuse protection), Inngest (scheduled tasks), KLIPY (GIF search: it receives only the words searched, never who you are) and Jibble (work screenshots, as described in the Monitoring policy). Some handle data outside the Philippines. We use them only for the purposes above.

## How long we keep it

- **People records:** while you work with ERS and for **five years after the engagement ends**. After that they are kept for **one more year in a restricted archive** that only HR can open, then deleted or anonymized unless the law needs us to keep them longer.
- **Training results and certificates:** **five years** after you complete the course, then **one more year in a restricted archive** that only HR can open, then deleted or anonymized unless the law needs us to keep them longer. Answers to individual exam and practice-call questions are removed after **12 months**; scores and pass or fail stay.
- **Time records and approved hours:** the engagement and **three years** after it ends.
- **Messages and files:** **one year** after they are sent, then deleted. A message you delete is hidden at once and removed with the rest.
- **Tax and payment records:** as long as the law requires.
- **Applications:** **12 months** after a rejection and **6 months** after a withdrawal, then anonymized. If you ticked "keep my details for 12 months", we keep it until that ends. If you are hired, your application becomes part of your people record.
- **Time-claim screenshots you upload:** 90 days after the decision.
- **Security log:** at least three years.
- **Safe Voice reports** are anonymous and covered by their own page.

## Your rights

You may, at any time and free of charge:

- be told what personal data we hold and how it is used, and get a copy (use "My data" in ELEVATE);
- ask us to correct what is wrong or out of date;
- object to some uses, or withdraw consent you gave;
- ask us to block, remove or erase data that is no longer needed or was collected unlawfully, where the law allows;
- be indemnified for damages caused by a breach of your rights; and
- complain to the **National Privacy Commission** (privacy.gov.ph).

To use these rights, open "My data" or contact the ERS HR team. We answer within a reasonable time and will tell you if we cannot do what you ask, and why.

## Security

Sign-in needs a password or Google and a second step. Sensitive details are encrypted, files are private, and access is limited by role and logged. If a breach affects you, we will tell you and the National Privacy Commission as the law requires.

If we change this notice, you will be asked to read the new version the next time you sign in.
