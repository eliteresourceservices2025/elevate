Social · Voice
  • Speak like one operator to another — direct, specific, low ego.
  • Caption opens with a number, observation, or claim — never "Excited to share…".
  • Credit the team and the client by name when the post permits it.
  • Hashtags belong below a line break, max five, all relevant to the trade.
Social · Avoid
  • Trend-chasing, dance reels, or AI-generated stock imagery.
  • Memes that make the client, the trade, or the work the punchline.
  • Logo on full-bleed photography — always reserve a $off or $pdd plate.
  • Engagement bait ("comment YES below…"). Ever.

Focus & rulesWCAG 2.4.7 · 1.4.11

All interactive elements show a visible focus ring at 3 px in $p, offset 3 px. Never remove :focus-visible — only restyle it.

  • Don't rely on color alone. Pair status colors with an icon or label (e.g. badges have a dot prefix).
  • Body copy is 16 px minimum; captions stay at 11 px in mono only — never for prose.
  • Touch targets ≥ 44 × 44 px (WCAG 2.5.5). Small buttons keep 40 px hit-area via padding.
  • Motion respects prefers-reduced-motion; no auto-playing transitions over 200 ms.
  • Form fields show labels above inputs, never placeholder-only — and pair errors with text, not just a red border.
  • Logo < 40 px drops to the standalone yin-yang mark; the wordmark falls below legibility threshold.
