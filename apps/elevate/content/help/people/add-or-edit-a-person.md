A person's **record** holds their name, position, team and manager. When they sign in with the same work email, their account links to the record automatically.

# Add a person

1. Click **People** in the left menu.
2. Click **Add person**.
3. Fill in the form. It has the sections **Name**, **Employment**, **Team and manager**, **Contact** and **Personal**.
   - **Legal first name** and **Legal last name** are required. **Preferred name** is the name shown in the directory.
   - **Work email** must be the email they will use to sign in.
   - **Worker type**, **Position** and **Start date** are under **Employment**.
   - Choose the **Manager** and **Team** from the lists. Pick a name from the list, do not type a new one.
4. Click **Add person**. A message says **Person added.**

If ELEVATE shows **Possible duplicate**, check the People list first. The person may already exist.

Government IDs and bank details are not on this form. Add them afterwards on the person's profile, where they are stored encrypted.

# Edit a person

1. Open the person's profile from **People**.
2. Open the tab you want to change and use its edit button.
3. Click **Save changes**. A message says **Changes saved.**

Every change is saved in the **History** tab.

# Common mistakes

- **A typo in the work email.** The person's account will not link to the record. Fix the email on the record.
- **Picking the wrong manager.** Their reporting line and approvals follow the manager. See "Set who reports to whom".

# Try it yourself

- Add a test person with a fake email address.
- Change their preferred name and check **History**.
