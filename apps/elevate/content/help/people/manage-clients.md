# The client list

Clients are the companies ERS people work for. Only the name and time zone are stored. Never enter patient or customer information.

1. Click **People** in the left menu, then **Manage**, then **Clients**.
2. To add one, fill in:
   - **Client name**
   - **Time zone**
   - **Holidays**: which holiday calendars apply, **US holidays** and **Philippine holidays**.
   - **Works with patient information**: tick it for clients with patient data. People assigned there take the full HIPAA training. Turn it off for clients with no patient data.
3. Click **Add client**.

Each client in the list shows its time zone, holiday calendars and whether it handles patient information. Use **Edit** to change it, or **Mark inactive** when you stop working with them.

![Clients: name, time zone, holidays and whether they work with patient information.](help:manage-clients/01-clients.png)

# Assign a person to a client

1. Open the person's profile and click the **Client assignments** tab.
2. Choose the **Client**, set the **Start date** and **Hours per week**.
3. Save.

Choosing a client that handles patient information shows the person's HIPAA training status as a reminder. It never blocks the assignment.

# Change or end an assignment

On the same tab, change the **Assigned client**, the dates or the hours. Set an **End date (optional)** when the person stops working for that client. A new client assignment ends the old one the day before.

# Why it matters

- The client's time zone shows on schedules.
- The client's holidays are left out of absence flags and time-off day counts.
- Training is assigned from the client's assignment.
- Extra hours are requested for a client the person is assigned to.

# Common mistakes

- **Typing patient details as a client name.** Use only the company's name.
- **Forgetting to end an old assignment.** The person keeps showing there.

# Try it yourself

- Open **Clients** and read one entry.
- Open a person's **Client assignments** tab.
