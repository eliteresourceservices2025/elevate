The **Structure** page holds the lists everything else uses. Who reports to whom is not set here. It is set on each person's profile and shown on the org chart.

# Open Structure

Click **People** in the left menu, then **Manage**, then **Structure**.

![Structure: departments, teams and positions.](help:set-up-departments-teams-and-positions/01-structure.png)

# Departments

A department is the top level. Each team belongs to one department.

1. Under **Departments**, type a name and click **Add** (the field is called **New department**).
2. Use **Rename** to change a name.
3. Use **Archive** to retire one. A department that still has active teams cannot be archived. Archive or move the teams first.

# Teams

A team is where people sit. A person is in one team. Their manager is set separately.

1. Under **Teams**, click **Add a team**. Choose its department.
2. Each team shows its department and how many people it has.
3. **Rename** or **Archive** a team the same way.

# Positions

Positions are the job titles people can hold. People pick from this list instead of typing a title.

1. Under **Positions**, click **Add a position**.
2. Each position shows how many people hold it.
3. **Rename** or **Archive** a position as needed.

# Good to know

- Renaming keeps the history, because the person is linked to the item, not to the word.
- Archiving never deletes. It only hides the item from new choices.
- A position can have its own onboarding checklist. See "Edit onboarding and offboarding checklists".

# Common mistakes

- **Making a new team for every small change.** Rename the old one.
- **Archiving a team with people in it.** Move them first.

# Try it yourself

- Open **Structure** and read the three lists.
