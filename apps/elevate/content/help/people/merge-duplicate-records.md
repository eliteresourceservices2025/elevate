A person who changed their name or work email can end up in the directory and the org chart twice. A merge keeps one record and archives the other. The sign-in account follows the person.

# Open Duplicates

1. Click **People** in the left menu.
2. Click **Manage**, then **Duplicates**.

# Records Elevate found

Under **People who may be in the directory twice**, ELEVATE lists records that share a name, a personal email, a mobile number or a birth date. If there are none, the page says so.

![Duplicate people: choose the record that stays, and merge by hand.](help:merge-duplicate-records/01-duplicates.png)

# Choose which record stays

For each pair, choose **Keep this record**. The other one is archived. **Keep the older record**, because it holds the history.

# Merge two records by hand

When the name and email on the two records are different, because someone changed both, ELEVATE cannot see the link.

1. Under **Merge two records by hand**, choose the record to keep in **Keep this record**. Choose the older one, with the history.
2. In **Merge this one into it (it is archived)**, choose the other record.
3. If the person changed their email, tick the box about the record that stays taking the other record's work email. Use it when the account they sign in with now belongs to the other record.
4. Confirm. A box asks **Merge these two records?** and tells you the second one is archived and nothing is deleted. Anything recorded on it, such as time clock, leave and documents, moves to the kept record.

The list shows which records already have a sign-in account. If the record already has an account, ELEVATE asks before it replaces it. The old account stays, without a people record.

# Sign-in accounts with no record

The page also lists **Sign-in accounts with no people record**. That is someone who signed in with an email HR did not have on file. Link the account to their existing record so they do not get a second one. A message says **Account linked.**

# What happens

- The record you keep stays active with its history.
- The other record is archived. Nothing is deleted.
- The sign-in account moves to the kept record.
- The merge is written to the audit log.

# Common mistakes

- **Keeping the newer record.** You lose the clear history on the page. Keep the older one.
- **Merging two different people with the same name.** Check the email, birth date and start date first.

# Try it yourself

- Open **Duplicates** and read the list.
- Look at the **Merge two records by hand** choices (do not confirm one).
