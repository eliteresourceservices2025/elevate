Archiving takes a person out of the People list. Their history stays. Nothing is deleted.

Normally you do this through **Offboarding**, which also handles equipment and final hours. See "Offboard someone who is leaving". Use this guide for a record that should simply be closed.

# Before you start

- A person who still has people reporting to them cannot be archived. Move their reports first. See "Set who reports to whom".

# Steps

1. Open the person's profile.
2. Click the **More actions** button (three dots) at the top.
3. Click **Archive**.
4. A box asks **Archive this person?** and says they disappear from the directory but their history is kept. Confirm.

# Bring someone back

Open the archived person's profile, click **More actions** and choose **Restore record**.

# Common mistakes

- **Archiving a person who only changed teams.** Change their team instead. Archiving is for people who left.

# Try it yourself

- Archive a test person, then restore them.
