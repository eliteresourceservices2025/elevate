Use the **People** page to find a person and see their details.

# Step 1: Open People

Click **People** in the left menu. The page shows how many people there are.

# Step 2: Search or filter

Type a name in **Search people** and search. You can also use the filters. When filters are on, a link lets you clear them to see everyone again.

![The People list. Search by name, then click a person.](help:find-a-profile/01-people-list.png)

# Step 3: Open a profile

Click the person's name. Their profile has tabs:

- **Personal** has their name, work email and contact details.
- **Employment** has position, team, manager and start date.
- **Government IDs** and **Payout** are hidden by default and only HR can see them.
- **Emergency** has their emergency contacts.
- **Client assignments** shows which clients they work for.
- **Documents** has their files and folders.
- **History** shows what changed and when.

You only see the tabs your role allows.

![A profile has tabs: Personal, Employment, Documents and more.](help:find-a-profile/02-profile-tabs.png)

# What a Team Lead sees

A Team Lead sees a limited view of the people who report to them. Birth date, civil status, personal email and home address are not shown.

# Private information

Government IDs and bank details are hidden by default. Showing them is recorded in the audit log. Only reveal them when you really need to.

# Try it yourself

- Search for a person by name.
- Open three tabs on their profile.
- Open **History** and read the last change.
