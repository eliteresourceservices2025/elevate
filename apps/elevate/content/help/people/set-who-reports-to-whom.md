Who someone reports to decides who sees their profile, reviews and requests.

# Change a manager or team

1. Open the person's profile and click the **Employment** tab.
2. Find **Team and manager**.
3. Choose the **Manager** and **Team** from the lists.
4. Set the **Effective date**. It must be today or earlier.
5. Save. A message says **Reporting updated.**

ELEVATE keeps the old assignment in the person's history. It will not allow loops (two people who report to each other).

![The Employment tab, where the manager, team and attendance lead are set.](help:set-who-reports-to-whom/01-employment-tab.png)

# Set an attendance lead (optional)

Some people report their time to a lead who is not their manager.

1. On the **Employment** tab, find **Attendance lead (optional)**.
2. Pick the lead and save. A message says **Attendance lead saved.**
3. To remove it, clear the choice and save. A message says **Attendance lead removed.**

The attendance lead handles time matters only: hours review, time corrections, extra hours and time off. The manager keeps everything else. The lead must hold the Team Lead role to be able to approve.

# Move all of a manager's reports to someone else

Do this before a manager leaves. Use **New manager** on the same card. It moves everyone who reports to them in one step. A message says how many people were reassigned.

# Common mistakes

- **Archiving a manager first.** ELEVATE will refuse. Move their reports first.
- **A date in the future.** Use today or an earlier date.

# Try it yourself

- Change a test person's team and read the new line in **History**.
- Set an attendance lead, then remove it.
