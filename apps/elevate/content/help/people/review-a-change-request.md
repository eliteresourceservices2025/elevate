People cannot change their own contact details, emergency contacts or bank details. They send a request, and HR decides.

# Open Change requests

1. Click **People** in the left menu.
2. Click **Manage**, then **Change requests**.

The list is split into **Pending**, **Approved** and **Rejected**. If nothing is waiting, it says **Nothing here.**

![Change requests: Pending, Approved and Rejected.](help:review-a-change-request/01-requests.png)

# Decide a request

1. Open a pending request. It shows what the person wants to change and the current values.
2. For a bank change, the details are hidden. Use **View requested bank details**. Each view is recorded.
3. Check it against what you know. If you are not sure, ask the person.
4. You can add a **Note to the person (optional)**.
5. Approve it or reject it. A message says **Approved and applied.** or **Rejected.**

Approving applies the change to their profile at once, and the change is written to their history and the audit log.

# Rules

- You cannot review a request on your own record. Another HR Admin does.
- Bank details are stored encrypted and never shown in messages or email.
- A person can send a new request once the last one is handled.

# What the person sees

They send it from their profile with **Request a change to my contact details** (or emergency contacts, or bank details). They see **Sent to HR for approval.**

# Common mistakes

- **Approving a bank change without checking.** A wrong account means a failed payment. Confirm with the person first.
- **Leaving requests waiting.** People expect an answer.

# Try it yourself

- Open **Change requests** and look at the three lists.
