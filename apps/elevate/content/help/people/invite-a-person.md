Use this guide when someone new needs an ELEVATE account. You send an invitation to their email address. They use it to sign up. Nobody can sign up without an invitation.

# Before you start

- You need the **HR Admin** or **Super Admin** role.
- Ask the person which email address they want to use. It must be the same address they type when they sign up.

# Step 1: Open Invitations

Click **Settings** in the left menu. Then click **Invitations**.

# Step 2: Type their email and click Invite

Type the person's email in the box called **Invite by email**. Click the **Invite** button.

A green message appears at the bottom of the screen. It says **Invited** and the email address. ELEVATE emails them the sign-up link.

# Step 3: Check that it worked

The person now shows in the list below with the status **Pending**. When they finish signing up, the status changes to **Accepted**.

# What if they did not get the email?

1. Ask them to look in their spam or junk folder.
2. If it is not there, tell them to open the ELEVATE sign-in page and click **Accept an invite**. They type the exact email address you invited.

# Good to know

- An invitation lasts **7 days**. After that, send a new one.
- New people start with the **Employee** role. Only a Super Admin can give other roles when inviting.
- To cancel an invitation, click **Revoke** on that row.

# Common mistakes

- **A typo in the email.** The invitation only works for the exact address. Revoke it and invite again with the right one.
- **Inviting someone who already has an account.** Search for them under **People** first.

# Try it yourself

- Invite a test address you own.
- Find it in the list and check its status.
- Revoke it.
