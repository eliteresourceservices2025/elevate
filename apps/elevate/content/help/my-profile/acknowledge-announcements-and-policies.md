# Where to find them

Click **Announcements** in the left menu. It has two tabs: **Announcements** for company news, and **Policies** for the rules everyone follows.

![Announcements: the ones that need your acknowledgment are marked.](help:acknowledge-announcements-and-policies/01-announcements.png)

# Items that need you

When something needs your acknowledgment, a note at the top of the page says **1 item needs your acknowledgment**, or how many. The item itself shows **Acknowledgment needed**.

# Acknowledge

1. Open the announcement or policy and read it.
2. Click **I have read and understand**. A message says **Thank you. Your acknowledgment is recorded.** It records your name, the version and the time. Nothing else.

Once you have acknowledged it, it shows **Acknowledged** with the date. If an announcement was not addressed to you, the page says you do not need to acknowledge it.

![Read it, then click I have read and understand.](help:acknowledge-announcements-and-policies/02-acknowledge.png)

# Policies have versions

When a policy changes, HR publishes a new version with **What changed**, so you can see the difference. You acknowledge each version. An old version never changes.

# The privacy notice

The privacy notice is the first policy. You must accept it before you use ELEVATE. A screen asks for it when you sign in.

# Emails about this

Choose which emails you get under **Settings**, then **Email notifications**. Emails about something you must acknowledge are always sent.

# Common mistakes

- **Acknowledging without reading.** You are confirming that you read it.
- **Ignoring a reminder.** HR can see who has not acknowledged yet.

# Try it yourself

- Open **Announcements** and check both tabs.
- Open **Settings > Email notifications** and read the two choices.
