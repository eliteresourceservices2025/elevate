Your documents are kept in a private vault on your profile. You and HR can see them. Nobody else can.

# Never upload client or patient information

The page says it clearly: **Do not upload client records or patient information.** This vault is for employment documents only. When you upload, you also confirm that the file contains no client or patient information.

# Upload one document

1. Click **My profile** in the left menu, then open the **Documents** tab.
2. Click **Upload a document** above your file list. In the window that opens, choose the **Document type**, for example **Government ID**, **NBI clearance**, **HIPAA training certificate**, **Contract** or **Resume**.
3. Type a **Title**, such as "NBI clearance 2026".
4. Add an **Expiry date (optional)** if the document expires.
5. Choose the **File**. It can be a PDF, JPG, PNG, WEBP, DOC or DOCX, up to 10 MB.
6. Tick that the file contains no client or patient information.
7. Click **Upload**.

![Your Documents tab: choose the type, add a title and the file, then Upload.](help:upload-your-documents/01-documents.png)

# Upload many files

Click **Upload many files** above your file list to choose or drag in up to 50 files at once. Keep the window open until the uploads finish. They are saved as **Unsorted**. Open **More**, then **Set type** on each file to give it a type afterwards.

# Sort into folders

Click **New folder** to make a folder with a plain name, such as "Contracts". Then open **More** on a document, choose **Move to folder**, select the folder and click **Move document**. Do not put private details or client names in a folder name.

# After you upload

HR can verify a document. A document that expires is listed for HR, and you get reminders. Your required documents also count toward your onboarding checklist. ELEVATE ticks that task by itself when they are all there.

# Common mistakes

- **Choosing the wrong type.** HR may not count it. Open **More**, then **Change type** to fix it before HR verifies the file.
- **A big scan.** Files over 10 MB are refused. Make the file smaller.
- **A screenshot with client information.** Do not upload it.

# Try it yourself

- Open the **Documents** tab and read the document types.
- Create a folder.
