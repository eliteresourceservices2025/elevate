When you join, you get an onboarding checklist. It lists what you, HR and your lead need to do so you can start well.

# Find it

Click **Onboarding** in the left menu. At the top, **Your tasks** lists the tasks that are yours, with their due dates. Some have an **Open** link that takes you straight to the right page. Below, **Your onboarding** shows your checklist with how many tasks are done, and a note if some are late. Click your name to open the full checklist. If the page says **Nothing here yet**, you have no checklist, and you have nothing to do.

![Your onboarding checklist: who owns each task and what is due.](help:finish-your-onboarding/01-onboarding.png)

# Read the tasks

On the full checklist, each task shows who owns it: **The person** (you), **HR** or **Their lead**. It also shows a due date. A task that is past due says **Late**. A task that does not block you says **Optional**.

# Your tasks

The usual ones for you are:

- **Acknowledge the privacy notice.** Read it and acknowledge it. See "Read and acknowledge announcements and policies".
- **Acknowledge the monitoring policy.** Needed before the clock can use screenshots, location or selfies.
- **Upload your required documents.** See "Upload your documents".
- **Create your ELEVATE account and set up your sign-in code.** See "Set up two-step sign-in".
- **Complete your HIPAA training.** See "Take your training".

# What ELEVATE ticks for you

Many tasks say **ELEVATE ticks this when it is done**. For example, when you acknowledge the policy or upload the documents, the task is ticked by itself. This can take up to 30 minutes. For a task you do yourself, click **Mark done**.

# When you are finished

When every required task is done, HR completes your onboarding and your status changes from onboarding to active. HR will tell you if something is missing.

# Common mistakes

- **Marking a task done before you did it.** HR can see and may reopen it.
- **Waiting for ELEVATE to tick a task right away.** It can take up to half an hour.

# Try it yourself

- Open **Onboarding** and read your tasks.
