# Open your profile

Click **My profile** in the left menu. Your name, position, team and manager are at the top. Below are tabs: **Personal**, **Employment**, **Government IDs**, **Payout**, **Emergency**, **Client assignments**, **Documents** and **History**.

![My profile: your photo, your details and the request links.](help:update-your-profile/01-profile.png)

# Add a photo

Click **Add a photo** (or **Change photo** later) and choose a picture. You can remove it again.

# Edit about me

On the **Personal** tab, click **Edit about me**. The form is called **About me (optional)** and asks for your **Gender**, **Pronouns** and **Religion**. Every question is optional, and you can skip any of them. Your pronouns are the only part other people can see. Nobody else reads your gender or religion, and they are never used for hiring, pay, promotion or reviews.

# Change your contact details

You cannot change these yourself. HR checks every change first.

1. On the **Personal** tab, click **Request a change to my contact details**.
2. Change what you need: your **Personal email**, **Mobile** or address. The page says: **Change what you need. HR reviews it before it applies.**
3. Send it. A message says **Sent to HR for approval.**

![Request a change to your contact details. HR reviews it before it applies.](help:update-your-profile/02-contact-request.png)

# Emergency contacts

1. Open the **Emergency** tab.
2. Click **Request a change to my emergency contacts**.
3. Add up to five people HR can call in an emergency, with their **Name**, **Relationship** and **Phone**. Choose one primary contact.
4. Send it for approval.

# Bank details

Open the **Payout** tab and click **Request a change to my bank details**. Enter the **Bank name**, **Account name** and **Account number**. They are stored encrypted and shown hidden. HR approves the change.

# After you send a request

HR sees it under **Change requests** and either approves or rejects it. When it is approved, your profile updates. You can send another once the first is handled.

# Common mistakes

- **Typing a wrong account number.** Check it twice before you send it.
- **Expecting an instant change.** HR has to approve it first.
