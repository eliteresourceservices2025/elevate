**Prize days off** are days HR awards, for example for winning a game or for good work. There is no accrual. A person's balance is the total of all awards and uses. It is never typed in by hand, and every entry is logged.

# Award prize days

1. Click **Time off** in the left menu and open the **Award** tab.
2. Under **Award prize days**, choose the **Person** from the list.
3. Choose the **Leave type**. If there are none, add one on the **Types** tab first.
4. Enter the number of **Days**. An award is at most 5 days, whole or half days.
5. Write the **Game or reason**.
6. Optionally set **Use by (optional)**. Leave it empty if the days do not expire.
7. Submit. A message says **Prize days awarded.**

You cannot award days to yourself. Another admin must do it.

# Correct a balance

Use **Correct a balance** only for mistakes.

1. Choose the person and leave type.
2. Enter **Days (+ or -)**. A minus sign removes days.
3. Write the **Reason** and submit. A message says **Balance corrected.**

A balance cannot go below zero. You cannot correct your own days.

# Look at balances

The **Balances** tab shows who holds prize days, their **Balance** and their **Next expiry**. If nobody holds any yet, it says so.

# Holidays

On the **Holidays** tab you add or import holidays for a year. Only holidays marked as checked count: they are left out of absence flags and day counts. Tick each holiday as verified, or use the button to mark a year as checked.

# Expiry

Days that pass their **Use by** date and were not used expire automatically. The days that expire soonest are used first.

# Common mistakes

- **Using Correct a balance for a normal award.** Use Award so there is a reason on record.
- **A forgotten expiry date.** Awards without a "use by" date never expire.

# Try it yourself

- Award 1 day to a test person with a reason.
- Look at the **Balances** tab to see it.
