Our workers are independent contractors, so there is no accrued paid leave. Days off are **prize days off** that HR awards. A request for a day off goes to the person's lead first, then to HR.

# Open Time off

Click **Time off** in the left menu. The tabs you see depend on your role. They include **My prize days**, **Requests**, **Approvals**, **Calendar** and, for HR, **Balances**, **Types**, **Holidays** and **Award**.

![The Time off page with its tabs. Approvals holds the requests waiting for you.](help:decide-a-time-off-request/01-time-off.png)

# Decide a request

1. Open the **Approvals** tab.
2. Read each request: the person, the dates and the type. If nothing is waiting, the page says so.
3. Click **Approve**, or **Decline**.

How it moves:

- **Waiting for lead.** The person's lead (including an attendance lead who is a Team Lead, or an Executive who is the person's manager) decides first.
- Then HR gives the final decision.
- HR can only decline at the lead step, not approve. If the lead step waits 4 working days, HR is asked to step in.
- Nobody decides their own request.

![The Approvals tab: read the request, then Approve or Decline.](help:decide-a-time-off-request/02-approvals.png)

# Days counted

Days are working days. Weekends and holidays are not counted. A request cannot start in the past. HR can file one for someone with **File for someone**.

# See who is away

Open the **Calendar** tab. HR sees everyone. A Team Lead sees their team. Other people see their teammates' approved days off. An Executive sees only counts per day.

# Cancel

A request that is still waiting can be cancelled by the person. Approved time off can be cancelled by the lead or HR while it has not started.

# What the person does

People use **Request time off** and see their requests in **My requests**. When approved, they get a calendar invite.

# Common mistakes

- **Approving without checking the calendar.** Look at who else is away.
- **Saying "PTO" or "paid leave".** Use "prize days off" or "time off".

# Try it yourself

- Open **Approvals** and read one request.
- Open the **Calendar** and find a day with someone away.
