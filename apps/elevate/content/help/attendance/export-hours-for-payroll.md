HR exports the approved hours for each pay period. The file shows hours only. ELEVATE does not compute pay.

# Before you start

- Make sure every week in the period is approved. See "Approve weekly hours".

# Steps

1. Click **Attendance** in the left menu.
2. Open the **Hours export** tab, called **Export hours for payroll**.
3. Look at the progress note. It says either that all finished days are approved, or how many days are not approved yet and for how many people. Unapproved days are left out of the file.
4. Choose the **Pay period**.
5. Click one of:
   - **Download daily detail (CSV)**, a row per day.
   - **Download per-person summary (CSV)**, totals per person, with absent and late days and approved leave days.
6. A message tells you how many rows were downloaded.

![The Hours export tab: choose the pay period and download.](help:export-hours-for-payroll/01-hours-export.png)

# Include days that are not approved

Tick **Also include days that are not approved yet (they are labelled)** if you need them. Be careful: those numbers are not final.

Hours that were corrected after approval are left out until they are approved again.

# Change how pay periods are cut

Under **How pay periods are cut**, choose semi-monthly (the default), weekly, every two weeks, or monthly. For every two weeks, set **A Monday a period starts on**. Save to see **Pay periods saved.**

# Common mistakes

- **Exporting before approving.** Days that are not approved are missing from the file.
- **Sharing the file by chat or email.** It holds personal information. Keep it where payroll keeps files.

# Try it yourself

- Open the Hours export tab and read the progress note.
- Download the daily detail for a test period.
