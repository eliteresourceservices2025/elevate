ELEVATE is the time clock and the only source of hours. Each week, a lead checks the hours and approves them. Approved hours are what HR exports for payroll. Approving hours computes nothing; pay is worked out in the Payroll module.

# Open Hours review

Click **Hours review** in the left menu. It starts on last week. Use **Previous** and **Next** to move between weeks.

A Team Lead sees their own team. HR sees everyone.

![Hours review: check each week and approve it.](help:approve-weekly-hours/01-hours-review.png)

# Read a person's week

Each row shows the person's **Scheduled** time, **Worked** time, **Extra** time and any **Flags**. The status tells you where it stands:

- **Not approved**
- **Partly approved**
- **Approved**
- **Changed after approval** means a correction changed a day after it was approved. Approve it again.
- **Nothing to approve yet**

Open **Days** on a row to see each day.

# Approve

- Click **Approve week** on a person's row. A message says how many days were approved.
- Or click **Approve everyone without flags** to approve all the clean weeks at once. People with flags are left for you to check.

You approve only the days that have finished. A day still running (**Day not over**) waits.

# Take an approval back

If a week was approved by mistake, or the hours changed, open **Days** on the person's row and click **Reopen** on a day, or click **Reopen week**. Write why (at least 5 characters). The days show **Not approved** again and leave the payroll export until you approve them again. Nothing is deleted: the reason stays in the history, and the person is told.

- HR can reopen any approval. A lead can reopen only the approvals they gave, within 7 days.
- You cannot reopen your own hours.
- Days that are already in an approved or paid payroll run cannot be reopened here. Ask payroll to correct them in Payroll.

# Very long sessions

A session longer than 12 hours is flagged **Very long session (check the clock-out)**. It is almost always a forgotten clock-out. The week cannot be approved until it is fixed. Open **Days** on the row and click **Fix clock-out**: it opens **Change someone's time** on that day, where you remove the wrong clock-out and add the real one, with a reason. HR saves the change at once. A lead's change goes to HR to approve. Once the change is saved, approve the week.

A day with no schedule shows **No schedule**. **Rest day** means the person has a schedule but no shift that day.

![Open Days to see each day, then click Approve week.](help:approve-weekly-hours/02-approve-week.png)

# Rules

- You can never approve your own hours.
- Approval is saved as it was. If a correction changes a day later, it shows as changed.
- ELEVATE refuses while someone's session from that week is still open. Ask them to clock out, or fix it with a correction.

# Test days

If someone only tried the clock, mark that day with **Mark as test**. Add a reason such as "testing the time clock". The day stays in the history but is left out of this review and of payroll exports. You can take the mark off.

# Common mistakes

- **Approving before looking at flags.** Check late, absent and extra hours first.
- **Waiting too long.** You can approve up to 5 weeks back, no more.

# Try it yourself

- Open last week in **Hours review** and read one person's days.
- Approve a clean week for a test person.
