**Activity** shows what the ELEVATE Tracker App recorded while people were clocked in. It holds only the **app name**, active or idle, and blurred screenshots. Hours are never changed by it: hours still come from the time clock. A Team Lead sees their own team. HR sees everyone.

# Who is working now

1. Click **Activity** in the left menu.
2. The row of numbers counts people by state: **Active**, **Idle**, **On break**, **Tracker offline** and **Clocked out**. Click a state to show only those people.
3. Click **Show only people who need attention** to see people whose tracker is offline, or who have been idle for 15 minutes or more.

The page updates by itself every minute. People whose tracker is offline are listed first.

# Open a person's day

1. Click a person's card.
2. Use the arrows by the date to move between days. The page says which time zone the day is in, and times are shown in yours.
3. Read the strip: green is active, amber is idle, and the faint bar behind it is the time they were clocked in.
4. Under **Screenshots**, click a time. You must choose a reason: **Quality check**, **Dispute**, **Coaching** or **Client request**. Your name and the reason are recorded, and the person can see who looked and why.

Open a screenshot only for a real reason. It is a blurred picture, but it can still show what someone was doing.

# Computers (HR and Super Admin)

1. On **Activity**, open the **Devices** tab.
2. Click **Needs attention** to see computers whose permission is missing, that have not reported for a day, or that have a long list waiting to be sent.
3. To stop a computer, click **Sign out** and confirm. The app stops recording at once and must be added again with a new code.

# Settings (HR and Super Admin)

Open **Settings**, then **ELEVATE Tracker**. Here you can change:

- **Idle after**: how long without a keyboard or mouse counts as idle.
- **Offline wait**: how long a clocked-in person's app can stay silent before their lead is told.
- **Low activity below**: the share of recorded time that must be active before a day gets a **Low activity** note.
- **Add a rule**: say which apps count as work.

Changes apply from now on. Days already recorded keep the notes they have.
