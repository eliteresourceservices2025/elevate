The **ELEVATE Tracker App** runs on your work computer while you are clocked in. It records only the **name of the app** you are using and whether you were active or idle. It never records window titles, web addresses or what you type. It also takes a screenshot now and then, blurred on your computer before it is sent.

You can always see what it recorded about you. This page shows you where.

# Download the app

1. Click **ELEVATE Tracker App** in the left menu.
2. Click the **Download** button for your computer: **Download for Windows**, **Download for Apple silicon** or **Download for Intel Mac**. The page picks the right one for the computer you are using. If a button says **Not available yet**, that kind of computer is not supported yet.
3. Open the file and let it install. On a Mac, when asked, allow **Screen Recording** and **Accessibility** in System Settings, then reopen the app.

The page also lists **What changed** in each version. A version marked **Required update** must be installed: the app stops sending until you update. The app updates itself when it can.

# Add your computer

This part appears once your company has published its monitoring policy. You can install the app before then; it records nothing until the policy is published.

1. Click **Attendance** in the left menu. You land on **My time**.
2. Find the card **My day in the ELEVATE Tracker App** and click **Add this computer**.
3. Click **Get a code**. A short code appears. It works once and for 15 minutes.
4. Open the ELEVATE Tracker App on your computer, choose **Sign in with a code** and type it.

If the code runs out, click **Get a new code**.

# Read your day

After the app is connected, the same card shows your day:

- A strip that runs from the start to the end of your day. Green is time you were active, amber is time you were idle, and the faint bar behind it is the time you were clocked in. Move the pointer over a block to see the app and the times.
- **Active**, **Idle**, **Work apps** and **Other apps**, in minutes.
- **Top apps**, with minutes for each.
- **Screenshots**: click a time to open that picture. The link works for one minute. If it runs out, click **Open it again**.

Use the arrows next to the date to move to another day. You cannot go past today.

# See who looked at your screenshots

Open **Who looked at my screenshots** on the same card. It lists the last 30 days: who opened a screenshot, the reason they gave, and how many times. Your own views are not listed.

# If the card says "App not reporting"

The app may be closed, your computer asleep, or the connection down. Open the app and check that it says you are clocked in. If it still does not report, use **Contact support** from Settings.

# Stop the app on a computer

Ask HR to sign the computer out, or ask them to remove it if you no longer use it. The app then stops recording at once.
