The **ELEVATE Tracker App** runs on your work computer while you are clocked in. It records only the **name of the app** you are using and whether you were active or idle. It never records window titles, web addresses or what you type. It also takes a screenshot now and then, blurred on your computer before it is sent.

You can always see what it recorded about you. This page shows you where.

# Download the app

1. Click **ELEVATE Tracker App** in the left menu.
2. Click the **Download** button for your computer: **Download for Windows**, **Download for Apple silicon** or **Download for Intel Mac**. The page picks the right one for the computer you are using. If a button says **Not available yet**, that kind of computer is not supported yet.
3. Open the file. It installs for you only: you do not need an administrator password. When it finishes, the app starts and its window opens on **Connect this computer**.
4. Windows may warn you first ("Windows protected your PC"). That happens while the company's installer is not yet signed with a company certificate. If you downloaded the file from the **ELEVATE Tracker App** page, choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. If you got the file anywhere else, do not run it.

On a Mac, the Mac version is not available yet.

The page also lists **What changed** in each version. A version marked **Required update** must be installed: the app stops sending until you update. The app updates itself: a normal update waits until you are not working, a required one installs at once. After an update the app shows **What's new** once.

# Add your computer

This part appears once your company has published its monitoring policy. You can install the app before then; it records nothing until the policy is published.

1. Click **Attendance** in the left menu. You land on **My time**.
2. Find the card **My day in the ELEVATE Tracker App** and click **Add this computer**.
3. Click **Get a code**. A short code appears. It works once and for 15 minutes.
4. In the ELEVATE Tracker App, on **Connect this computer**, type the code and choose **Connect**.

If the code runs out, click **Get a new code**.

# Use the app

The app sits in the tray, the small icons next to the clock. If you cannot see it, click the arrow ^ to show hidden icons. Click its icon to open the window. Closing the window does not stop the app.

- The big round button clocks you in (play), ends a break (play) or clocks you out (stop). The dark timer shows how long you have worked today. The coffee button starts a break (15 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour or no time limit). These work exactly as in ELEVATE, and ELEVATE records the time. The same choices are in the icon's menu.
- If you work for more than one client or project, the list under the timer shows each one with the time you worked on it today. Press the play button on a row to clock in for it, or the arrows button to move the rest of your shift to it. ELEVATE then shows which client and project each stretch of your day was for. The ELEVATE page follows what you do in the app within about half a minute.
- The app updates itself. When a new version is ready it installs as soon as you are not working (clocked out or on a break), then opens again in the tray and shows what is new.
- The icon is **green** while the app is recording, **grey** when you are clocked out, on a break, or recording is not switched on yet, **orange** when ELEVATE cannot be reached (it keeps what it records and sends it later) and **red** when the app needs an update.
- **What was captured** shows the apps and minutes it noted today and your last blurred picture. Nothing is hidden from you.
- The app never clocks you in or out by itself. Only your click does.
- A team that needs a selfie to clock in, or a person whose hours come from Jibble, clocks in on the ELEVATE page: the buttons in the app say so and stay off.

If you start working on a different computer, ask HR to sign the old one out on the **Devices** page, then connect the new one with a new code.

# Read your day

After the app is connected, the same card shows your day:

- A strip that runs from the start to the end of your day. Green is time you were active, amber is time you were idle, and the faint bar behind it is the time you were clocked in. Move the pointer over a block to see the app and the times.
- **Active**, **Idle**, **Work apps** and **Other apps**, in minutes.
- **Top apps**, with minutes for each.
- **Screenshots**: click a time to open that picture. The link works for one minute. If it runs out, click **Open it again**.

Use the arrows next to the date to move to another day. You cannot go past today.

# See who looked at your screenshots

Open **Who looked at my screenshots** on the same card. It lists the last 30 days: who opened a screenshot, the reason they gave, and how many times. Your own views are not listed.

# If the card says "App not reporting"

The app may be closed, your computer asleep, or the connection down. Open the app and check that it says you are clocked in. If it still does not report, use **Contact support** from Settings.

# Stop the app on a computer

Ask HR to sign the computer out, or ask them to remove it if you no longer use it. The app then stops recording at once.
