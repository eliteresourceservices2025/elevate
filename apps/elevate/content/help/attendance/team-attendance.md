**Team attendance** shows who is working right now, which days need a look, and the notes people write at the end of their day. A Team Lead sees their own team. HR sees everyone.

# Open the page

Click **Team attendance** in the left menu.

# Working now

The **Working now** list shows each person who is clocked in, their team, status and the time they started (**Since**). If nobody is clocked in, it says so.

# Flags in the last two weeks

The **Flags** list shows days that need a look. They are rebuilt every night from the clock events. Common flags:

- **Late** and **left early**, beyond the team's grace time.
- **Absent**, a scheduled day with no clocking, no approved leave and no holiday.
- **Over 12 hours**, a very long day. This often means someone forgot to clock out.
- **Outside IP range**, clocked in from a place the team does not allow.
- **Possibly offline**, when ELEVATE lost contact with their clock.
- Unapproved **extra hours**, work beyond the schedule that nobody approved.

People with no schedule get no late or absent flags.

# End-of-day reports

The **End-of-day reports, last 7 days** list shows the short notes people wrote when they clocked out. If none were sent, it says **No reports yet.**

# Change someone's time

If a clock event was recorded wrongly, use **Change someone's time**. It removes a wrong event and adds the right ones, with a reason. See "Approve a time correction".

# Common mistakes

- **Treating every flag as a problem.** A flag is a prompt to look, not a verdict. Ask the person first.
- **Expecting live numbers for flags.** Flags are rebuilt each night.

# Try it yourself

- Open **Team attendance** and find who is working now.
- Open one flagged day and read why it was flagged.
