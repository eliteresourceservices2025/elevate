The **ELEVATE Tracker App** never changes your hours. If something is wrong with it, your hours in ELEVATE are still correct. A day it did not report may show **Tracker offline** for your lead and HR to look at. That flag never blocks approval.

# The icon is orange or the window says "You are offline"

ELEVATE cannot be reached: your internet may be down, or ELEVATE may be busy. The app keeps what it records and sends it when the connection is back. You do not need to do anything. While it is offline the clock buttons are off, so use the ELEVATE page if you must clock in or out.

# The icon is red or the window says "Update needed"

This version is too old. The app records and sends nothing until it is updated. The clock buttons in the app (and on the ELEVATE page) still work, so you can always clock out. It looks for the update by itself. To look now, use **Update now** in the window, or **Check for updates** in the icon's menu. If it does not update, open the **ELEVATE Tracker App** page in ELEVATE, download the newest version and install it over the old one.

# The clock buttons are off

The app shows the reason in plain words:

- **You clock in Jibble.** ELEVATE reads your hours from there, so the clock in the app is off. This is not a fault.
- **Your team needs a selfie to clock in.** Clock in on the ELEVATE page.
- **You are offline.** See above.

# A button says "You are already clocked in" (or another sentence from ELEVATE)

That is ELEVATE's own answer: your clock was already in that state, for example because you clocked in on the web page a moment ago. Nothing was changed. The window updates within half a minute.

# The code does not work

- **That code has expired.** A code works for 15 minutes. In ELEVATE, open **Attendance**, click **Add this computer**, then **Get a new code**.
- **That code was already used.** A code works once. Get a new one.
- **Recording is not switched on yet.** Your company has not published its monitoring policy. Ask HR.
- **Could not reach ELEVATE.** Check your internet connection and try again.

# I cannot see the app

Look in the tray, the small icons next to the clock. Click the arrow ^ if some are hidden. If it is not running, open **ELEVATE Tracker App** from the Start menu.

# The app asks me to connect again

HR may have signed this computer out, or the sign-in was lost. Get a new code from **Attendance**, then **Add this computer**, and connect again.

# Remove the app

1. Open **Settings**, then **Apps**, then **Installed apps** in Windows, find **ELEVATE Tracker App** and choose **Uninstall**.
2. The uninstaller always removes this computer's sign-in. It also shows a box, **Delete the application data**, which is not ticked. If you tick it, anything the app had recorded but not yet sent is deleted too. Your hours in ELEVATE are never affected.

Ask HR to use **Sign out** for the computer on the **Devices** page as well, so it no longer shows as connected.
