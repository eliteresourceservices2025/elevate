Work beyond a person's schedule needs the client's OK and a lead's approval before it counts. ELEVATE calls this **extra hours**. It labels the hours and never works out pay.

# Open Extra hours

Click **Extra hours** in the left menu. A Team Lead and HR also see **Requests to review**.

# Decide a request

1. Open **Requests to review** and read the request: the client, the time window, the reason and the screenshot of the client's approval.
2. Click **Approve**, or **Decline**. You may change the time window when you approve.

Nobody decides their own request or one they filed. HR decides when nobody is above the person. HR also decides a request made after the fact (the work had already started) when it is more than 7 days old.

# When the client asked

If the client asked for extra hours, a lead or HR files it from **File extra hours the client asked for**. Choose the person, client and window, and add the proof or the client's request. The person then has to **Confirm** or **Decline** it under **Your client asked for extra hours**.

# What the person does

People use **Ask to work extra hours**. They must choose their client, the time (**From** and **Until**), say **Who at the client approved it?**, and attach 1 to 3 screenshots of the client's approval. They see their requests under **My extra hours**.

# Limits

A day has a limit for extra hours. A request is between 15 minutes and 24 hours, up to 30 days ahead and 31 days back.

# Common mistakes

- **Approving with no proof.** The client's approval screenshot is required for a reason.
- **Putting client or patient information in the reason.** Never do this.

# Try it yourself

- Open a test request and read its proof.
- Approve it, then see how it shows in that week's hours review.
