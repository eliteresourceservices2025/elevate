ELEVATE is the time clock and the only source of hours. **Jibble is used only for screenshots.** When someone clocks in or out, ELEVATE tells Jibble. Jibble hours are never used for payroll, and Jibble screenshots are never copied into ELEVATE.

# Open the Jibble page

Click **Jibble** in the left menu.

![The Jibble page shows the connection and the people who are not matched.](help:check-the-jibble-link/01-jibble.png)

# Connection

The **Connection** section shows whether ELEVATE is connected. You may see:

- **Not set up**
- **Token set**
- **Self-test is on**
- **Sending to Jibble is paused.** HR can pause it. Nothing is sent while it is paused.
- **Calls are reaching Jibble late.** Look at the recent calls.

If Jibble is down, the ELEVATE clock keeps working. Nothing is blocked.

# Recent calls to Jibble

The list shows each call, the person, the status and the note. Use **Sent again** (resend) for a failed call.

# People matching

ELEVATE matches people to Jibble by work email. If someone uses a different email in Jibble:

1. Find them in **People**. People with **Not matched** need a match.
2. In **Match by hand**, type the email they use in Jibble, or their Jibble member code.
3. Save. A message confirms it. You can remove a match later.

People with no match are skipped.

# Privacy

Jibble screenshots can show client or patient information. Never download them into ELEVATE or share them. The sending to Jibble is only turned on for a team once a signed monitoring policy exists.

# Common mistakes

- **Using Jibble hours to settle a dispute.** Use the ELEVATE clock. It is the only source of hours.
- **Matching the wrong person.** Check the email twice.

# Try it yourself

- Open the Jibble page and read the connection status.
- Find a person who is not matched.
