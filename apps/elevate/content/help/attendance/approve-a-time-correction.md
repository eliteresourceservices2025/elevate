Clock events are never edited. A mistake is fixed with a **correction**: a request with a reason that a lead approves. Then the right events are added.

# Open the corrections queue

1. Click **Attendance** in the left menu.
2. Open the **Corrections** tab.

A Team Lead sees corrections from their team. HR sees every pending correction. HR decides when nobody above the person can. Corrections that only HR can decide show the label **HR decides**.

# Decide a correction

1. Read the request: the reason, the proposed events and any screenshot.
2. Click **Approve** to accept it. A message says **Correction approved.**
3. Or click **Reject**, with a note. A message says **Correction rejected.**

Nobody can decide their own correction.

# File a correction for someone else

If a person's device is down or they cannot sign in, you can file it for them.

1. In the **Corrections** tab, find the form to file for another person.
2. Choose the person, the kind of correction and the times. Times are shown in the zone on the form.
3. Write a **Reason**.
4. Submit. A message says **Correction filed. The person was told and HR will decide.**

HR decides it, not you.

# Fix many people at once (HR)

HR can upload a CSV with `email,type,time` columns to file many corrections together. Another HR Admin approves the whole batch.

# What people see

People ask for corrections on the **Attendance** page: **Ask for a time correction**. A screenshot of the problem helps. They see their own in **My corrections**.

# Common mistakes

- **Approving without a reason that makes sense.** Ask the person when you are not sure.
- **Forgetting the time zone.** Check the zone shown on the form.

# Try it yourself

- Open the Corrections tab and read one pending request.
- File a test correction for a test person.
