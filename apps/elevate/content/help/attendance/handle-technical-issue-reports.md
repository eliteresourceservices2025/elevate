When someone cannot clock because of a technical problem, they send a report. It is only a report: it never adds, changes or removes a clock event or any hours.

# Where to find them

1. Click **Attendance** in the left menu.
2. Open the **Technical issues** tab.

You see **My reports**, the ones you sent yourself, and **All reports**, the ones from people you look after. A Team Lead sees their team. HR sees everyone. If nothing is waiting, it says **No technical issues are waiting.**

![Technical issues: your reports and all reports.](help:handle-technical-issue-reports/01-issues.png)

# Read a report

Each report shows the person, what went wrong, when it started and what happened:

- The ELEVATE clock did not work
- Jibble did not work (screenshots, clock in or out)
- Internet or power outage
- My computer or device failed
- Something else

It also has a screenshot of the problem. Opening it is recorded. Never share it outside ELEVATE, because it could show client information.

# Act on it

- If the problem is on our side, such as the clock or Jibble, tell the right person and fix it. See "Check the Jibble link".
- If hours are missing, the person should also ask for a time correction, or you can change their time. See "Approve a time correction".
- Reply to the person and tell them what you did.

# Mark it handled

Click **Mark as handled**. A message says **Marked as handled.** The screenshots are deleted 90 days after the report is handled.

# Common mistakes

- **Thinking a report fixes the hours.** It does not. A correction does.
- **Forwarding a screenshot with client information.** Do not.

# Try it yourself

- Open **Technical issues** and read the two lists.
