# Open it

Click **Org chart** in the left menu. The page shows how many people there are, and the company at the top.

![The org chart: the chart view and the list view.](help:read-the-org-chart/01-org-chart.png)

# Two views

- **Chart**: boxes for each person, joined by lines to their manager. Drag the chart to move around it.
- **List**: the same people as a plain list. This is easier on a small screen.

Each person shows their name, position and team, and how many people report to them.

# Open a profile

Click a person to open their profile if you have access. You may see only part of it, depending on your role.

# Who is in the chart

Everyone who works for the company is shown, with their manager and team. HR sets who reports to whom on each person's profile. If you think yours is wrong, tell HR. See "Ask for help".

# Common mistakes

- **Expecting the chart to show a second lead.** An attendance lead for time matters is not drawn as a manager.
- **Looking for people who left.** They are archived and no longer appear.

# Try it yourself

- Switch between **Chart** and **List**.
- Find your own box and your manager.
