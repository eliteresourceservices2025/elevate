ELEVATE is where you clock in, ask for time off, see your documents and keep your details up to date. This guide shows you where things are.

# The menu on the left

The menu groups the pages by topic. You only see the pages that belong to your role, so your menu may be shorter or longer than a coworker's.

Click a section name, such as **Core HR**, to open its pages. Opening another section closes the previous one. The section containing your current page opens automatically. On a phone, use **Open menu** first. On a laptop, **Hide menu labels** narrows the sidebar to icons; **Show menu labels** expands it again.

- **Overview** has your dashboard, a summary of your day.
- **Core HR** has your profile, your documents and company announcements.
- **Time and attendance** has the time clock, time off and schedules.
- **Learning** has this Help center and your assigned training.

![The left menu groups the pages by topic.](help:find-your-way-around/01-menu.png)

# The search box

Press **Ctrl+K** (on a Mac, **Cmd+K**) to search for a person or a page. Type a few letters and pick a result.

# Your own settings

Click your name or picture at the top right to open the account menu. The theme buttons in the header let you choose light, dark or your device setting. On a phone, these buttons are at the bottom of the menu.

# Getting help

1. Look for your question in this **Help center**. Use the search box at the top.
2. If you cannot find it, open **Contact support** in the menu and tell us what went wrong.

# Common mistakes

- **Looking for a page that is not in your menu.** Some pages are only for HR or team leads. If you think you should have access, ask HR.

# Try it yourself

- Open three pages from the left menu.
- Use Ctrl+K to find your own profile.
- Search the Help center for "time off".
