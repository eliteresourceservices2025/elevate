Most questions are answered in this Help center. If yours is not, or something is not working, send a request to support.

# Step 1: Search the Help center

Open **Help center** in the left menu. Type a few words in the search box, such as "time off", and click **Search**.

# Step 2: Open Contact support

Click **Contact support** in the left menu. Click **Send a request to support**.

![Open Contact support, then click Send a request to support.](help:ask-for-help/01-contact-support.png)

# Step 3: Fill in the form

- **What is it about?** Choose the closest topic, for example *Signing in or my account* or *The time clock or my hours*.
- **Short title.** A few words, such as "I cannot sign in".
- **Details.** Say what you did, what you expected and what happened. Write the page you were on if you can.

Send the request. HR is told, and you see your request in the list **Your requests**.

![The support form: choose a topic, add a short title and the details.](help:ask-for-help/02-support-form.png)

# Do not write private information

Never type passwords, government ID numbers or any client or patient information in a request.

# Common mistakes

- **A request with no details.** "It does not work" is hard to fix. Say which page and what you saw.

# Try it yourself

- Find a guide using the search box.
- Open **Contact support** and look at the topics in the list.
