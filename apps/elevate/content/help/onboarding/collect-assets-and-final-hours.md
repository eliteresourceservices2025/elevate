Two offboarding tasks need care: getting company equipment back and approving the last hours. ELEVATE tracks both but never works out pay.

# Hand equipment to someone

1. Click **Assets** in the left menu.
2. Find the item. Search by tag, name or serial number in **Search**.
3. Open it and use **Assign to a person**.
4. Choose the **Person** and write the **Condition when handed over**.

To add a new item, use **Register an item**. Give it an **Asset tag** (it is printed on the label and cannot be changed later), a **Name** and a **Category**. Serial number and purchase date are optional.

# Record a return

1. Open the item.
2. Click **Record the return**.
3. Write the **Condition when received** and choose **Then the item is** (for example available or in repair).

When nothing is still assigned to the person, the offboarding task **Equipment and assets returned** is ticked by ELEVATE.

# Print labels

Use **Labels** to print QR code labels for your items.

# Final hours

1. Open **Hours review** and approve the person's last week. See "Approve weekly hours".
2. Export the pay period. See "Export hours for payroll".
3. Tick the offboarding task **Final hours are approved and exported**. It links to Hours review.

# Private information

Assets keep items only. Do not store prices, passwords, or any client or patient information in the notes. Only HR sees the notes.

# Common mistakes

- **Giving equipment to someone without recording it.** The return task cannot finish correctly. Always assign in ELEVATE.
- **Forgetting the last week.** Approve it before you export.

# Try it yourself

- Register a test item, assign it to a test person and record the return.
- Open the offboarding checklist of a test person and find the equipment task.
