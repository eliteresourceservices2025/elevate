Offboarding is the checklist for someone who is leaving: handover, equipment, final hours and removing their access.

# Before you start

- A person who still has people reporting to them cannot be offboarded. Move their reports first. See "Set who reports to whom".

# Step 1: Start an offboarding

1. Click **Offboarding** in the left menu.
2. Find **Start an offboarding**. Tick one person or many.
3. Choose the **Last working day** and the **Main reason for leaving** (*Resignation*, *End of contract*, *Termination* or *Other*). Add a **Note (only HR sees it)** if you wish.
4. Start it. A message says **Offboarding started.**

Task due dates count from the last working day. The checklist comes from the template for their position, or the default one.

![The Offboarding page, where you start an offboarding.](help:offboard-someone/01-offboarding-page.png)

![Start an offboarding: tick the person, then set the last working day and the reason.](help:offboard-someone/02-start-dialog.png)

# Step 2: Follow the checklist

Open the case from the **Offboarding cases** list. The default tasks are:

- Write and hand over turnover notes (the person).
- Exit interview (the person, optional).
- Lead confirms the handover and the clearance.
- Equipment and assets returned. ELEVATE ticks this when nothing is still assigned to them. Record each return in **Assets**.
- Access removed at the end of the last working day. This is automatic.
- Final hours are approved and exported. See "Approve weekly hours" and "Export hours for payroll".
- Certificate of engagement, if they ask for one.

![An offboarding case: the checklist, and the HR actions such as Remove access now.](help:offboard-someone/03-case.png)

# The exit interview

The person fills in a short private form. It asks the main reason for leaving, *What went well*, *What could be better* and *Would you work with ERS again?* **Only HR can read the answers.** The page says so, and asks people not to include client or patient information.

# Removing access

By default, access is removed automatically when the last working day ends in the person's own time zone. On that day they are marked separated, lose their client assignments and schedule, and can no longer sign in.

To do it sooner, use **Remove access now** under **HR actions**. You confirm with **Yes, remove access now**.

# Cancel or finish

- **Cancel offboarding** works until access is removed.
- **Offboarding complete** closes the case when the tasks are done.

# Certificate of engagement

If the person asks for one, click **Issue certificate of engagement**. It is a PDF that shows dates and role only.

# Common mistakes

- **Forgetting to record equipment returns.** The task stays open until all items are marked returned in Assets.
- **Offboarding a manager first.** Move their reports first.

# Try it yourself

- Start an offboarding for a test person with a last working day next week.
- Open the case and find the exit interview task.
- Cancel the offboarding.
