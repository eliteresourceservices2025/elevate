Onboarding is a checklist of steps a new person must finish: sign agreements, read policies, upload documents and meet the team. ELEVATE ticks many steps by itself.

# When does onboarding start?

- **Someone you hired through Recruiting.** Onboarding starts by itself when you hire them. Nothing to do here.
- **Someone you added under People.** They have no checklist yet. Start one as shown below.

# Start onboarding for someone already added

1. Click **Onboarding** in the left menu.
2. Click **Start onboarding** at the top right. It is for people added under People who have no checklist.
3. Tick one person or many people. Use the search box to find them.
4. Set the **Start date**. Task due dates count from this day. If the people you ticked already have their own start date, the box **Use each person's own start date when they have one** is ticked. Untick it to use the date you typed for everyone.
5. Click the start button at the bottom of the box. A message says **Onboarding started.**

The checklist comes from the template for their position. If there is none, the default list is used. If everyone already has a checklist, the page says so.

![Start onboarding: tick the people and choose the start date.](help:start-onboarding/02-start-dialog.png)

# Follow the checklist

1. Open **Onboarding** and click the person's case.
2. Each task shows who owns it: **HR**, **Their lead** or **The person**. Some tasks say **Done automatically**: ELEVATE ticks them when it sees them done, for example when a policy is acknowledged or documents are uploaded.
3. For a task you own, click **Mark done**. Some ask you to type a short reason.
4. If a task is not needed, click **Skip** and say why.
5. Use **Reopen** to undo a done or skipped task.
6. Tasks marked **Late** are past their due date. **Optional** tasks do not block completion.

For the agreement task, click **Send agreement** to send it for signature.

![A person's onboarding checklist: who owns each task and what is done.](help:start-onboarding/03-case.png)

# Finish onboarding

When every required task is done, click **Complete onboarding**. The person changes from onboarding to active.

If a task was forgotten or done outside ELEVATE, HR can use **Complete anyway**. You type a reason, the open tasks are closed as skipped, and it is recorded.

# What a Team Lead can do

A Team Lead sees the cases of their team. They tick the tasks that belong to the lead, such as **Meet your lead and your team**.

# Common mistakes

- **Waiting for ELEVATE to tick a task.** Automatic ticks can take up to 30 minutes.
- **Starting onboarding twice.** Each person gets one checklist. Open their existing case instead.

# Try it yourself

- Open an onboarding case and find one task owned by HR.
- Mark a task done, then reopen it.
