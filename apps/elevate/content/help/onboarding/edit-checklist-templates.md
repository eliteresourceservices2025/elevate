A **template** is the list of steps used when you start onboarding or offboarding. You can keep the default list, or make a template for a position.

# How templates are chosen

- A template for a position is used for people in that position.
- Otherwise the **default** template is used.
- If there is no template at all, ELEVATE's built-in list is used.
- Changing a template affects **new cases only**. People already in onboarding keep their list.

# Open the templates

1. Click **Onboarding** in the left menu.
2. Click **Checklist templates**.

# Change a template

1. Open the template you want, or start a new one.
2. Give it a **Template name** and choose what it is **Used for**: *Everyone (the default)* or a position.
3. For each task set:
   - the title and **Details (optional)**,
   - the owner: **HR**, **Their lead** or **The person**,
   - the **Due day**, counted from the start date for onboarding or the last working day for offboarding,
   - whether it is required,
   - **Done when**: *Ticked by hand*, or one that ELEVATE ticks itself, such as *A policy is acknowledged* or *All required documents are uploaded*.
   - **Link inside ELEVATE (optional)** such as `/signing`, which sends people to the right page.
4. Click **Add a task** for more steps.
5. Click **Save template**.

# Tips

- Keep the list short. Fewer clear tasks get finished.
- Use automatic checks where you can. They save HR from ticking by hand.
- Never ask people to upload client or patient records.

# Common mistakes

- **Editing and expecting old cases to change.** They will not. Edit the case's tasks instead.
- **Making every task required.** Mark nice-to-haves as optional so they do not block completion.

# Try it yourself

- Open the default onboarding template and read its tasks.
- Add a task to a test template and save it.
