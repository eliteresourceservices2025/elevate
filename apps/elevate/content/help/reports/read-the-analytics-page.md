**Analytics** shows totals only. It never shows pay, review ratings or single people. It is read-only.

# Who sees what

- HR Admin and Super Admin see everything.
- A Team Lead sees their team.
- A Recruiter sees hiring only.
- An Executive reads on screen. Only HR can export.

# Open it

Click **Analytics** in the left menu.

![Analytics shows totals only, and hides groups under 5 people.](help:read-the-analytics-page/01-analytics.png)

# What the page shows

- **Headcount**, with **Headcount by team** and **Headcount by client**.
- **Joiners and leavers** and **Turnover** (the page also shows **Turnover last month**).
- **Prize days used**.
- **Attendance flags**, such as **Late days, latest week**.
- **Hiring**: **Hiring funnel** (applications that reached each stage), **Funnel by job** and **Time to hire**.

Use **Choose what to show** and the **Range** control to change what you see.

# Small groups are hidden

Any group with fewer than 5 people is hidden to protect privacy. You see a message such as **This group has fewer than 5 people, so its numbers are hidden.** Small groups are combined under **Other**, and small periods show as gaps. This is on purpose.

# Export

HR can use the export button to download a CSV of what the dashboard shows.

# Reading the numbers with care

- Numbers come from tables rebuilt regularly, so they may lag by a day.
- A change over a short time in a small team is only a hint. Look at the people behind it before you decide anything.

# Common mistakes

- **Trying to find one person's numbers here.** There are none by design. Use their profile.
- **Sharing a picture of the page with names of small groups.** The page shows totals only. Keep it that way.

# Try it yourself

- Open **Analytics** and read the headcount by team.
- Change the range on the hiring funnel.
