# Equipment (Assets)

**Assets** tracks items only: who has what. It does not keep prices or values.

1. Click **Assets** in the left menu.
2. HR sees **Inventory**. A Team Lead sees **Team assets**. People see **My assets**.
3. Search by tag, name or serial number, and filter by **Category** or **Status**.
4. HR can **Register an item**, assign it to a person and record the return. See "Collect equipment and finish final hours".

Every hand-over is recorded and cannot be edited. A Team Lead sees an item only if it is or was with someone on their team.

You can print QR labels. Scanning a label only opens the item's page.

# Certificates

**Certificates** are training certificates that expire, such as HIPAA awareness. ELEVATE reminds the person and HR 30 days and 7 days before, and on the end date.

1. Click **Certificates** in the left menu.
2. Use the tabs **My certificates**, **Team certificates** or **All certificates**, depending on your role.
3. Search with **Person or certificate**, and filter by **Status**.
4. HR can use **Add a certificate**. A certificate is a name and dates only: no file, number or score. A renewal is a new row.
5. HR can use **Load from TalentHR** to bring in older certificates.

# Who sees what

Certificates and assets show only what your role allows. Training results are completion only. Leads and clients never see scores.

# Common mistakes

- **Adding a certificate number or file.** Not needed. Name and dates only.
- **Editing an old certificate on renewal.** Add a new one instead.

# Try it yourself

- Search the inventory for a test item.
- Add a test certificate with an end date next month.
