Your clock history is never edited. A mistake is fixed with a **correction request**. Your lead approves it, and then the right events are added.

# Steps

1. Click **Attendance** in the left menu. On **My time**, click **Ask for a correction**.
2. Choose what happened:
   - **I forgot to clock in**
   - **I forgot to clock out**
   - **I forgot to end a break**
   - **Something else**
3. Fill in the time, for example **I stopped working at**. Times are shown in your time zone.
4. Write a **Reason**.
5. Add **Screenshots as proof**. A screenshot is required. It supports your request. It is never used as your hours.
6. Send it. A message says **Correction sent for approval.**

Never include client or patient information in a screenshot or a reason.

![Ask for a time correction: choose what happened, say why and add a screenshot.](help:ask-for-a-time-correction/01-ask-correction.png)

# What happens next

- Your lead decides. If nobody is above you, HR decides. A request that reaches back more than 7 days goes to HR.
- You stay clocked in until a clock-out request is decided, and your hours are not final until then.
- You see your requests under **My corrections**. You can cancel one that is still waiting.
- When it is approved, new events are added to your record. If you already clocked out late, the later clock-out is replaced.

# If it is your internet, your device or Jibble

Use **Report a technical issue** too. See "Report a clock or Jibble problem".

# Common mistakes

- **No screenshot.** The request cannot be sent without one.
- **Waiting a long time.** Requests older than a week need HR. Ask early.
- **Asking for a time you did not work.** Corrections are checked and recorded.

# Try it yourself

- Find **Ask for a correction** and read its choices.
- Look at **My corrections** to see your past requests.
