You work as an independent contractor, so there is no paid leave that builds up. Days off are **prize days off** that HR awards, for example as a game prize. You can also ask for other days off. Your request goes to your lead first, then to HR.

# See your prize days

1. Click **Time off** in the left menu.
2. Open the **My prize days** tab.

It shows your balance, when days expire and your history. If you have none, it says **No prize days yet.**

![Time off: your prize days and your requests.](help:request-time-off/01-prize-days.png)

# Request time off

1. On **Time off**, click **Request time off**.
2. Choose the **Leave type**.
3. Set the **First day** and the **Last day**. For one day, use the same date twice.
4. Tick **Half day (only for a single day)** if you need only half.
5. Add a **Note (optional)**.
6. Read the line under the form. It tells you how many working days it counts and warns you of problems.
7. Click **Send request**. A message says **Request sent.**

![Request time off: leave type, first and last day, and a note.](help:request-time-off/02-request-form.png)

# Rules to know

- A request cannot start in the past. If you missed it, ask HR to file it.
- Prize days need one working day's notice.
- Weekends and holidays are not counted.
- You cannot have two requests that overlap.

# Follow your request

Open the **Requests** tab. It shows **Waiting for lead**, then **Waiting for HR**, then **Approved** or **Declined**. When it is approved you get a calendar invite. You can cancel a request that is still waiting. For approved time off, ask your lead or HR.

# Common mistakes

- **Asking for a day with no prize days left.** Check **My prize days** first.
- **Asking too late.** Send it as early as you can.

# Try it yourself

- Open **My prize days** and read your history.
- Click **Request time off** and read the form, then close it.
