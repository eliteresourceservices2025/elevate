# Open your clock settings

1. Click **Attendance** in the left menu.
2. Open the **My time** tab.
3. Click **Clock settings** at the top right. The box that opens is called **Your clock settings**.

# Choose your time zone

Type or pick **Your time zone**. It decides which calendar day a clock-in belongs to. If you leave it empty, ELEVATE uses the company zone. A night shift counts as the day it started.

![Your clock settings: your time zone and the location option.](help:set-up-your-clock/01-clock-settings.png)

# Location

The option **Record my approximate location when I clock in** is yours to choose. It is saved only when you tick it, and it is rounded to about one kilometre.

- It works only after HR has published the monitoring policy. Until then the box is not available.
- You can turn it off again at any time.

Click save. A message says **Settings saved.**

# Rules set by your team

HR sets some clock rules for each team. They are not your choice, but you should know them:

- **Allowed IP addresses.** If you clock in from somewhere else, the clock is not blocked, but your lead is told.
- **Idle prompt.** After a while with no activity you see **Are you still working?** It never clocks you out.
- **Selfie.** If your team asks for one, you take a photo when you clock in. It is deleted after 30 days.
- **Late and early-leave grace.** Arriving or leaving within a few minutes of the shift is not flagged.

Selfies and location are monitoring, so they are only used after a monitoring policy is published and you have acknowledged it.

# Common mistakes

- **Choosing the wrong time zone.** Your days may land on the wrong date. Pick the zone where you work.
- **Expecting the location box to work.** It needs the published policy first.

# Try it yourself

- Click **Clock settings** and check your time zone.
- Read which rules apply to your team.
