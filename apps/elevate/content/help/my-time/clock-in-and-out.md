ELEVATE is the time clock and the only source of your hours. The clock is at the top right of every page.

# Before you start

- You need to be signed in, with internet. The clock works only online, on purpose. If you are offline, the buttons stop and the clock says it is paused.
- You need a people record. If the clock says **Set up your time clock**, you have no record yet. Ask HR to add you in People with your sign-in email.

# Clock in

1. Click **Clock in** in the header.
2. If your team asks for a selfie, allow the camera and take the photo. Selfies are deleted after 30 days.

A message says **Clocked in.** The clock now shows how long you have been working.

![The time clock is at the top right of every page.](help:clock-in-and-out/01-clock.png)

# Take a break

Breaks are unpaid. They are taken out of your worked time.

1. Click the break button.
2. Choose how long: **15**, **30** or **60** minutes, or a break with no set length.
3. When you are back, click to end the break. A message says **Break ended.**

If you stay on a timed break longer than you chose, a pop-up says **Your break is up**. Your lead is told, and the extra minutes show on your timesheet as **Over break**.

# Clock out

1. Click **Clock out**. A message says **Clocked out.**
2. A box called **Wrap up your day** opens. Write what you got done, or click **Skip**. Your lead and HR can read it. Never include client or patient information.

You can add or change the note for 24 hours after you clock out, from **Attendance**.

![My time, under Attendance, shows today and your week.](help:clock-in-and-out/02-my-time.png)

# If the clock asks "Are you still working?"

After a while with no activity, ELEVATE asks **Are you still working?** This does not clock you out. It only helps your lead see that you stepped away.

# If you come back after a gap

If your computer slept or lost the connection, you may see **Welcome back** when you return. Choose that you were working, or say when you stopped.

# Common mistakes

- **Forgetting to clock out.** Ask for a correction. See "Ask for a time correction".
- **Using the clock offline.** Nothing is recorded. Wait until you are back online and try again.
- **Writing client or patient details in your end-of-day note.** Never do this.

# Try it yourself

- Find the clock in the header.
- Look at **My time** under **Attendance** to see today's hours.
