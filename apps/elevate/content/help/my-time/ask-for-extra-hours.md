Work beyond your schedule needs your client's OK and your lead's approval before it counts. ELEVATE calls this **extra hours**. It labels the hours and never works out pay.

# Before you start

- You need to be assigned to a client. If you are not, the page says so. Ask HR.
- Ask the client first, and keep a screenshot of their approval. Never include client or patient information in it.

# Steps

1. Click **Extra hours** in the left menu.
2. Open **Ask to work extra hours**.
3. Choose the **Client**.
4. Set **From** and **Until**: the time you plan to work.
5. Type **Who at the client approved it?**
6. Write a **Reason**.
7. Add **Screenshot of the client's approval**. You can add one to three pictures, up to 5 MB each.
8. Click **Send for approval**. A message says **Sent to your lead for approval.**

![Ask to work extra hours: the client, the time, who approved it, and a screenshot.](help:ask-for-extra-hours/01-ask-extra-hours.png)

# Follow it

Your request shows under **My extra hours** as **Waiting for approval**. Your lead decides. If nobody is above you, HR decides.

# If the client asked you

Sometimes your lead or HR files extra hours that the client asked for. You see it under **Your client asked for extra hours**. Click **Confirm** to accept it, or **Decline** if you cannot. You are allowed to say no.

# Good to know

- A request is between 15 minutes and 24 hours.
- Approved extra hours show in your week on **My time** as **Extra**.
- Work beyond your schedule that nobody approved is shown as not approved.
- After your shift ends, ELEVATE may ask if you want to ask for extra hours, clock out or dismiss. It never clocks you out.

# Common mistakes

- **Working first and asking later.** If you ask more than 7 days late, only HR can decide.
- **No screenshot.** The request needs proof of the client's approval.

# Try it yourself

- Open **Extra hours** and read the form.
- Look at **My extra hours**.
