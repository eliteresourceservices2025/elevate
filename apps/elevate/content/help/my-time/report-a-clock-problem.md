If something stopped you from working or clocking, send a report. It tells your lead and HR what happened. It is only a report: it never changes your hours. To fix your hours, ask for a time correction too.

# Steps

1. Click **Attendance** in the left menu.
2. On **My time**, click **Report a technical issue**.
3. Choose **What went wrong?**:
   - **The ELEVATE clock did not work**
   - **Jibble did not work (screenshots, clock in or out)**
   - **Internet or power outage**
   - **My computer or device failed**
   - **Something else**
4. Say when it started and write **What happened?**
5. Add **Screenshot of the problem**. A screenshot is required (one to three pictures).
6. Send it.

Never include client or patient information in the text or the screenshot.

![Report a technical issue: what went wrong, and a screenshot.](help:report-a-clock-problem/01-report-issue.png)

# After you send it

Your lead and HR are told. You see your reports under **My technical issue reports**, and HR marks them handled. Screenshots are deleted 90 days after the report is handled.

# Common mistakes

- **Sending only a report.** If hours are missing, also ask for a correction. See "Ask for a time correction".
- **A screenshot with client information.** Hide or crop it first.

# Try it yourself

- Open **Report a technical issue** and read the choices.
