Your **schedule** is your shift pattern. The time clock uses it to flag late arrivals, early leaves, absences and extra hours.

# Open it

Click **Schedules** in the left menu. Under **My schedule** you see your current shift, and the next one if it is already set.

![Your schedule, in your client's time and in Manila time.](help:see-your-schedule/01-schedule.png)

# What it shows

- When your shift starts and ends, in your client's time zone.
- The same times in Manila time (**In Manila**).
- Your working days. The other days are rest days.
- Your planned unpaid break.

A shift belongs to the day it starts.

# If you have no schedule

The page says **You have no schedule yet, so late, early and extra hours are not tracked. HR sets schedules.** That means nothing is flagged, but HR should set one soon. Ask HR if you think yours is missing or wrong. Only HR can change schedules.

# Your week on the clock

Open **Attendance**, then **My time**, to see each day's shift next to the time you worked.

# Common mistakes

- **Mixing up the two times.** The first time shown is your client's zone. Manila time is shown separately.
- **Trying to change your own schedule.** Ask HR.

# Try it yourself

- Open **Schedules** and find your next shift.
- Compare the client's time with Manila time.
