The **talent pool** is a short list of good applicants you may want to contact for another job later. Only people who agreed to it can be in the pool.

# How people agree

The apply form has an optional box: **Keep my details for 12 months so the team can contact me about other roles that may suit me (optional).** If the person does not tick it, you cannot add them to the pool, and their details follow the normal clean-up periods for applicants.

# Add or remove someone

1. Open an applicant's page. See "Review applications and move them along".
2. Find **Talent pool**.
3. Click **Add to the talent pool**. A message says **Added to the pool.** The page shows **In the pool**.
4. To remove them, click **Take out of the pool**.

If the person did not agree, the panel says so: **They did not tick “keep my details for future roles” when they applied, so they cannot be added to the pool.** If their agreement has run out, it says that instead.

![The talent pool panel on an applicant's page.](help:talent-pool/01-pool-panel.png)

# Consider someone for another job

For a person in the pool:

1. Under **Also consider for another open job**, choose a job.
2. Click **Add to that job**. A message says **Added to that job.**

A new application starts for that job, with the note **Added from the talent pool**.

# See and change many at once

- On the **Applicants** page, open **More filters** and tick **Only the talent pool**. See "Find applicants across all your jobs".
- To add or remove many people, use **Add to the talent pool** or **Take out of the talent pool** in the bulk bar. See "Act on many applicants at once". People who did not agree are skipped, and you see why.

# How long they are kept

A person stays in the pool until the date they agreed to, which is 12 months after they applied. The clean-up job does not remove pool members while their agreement lasts. After the date, they leave the pool by themselves and the normal periods apply again. Hired people are never removed by the clean-up.

# If someone asks to be removed

Take them out of the pool at once. They can also ask under the privacy notice to have their details removed.

# Common mistakes

- **Adding someone who did not agree.** ELEVATE does not let you. Ask them to apply again and tick the box if they want to.
- **Contacting people after their date has passed.** They have left the pool. Ask for their agreement again.
- **Writing client or patient information on the record.** Never.

# Try it yourself

- Open an applicant who agreed and add them to the pool.
- Use the **Only the talent pool** filter to find them.
- Take them out again.
