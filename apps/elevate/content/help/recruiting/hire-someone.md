Hiring turns an applicant into a person in ELEVATE. Only HR Admin and Super Admin can do it.

# Before you start

- The applicant has a **Signed** offer. If not, ELEVATE asks for a written reason, which is recorded.
- Check the work email on the offer. It must not already belong to another person.

# Steps

1. Open the applicant's page and find **Offer and hiring**.
2. Open **Hire**.
3. If there is no signed offer, fill in **Why hire without a signed offer? (recorded)**.
4. Click **Hire**. A box asks **Hire this person?** and says this creates their people record and invites them to ELEVATE. Confirm.

# What happens

All at once, or not at all:

1. A person record is created as an independent contractor, with the status onboarding.
2. The application is marked **Hired**.
3. An onboarding checklist is started. See "Start onboarding for a new hire".
4. An invitation is sent if they have no account.

A message says **Hired.** and tells you if the invitation was emailed. If the email could not be sent, tell the person to use **Accept an invite** on the sign-in page with the exact email address. See "Invite a new person to ELEVATE".

# After hiring

- Find them under **People**.
- Open **Onboarding** to follow their checklist.
- Hired people are never removed by the applicant data clean-up.

# Common mistakes

- **Hiring with a work email that already exists.** Nothing is created. Fix the email and try again.
- **Hiring before the offer is signed.** You can, but you must write the reason. It is recorded.

# Try it yourself

- Hire a test applicant who has a signed test offer, then find them in **People**.
