The **Applicants** page lists everyone who applied, for every job, in one place. Use it when you want to find a person without opening each job's pipeline.

A Team Lead sees only the people who applied to the jobs they are on the hiring team for. HR, Super Admin and recruiters see everyone.

# Open the list

Click **Applicants** in the left menu, under **Talent**. Each row shows the person's name and email, the job, the stage, their source and tags, and when they applied. Click a name to open their full record. See "Review applications and move them along".

![The Applicants page. Filters are on top, and each row is one application.](help:find-applicants/01-list.png)

# Search and filter

At the top you can use:

- **Name or email.** Type part of a name or an email address. Type it as plain text. Nothing special is needed.
- **Job.** Choose one job, or **All jobs**.
- **Stage.** Choose **Any stage**, **Still in the pipeline**, or one stage such as **Interview**.

Click **Apply filters**. Click **Reset** to clear them all.

# More filters

Click **More filters** to open the rest:

- **How it closed:** **Rejected**, **Not qualified** or **Withdrew**.
- **Source:** where they heard about the job, or **Not said**.
- **Tag:** one of your tags. See "Tag applicants and see where they come from".
- **Keyword match:** show only people at 25%, 50% or 75% or more. See "Use the keyword match on resumes".
- **Only the talent pool.** See "Keep good applicants in the talent pool".
- **Applied from** and **Applied to:** a range of dates.
- **Sort by:** newest applications, recently changed, name (A to Z), or keyword match with the highest first.

The filters stay in the address of the page, so you can bookmark a search you use often.

![More filters: how it closed, source, tag, keyword match, the talent pool, dates and sorting.](help:find-applicants/02-more-filters.png)

# Read the badges

In the **Source and tags** column you may see:

- **Talent pool**, if they were added to the pool.
- **Match 85%**, the keyword match for the job.
- Where they heard about the job, and any tags.

A person who was removed from the system shows no name or email, and a search never finds them.

# What else is here

- **Possible duplicates** (HR and Super Admin) opens the list of records that may be the same person. See "Merge duplicate applicants".
- **Jobs and boards** goes back to the Recruiting page.
- On a small screen the table becomes one card for each person.

# Common mistakes

- **Searching for a long phrase.** Type part of the name or the email only.
- **Forgetting a filter is on.** If the list looks short, click **Reset**.
- **Looking for someone who was hired.** They are in the list with the stage **Hired**, and their person record is under **People**.

# Try it yourself

- Search for a name, then click **Reset**.
- Open **More filters** and choose a **Source**.
- Sort by **Keyword match, highest first**.
