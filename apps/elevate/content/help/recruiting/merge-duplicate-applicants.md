Sometimes the same person applies twice with different email addresses. ELEVATE suggests records that may be the same person: the same phone number, or the same name with a different email. You decide whether to join them. Only HR Admin and Super Admin can merge.

# Open the list

1. Click **Applicants** in the left menu.
2. Click **Possible duplicates** at the top.

If nothing matches, the page says **No possible duplicates.**

![Possible duplicates. Each group is records that look alike.](help:merge-duplicate-applicants/01-duplicates.png)

# Decide

Each group shows the records with their email, phone and the jobs they applied for.

- **Not the same person?** Leave it. Nothing is merged for you.
- **The same person?** Select the record you want to **keep**, usually the one with the most complete details. Then click **Keep the selected record and merge the rest into it**.

# What happens

- The applications of the other record move to the record you kept.
- Blank details on the kept record are filled from the other one. A longer talent pool agreement is kept.
- The self-identification answers of the other record are deleted.
- The other record stays as a **Merged duplicate** row so the history is not lost.

Merging cannot be undone, so check the names, emails and jobs before you click.

# When it is refused

ELEVATE will not merge two records that applied for the same job, or a record that was removed. In that case, close one application with a reason instead. See "Review applications and move them along".

# Common mistakes

- **Merging two different people with the same name.** Check the phone and email first.
- **Keeping the record with less information.** Choose the fuller one.

# Try it yourself

- Open **Possible duplicates** and read a group, without merging.
