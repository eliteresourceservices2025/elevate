When you have many applications to sort, do not open each one. Tick them on the **Applicants** page and do one action for all of them. You can act on up to 100 at a time.

# Before you start

- Open **Applicants** in the left menu. Use the filters first, so the list shows only the people you mean. See "Find applicants across all your jobs".
- Only HR, Super Admin and recruiters see the tick boxes. A Team Lead does not.

# Steps

1. Tick the box in front of each person. Click **Select or clear this page** to tick everyone on the page, or to clear them. The bar says how many are selected.
2. Under **Do this**, choose an action (see the list below).
3. Fill in what the action asks for.
4. Click **Apply to** and the number. A box asks you to confirm. Each change is recorded in the person's history.

A message says how many were done, for example **8 of 8 done.**

![Tick the people, choose what to do, then click Apply.](help:act-on-many-applicants/01-bulk.png)

# The actions

- **Move to a stage.** Choose the stage. You cannot move people to **Hired** this way. Hiring is always done one person at a time, from an offer.
- **Close applications.** Choose **How it closes**: **Rejected**, **Not qualified** or **Withdrew**. Write a **Reason (internal only)** of at least three letters. You can tick **Send each a short, polite email**. The email never carries your reason.
- **Add a tag** and **Remove a tag.** Choose the tag.
- **Add to the talent pool** and **Take out of the talent pool.** Only people who agreed to be kept can join the pool. See "Keep good applicants in the talent pool".

# If some fail

Each person is changed on their own. If a few cannot be changed, you see a note such as **2 could not change; the other 6 did**, with the reason, for example that the person already has that stage. Fix those and run the action again for them.

# Common mistakes

- **Closing with a vague reason.** Write why, in plain words. Only your team reads it.
- **Ticking the wrong page.** The ticks are for the page you see. Filter first, then tick.
- **Trying to hire in bulk.** It is not allowed on purpose. Hire from the offer. See "Hire someone and hand off to onboarding".

# Try it yourself

- Tick two test applicants and add a tag to both.
- Move them to **Screening**.
- Close one as **Not qualified**, then reopen it. See "Review applications and move them along".
