A job opening is what applicants apply to. Open jobs show on the public careers page. Anyone can apply without an account.

# Step 1: Create the job

1. Click **Recruiting** in the left menu.
2. Click **New job**. It starts as a draft, so nobody sees it yet.
3. Fill in:
   - **Job title**
   - **Description (shown on the public careers page)**
   - **Location** (the default is Remote)
   - **Pay note (optional, shown publicly)**. This is free text. Do not promise pay or benefits you have not agreed.
   - **Team (optional)**
   - **Web address.** ELEVATE makes one from the title. You can change it only while the job is a draft, because the link may be shared.
4. Choose the emails applicants get: a "we received your application" email, and an email when they are not selected.
5. Under **Hiring team**, add Team Leads who should see this job's applicants and fill in scorecards. HR and recruiters always see everything.
6. Click **Create job (draft)**. You land on the job's page. Later changes use **Edit job** and **Save job**, and a message says **Job saved.**

![The New job form. A job starts as a draft.](help:post-a-job/02-new-job.png)

# Step 2: Publish it

When the text is ready, click **Publish job**. A message says **The job is open and listed on the careers page.**

![A job's pipeline: one column for each stage.](help:post-a-job/04-job-pipeline.png)

# Close, reopen or remove

- **Close** the job when it is filled. A message says **Job closed.** It leaves the careers page.
- **Reopen job** brings it back.
- A job that is not needed can be deleted or removed from the lists. Applicants stay in the system.

# Edit the careers page

The careers page itself has its own title, introduction and banner picture.

1. On **Recruiting**, open the **Careers page** link.
2. Change the **Page title** and **Introduction**.
3. To add a banner, click **Add a banner** (or **Change banner**) and choose a picture. Drag it to move it and use **Zoom**. Click **Save banner**.
4. Save the page. A message says **Careers page saved.**

Use pictures of the team or the work. Never use a client or a patient.

![Edit the careers page title, introduction and banner.](help:post-a-job/03-careers-page.png)

# Check how it looks

Use **View careers page** or **View job ad** on the Recruiting page to see it as an applicant does.

# Common mistakes

- **Publishing with a typo in the web address.** You can only change it while the job is a draft.
- **Writing "employee" or promising benefits.** Our workers are independent contractors. Keep the wording as "contractor".
- **Putting client or patient details in the description.**

# Try it yourself

- Create a draft job and save it.
- Publish it, open the careers page, then close the job.
