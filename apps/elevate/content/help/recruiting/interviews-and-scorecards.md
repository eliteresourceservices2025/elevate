# Schedule an interview

Only HR, Super Admin and recruiters can schedule.

1. Open the applicant's page from the job pipeline.
2. Find the interview section and click **Schedule interview**.
3. Choose the **Type**: *Screening call*, *Interview*, *Assessment review* or *Final interview*.
4. Set the **Date and time**. It uses your computer's time zone.
5. Set the **Length** and the **Meeting link or place**.
6. Pick the **Interviewers**. They can be HR, recruiters or Team Leads.
7. Add a **Note for the interviewers and the applicant (optional)**. Leave the box ticked if the applicant should get the invitation.

The interviewers get a calendar invite by email and a notification in ELEVATE. If you connected your Google Calendar, ELEVATE also creates the event on your calendar.

To cancel, cancel the interview. A message says **Interview cancelled.** A cancellation is sent.

# Fill in a scorecard

After the interview starts, each interviewer fills in their own scorecard. You cannot do it before.

1. Open the applicant's page and find **Your scorecard**.
2. Rate each item from 1 (weak) to 5 (excellent):
   - **Communication**
   - **Role skills**
   - **Reliability and work habits**
   - **Team fit**
3. Choose **Your recommendation**: *Strong yes*, *Yes*, *No* or *Strong no*.
4. Write a short reason and submit. A message says **Scorecard submitted.**

A scorecard cannot be edited after you submit it, so check it first.

# Who sees the scorecards

An interviewer sees other interviewers' scorecards only after submitting their own. HR and recruiters who are not interviewers on that interview see all of them.

# Common mistakes

- **Rating before the interview.** The form opens only once the interview has started.
- **Writing client or patient information in the reason.**
- **A wrong time zone.** The time is your computer's. Check it before saving.

# Try it yourself

- Schedule a test interview for tomorrow.
- Open the applicant and find where the scorecard will appear.
