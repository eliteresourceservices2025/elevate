ELEVATE sends a few short, polite emails to applicants. They have built-in wording. HR can write its own. Only HR Admin and Super Admin can change them.

# Open the page

1. Click **Recruiting** in the left menu.
2. Click **More**, then **Applicant emails**.

![Applicant emails: the built-in wording, with a preview.](help:reword-applicant-emails/01-emails.png)

# The emails

- **We received your application.** Sent right after someone applies, when the job has it switched on.
- **Not moving forward.** Sent when a recruiter closes an application as **We are not moving forward** and ticks the email box.
- **Not qualified for the role.** Sent when an application is closed as **Not qualified for the role** and the email box is ticked.

Interview invitations are written for each interview and cannot be changed here.

# Change one

1. Change the **Subject** and the **Message**. Write plain text, and leave a blank line between paragraphs.
2. You can use **{{first_name}}** and **{{job_title}}**. ELEVATE fills them in for each person.
3. Check the **Preview**, which shows a sample applicant.
4. Click **Save**. The badge changes from **Built-in wording** to **Your wording**.

Other words in double braces are not allowed, and ELEVATE tells you what to fix before it saves.

# Go back to the built-in wording

Click **Use the built-in wording** and confirm. Your text is removed. A message says **Back to the built-in wording.**

# Rules for the wording

- Be kind and short. Say nothing about scores, notes or the reason for the decision. The email goes to the applicant.
- Do not promise a job, pay or benefits. We work with independent contractors.
- Never write client or patient information.

# Common mistakes

- **Putting the reason for rejection in the email.** Keep reasons internal.
- **Forgetting the preview.** Always read it before you save.

# Try it yourself

- Open **Applicant emails** and read the three messages.
- Change one greeting, check the preview, then use the built-in wording again.
