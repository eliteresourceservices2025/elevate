Staff use **Privacy incidents** to report a privacy or security mistake or worry. For example, sending something to the wrong person, losing a device or clicking a suspicious link. Nobody is punished for reporting.

Only the person who reported it, HR, Super Admin and a **Privacy Officer** can see a report.

# Report an incident (everyone)

1. Click **Privacy incidents** in the left menu.
2. Click **Report an incident**.
3. Answer: **What happened?**, **Day it happened (or the day you noticed)**, **How did you find out?**, **Which client is involved?**, **Who else knows?** and **What have you already done about it?**
4. Say whether it may still be happening.
5. Click **Send the report**.

The form forbids patient names or details. Reports and notices never carry the text of the report.

# Handle a report (HR, Super Admin, Privacy Officer)

1. Open **Privacy incidents**. The **Inbox** lists reports by status, such as **In review** and **Ongoing**.
2. Open a report and read it.
3. Click **Mark as in review**. A message says **Marked as in review.**
4. Use **Messages** to ask the reporter a question or answer. Click **Send message**.
5. When it is resolved, write **How was it resolved? (only the privacy team reads this note)** and close it.

Ongoing issues are looked at first. A closed report can be reopened.

You cannot handle your own report. Another person on the privacy team must.

# Add a Privacy Officer

HR can name someone else as a **Privacy Officer** with **Add a Privacy Officer**, using their sign-in email. They then see and handle reports too.

# Common mistakes

- **Waiting to be sure.** Reports are welcome even when unsure.
- **Copying details of a report into chat or email.** Keep it in the report.

# Try it yourself

- Send a test report from a test account and handle it as HR.
