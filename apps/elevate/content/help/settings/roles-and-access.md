A **role** decides what a person can see and do. Everyone has the **Employee** role. Other roles are added on top.

# The six roles

- **Super Admin** has full access and manages roles and sign-in security.
- **HR Admin** manages people, onboarding, documents, time and invitations.
- **Team Lead** sees and approves for their team: hours, corrections, time off and onboarding tasks.
- **Recruiter** works on jobs, applicants, interviews and offers.
- **Executive** sees summaries and decides time off for people who report to them.
- **Employee** is the base role for everyone: clock, time off, profile and documents.

"Team" means everyone below you in the reporting chain. A Team Lead sees a limited view of a person's profile.

# Give someone a role

Only a **Super Admin** can give roles.

- **With an invitation:** in **Settings > Invitations**, tick the roles under **Give them, on top of Employee**.
- **For someone already in:** click **Settings**, open **Roles and access**, choose the person's roles and click **Save roles**.

Changing a role signs the person out, so the change applies right away. Every change is written to the audit log.

![Roles and access: choose a person's roles and save.](help:roles-and-access/01-roles.png)

# Reset someone's authenticator

If a person got a new phone, a Super Admin opens **Roles and access** and clicks **Reset authenticator** on their row, then confirms **Reset**. They set up the authenticator again at their next sign-in.

# Deactivate an account

Use **Deactivate** on the row. This stops them signing in. A person who still has an active people record is refused: use **Offboarding** instead.

# Look at what happened

The **Audit log** records sign-ins, role changes, approvals, exports and views of private fields. Only a Super Admin reads it.

# Common mistakes

- **Giving a role "just in case".** Give only what the person's job needs.
- **Deactivating instead of offboarding.** Offboarding also handles equipment and final hours.

# Try it yourself

- Open **Roles and access** and read your own roles.
- Find where an invitation lets you tick roles.
