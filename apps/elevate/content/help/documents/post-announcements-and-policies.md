Click **Announcements** in the left menu. It has the tabs **Announcements**, **Policies** and **Pop-up queue**.

# Post an announcement

1. On the **Announcements** tab, start a new one. The page is **New announcement**. Posting notifies everyone it is addressed to.
2. Write a **Title** and a **Message**.
3. Under **Who is it for?** choose **Everyone** or **Selected teams**, then pick the **Teams**.
4. Under **Options**, you can **Pin to the top** and **Require acknowledgment**. Add a **Due date (optional)**.
5. You can **Attach a company document (optional)**.
6. Click **Post announcement**. A message says **Announcement posted.**

Do not include client or patient information.

Once people have acknowledged it, the title and text are locked. You can still change the due date, pinning and attachment.

# Add a policy

A policy has versions. Each version is frozen once published, and people acknowledge a version.

1. On the **Policies** tab, click **Add a policy**.
2. Write the **Policy title** and the **Text**. Add a **Due date (optional)**.
3. Click **Create draft**. A message says **Policy created as a draft.** Nobody sees it until you publish it.
4. Open the draft and use **Save draft** while you work. You can discard a draft.
5. For a new version of a published policy, fill in **What changed (shown to readers)**. Readers see this.
6. Publish it when ready.

The **Privacy notice** and the **Monitoring policy** are special policies. Publishing the privacy notice is permanent and every person must acknowledge it. Publish a monitoring policy before turning on location, selfies or screenshots.

# Who acknowledged?

Open an announcement or policy to see who acknowledged and who did not. HR sees everyone. A Team Lead sees their team. HR can send a reminder.

# Pop-up queue

HR can set an announcement to appear as a pop-up. Use the **Pop-up queue** tab to see and order them.

# Common mistakes

- **Publishing a draft too early.** A published version cannot be changed. Add a new version instead.
- **Writing names or reasons about a person in an announcement.** Keep it general.

# Try it yourself

- Create an announcement for one test team.
- Create a policy as a draft and discard it.
