**ELEVATE Sign** lets people sign a document online. When everyone has signed, ELEVATE seals the document and adds a certificate with the details. The word we use for the person who signs is the **signee**.

# Open Signing

Click **Signing** in the left menu. HR sees the tabs **To sign**, **All documents** and **Templates**.

![The Signing page: documents to sign, all documents and templates.](help:send-a-document-to-sign/01-signing.png)

# Send a document

1. Click to send a document. The page is **Send a document**.
2. Under **The document**, choose how it starts: upload a **PDF (up to 8 MB, 100 pages)**, pick a template, write or paste it in ELEVATE, or start from a **ready-made document (optional)**.
3. Give it a **Document title**.
4. Under **Who signs**, add people with **Add a signee**. They need an ELEVATE account. You can add a whole **group**: everyone, a team, or the people assigned to a client.
5. Choose the **Signing order**: **Everyone at the same time**, or **One after another, in the order above**.
6. You can add **Countersigned by the Company (optional, signs after each person)**.
7. Set **Days to sign before it expires**.
8. Optional: tick that each person initials every page.
9. Click **Send for signature**. A message says **Sent for signature.**

For a group of more than 10 people, each person signs their own copy, so there is no signing order.

Do not include client or patient information. Signatures are added on a page at the end.

![Send a document: choose the file, who signs and the order.](help:send-a-document-to-sign/02-send-a-document.png)

# If you are practicing

Tick **Mark as test**. The document is marked **Test** everywhere, can be archived and a Super Admin can delete it. Use it when you are trying the flow out.

# Follow it

Open the document to see who has signed. You can send a reminder (**Reminder sent.**). Reminders also go out every 3 days. A document expires after the days you chose. A signee who declines ends it, and HR is told. HR can void it before everyone signs. Write **Why is it being voided?**

# Outside people

People who are not on ELEVATE, such as an applicant with an offer, sign by an emailed link and a 6-digit code. This is used for offer letters. See "Send an offer and track signing".

# After signing

The sealed copy and certificate are stored. Open them from the document. A copy with personal details, such as bank details typed while signing, can only be opened by that contractor, HR and Super Admin.

To check that a file is genuine, anyone can use the public **verify** page. It compares the file's fingerprint and shows match or no match only.

# Common mistakes

- **Adding someone with no ELEVATE account.** They cannot sign. Invite them first.
- **Sending a real document while testing.** Tick **Mark as test**.

# Try it yourself

- Send a small test PDF to yourself, marked as a test.
- Sign it, then open the sealed copy.
