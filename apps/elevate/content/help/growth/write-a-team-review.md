When HR launches a review cycle, each person's lead writes a review. The lead is whoever the person reports to.

# Find your reviews

Click **Reviews** in the left menu. Reviews you need to write are under **Yours to write**. You also see **Team reviews**, with where each one stands.

![Reviews, as a lead: the reviews you write and your team's reviews.](help:write-a-team-review/01-reviews-lead.png)

# Write the review

1. Open the person's review.
2. For each section, give a **Rating 1 to 5** and write your feedback.
3. Add your **Overall** rating.
4. Submit. **Once it is submitted it cannot be edited**, so read it first. Do not include client or patient information.

You do not have to wait for the person's self review. You can see their self review only after you submit yours.

# What happens next

HR calibrates the final rating and shares the result. The person then sees your written feedback and the final rating. If HR changes your overall rating, the person sees the final rating but not your original rating. HR keeps the reason private.

# When a review is not needed

If the person is on leave or an early review is already covered, you can waive it for your own team. Click to waive and write **Why is this review not needed? (required)**. A message says **Review waived.**

# Goals

Open **Reviews**, then **Goals**. You can add notes on your team's goals and see their progress. A goal is archived, never deleted. There is no weighting or score.

# Common mistakes

- **Writing about private matters.** Keep it to the work.
- **Missing the due date.** You get one reminder a day once it is due.

# Try it yourself

- Open **Reviews** and find **Yours to write**.
