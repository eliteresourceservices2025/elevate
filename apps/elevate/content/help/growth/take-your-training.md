Your assigned training, such as HIPAA and Cybersecurity basics, is in **ELEVATE Learn**. You open it from ELEVATE and you stay signed in.

# Open it

1. Click **My training** in the left menu.
2. Click **Open HIPAA training** (or the button for the course you were assigned).

It opens ELEVATE Learn in a new page with the same account. Your progress is saved as you go.

![My training: open your assigned course.](help:take-your-training/01-my-training.png)

# Never type patient information

The page says it: please never type patient information anywhere in training. If you suspect a privacy or security incident, tell HR straight away. See "Report a privacy concern".

# How it works

- A course has modules. Each module has a minimum time, so you read, not rush.
- After the modules, there is an exam. You need 80% to pass, and you have three tries.
- When you pass, you get a certificate you can download. ELEVATE tracks when it expires and reminds you.
- Training never stops you from working. If you are overdue, you get a warning only.

# Reminders

You get an email 30 days and 7 days before the due date, on the due date, and weekly while overdue. The email has only the due date and a link. You can turn these emails off under **Settings**, then **Email notifications**.

# Who sees your result

HR sees whether you passed, how many tries you used and your latest score. Your lead and your clients see only that you are done, never your score.

# Common mistakes

- **Leaving the exam open for a long time.** Finish it in one sitting.
- **Missing the due date.** You are not blocked, but HR sees it.

# Try it yourself

- Open **My training** and look at what is assigned to you.
