# Open Training status

Click **Training status** in the left menu (under **Learning**). It shows HIPAA training for each person: passed, not yet, or overdue. HR also sees tries and the latest score. A Team Lead sees their team and sees completion only, never scores.

![Training status: who passed, who has not, and who is overdue.](help:check-training-status/01-training-status.png)

# The two courses

Use the tabs **HIPAA** and **Cybersecurity basics**. If a course is not published yet, the page says so, and nobody is assigned.

# Read the numbers

At the top: **Everyone**, **Overdue**, **Not yet**, **Passed** and **Not assigned**. Below is a list with each person's team, track, status, due date and certificate.

Use **Search** and **Status** (any, overdue, not yet, passed, not assigned) with **Filter** to find people.

# Who gets the course

Under **Automatic assignment**, HR chooses **Who is assigned automatically**:

- **Everyone**: each night, everyone active who needs the course is assigned. That includes new hires still in onboarding.
- **Chosen teams only**: only the ticked teams. Anyone else can still be assigned by hand.
- **Paused**: nobody is assigned automatically. People already assigned keep their reminders.

Click **Save**.

# Proof for a client

The **Client evidence report** shows that the people assigned to a client held a current training certificate on a date.

1. Choose the **Client** and **As of** date.
2. Click **Download CSV** or **Download PDF**.

It has names, certificate references and dates only: no scores or answers.

# Good to know

- Training never blocks work. Overdue warns only.
- Assigning someone to a client that handles patient information shows their training status as a reminder.

# Common mistakes

- **Sharing a score with a lead or a client.** Only HR sees scores.
- **Pausing the automatic assignment and forgetting it.** New people will not be assigned.

# Try it yourself

- Open **Training status** and filter by **Overdue**.
