Reviews record ratings and feedback only. ELEVATE never links them to pay, and no review text goes in an email or a notice.

# The steps of a review

1. The person writes a **self review** (optional, reminded).
2. Their lead writes a **lead review**. It does not wait for the self review.
3. HR looks at both and **calibrates** the final rating.
4. HR **shares** the result with the person.
5. The person reads it and **acknowledges** it.

# Templates

Click **Reviews** in the left menu, then **Templates**. A template holds the questions. Use the built-in short template, or click **Add a template** and give it a **Template name**, sections and questions. Ratings run from 1 to 5, from needs improvement to outstanding.

A running cycle keeps its own copy of the questions, so editing a template later never changes it.

![Reviews: Templates, Launch a cycle and the list of cycles.](help:run-a-review-cycle/01-reviews-hr.png)

# Launch a cycle

1. On **Reviews**, click **Launch a cycle**.
2. Fill in the **Cycle name**, the **Type** (for example quarterly or annual) and the **Template**.
3. Under **Who is reviewed**, choose **Everyone**, **Some teams** or **Chosen people**.
4. Set the due dates: **Self reviews due**, **Lead reviews due** and **Calibration due**.
5. Click **Launch cycle**.

Each person's lead is whoever they report to today. To give a review to a different lead, use **Give the lead's review to someone else** before it is written.

![Launch a review cycle: name, type, template, who is reviewed and due dates.](help:run-a-review-cycle/02-launch.png)

# Early-engagement reviews

New people get a review at month 3 and month 5 by themselves. They appear on **Reviews** under **Early-engagement reviews**. They open automatically, and they are never filled in for people who started long ago.

# Follow the cycle

Open **Cycles** and then a cycle to see **Where it stands**: counts of self reviews due, lead reviews due and calibration due, and **Next step due** for each review. Reminders go to whoever has the next step. Averages are hidden when there are fewer than five reviews.

# Calibrate and share

1. Open a review that is ready for **HR calibration**.
2. Read both reviews. Set the **Final rating**, or choose **Use the average of my ratings**.
3. If you change the lead's rating, write the **Reason for changing the lead's rating**. The reason is only for HR and the person's leads.
4. Write the **Summary the person will read**. Click save. A message says **Calibration saved.**
5. Click share. A message says **Shared with the person.**

The person then sees the lead's feedback and the final rating. If you changed the rating, they see the final rating and the lead's written feedback, but not the lead's original rating.

# A review that is not needed

If someone is on leave or an early review is already covered, waive it. Click to waive and write **Why is this review not needed? (required)**. A message says **Review waived.** You can bring it back later. A waived review drops out of reminders and overdue counts.

# Close the cycle

When everything is done, close the cycle. A message says **Cycle closed.**

# Common mistakes

- **Sharing before calibrating.** The person sees the lead's final rating when you share.
- **Writing a reason that names the person's private details.** Keep it about the work.

# Try it yourself

- Open **Templates** and read the built-in short template.
- Open **Launch a cycle** and read the form.
