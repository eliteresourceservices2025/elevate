Reviews record ratings and feedback only. ELEVATE never links them to pay.

# Open Reviews

Click **Reviews** in the left menu. You see **Your reviews** and **Goals**. If there is nothing to do yet, the page says **Nothing here yet.**

![Reviews: your reviews and your goals.](help:your-reviews-and-goals/01-reviews.png)

# When you get a review

- Early on, you get early-engagement reviews at month 3 and month 5 after your start date.
- HR can also start a quarterly or annual review for everyone, a team or chosen people.
- A review you need to write is listed as **Yours to write**.

# Write your self review

1. Open the review.
2. For each section, give a **Rating 1 to 5** and write your comments. The scale runs from needs improvement to outstanding.
3. Submit. **Once it is submitted it cannot be edited**, so read it first. Please do not include client or patient information.

Your self review is optional. If you do not need to write one, the page says so.

# Read your result

- Your lead writes their own review. They can do it before yours is in.
- HR looks at both and may change the final rating. It can then share the review with you.
- **You see your lead's feedback and the final rating only after HR shares it.** Until then you see just your own self review.
- If HR changed the rating, you see the final rating and your lead's written feedback.

# Acknowledge it

Open the shared review and click **Acknowledge**. It means you read it. It does not mean you agree, and you can add a **Comment (optional)**.

# Goals

1. Open **Reviews**, then **Goals**.
2. Click **Add a goal**. Give it a title, **Details (optional)** and a **Target date (optional)**.
3. Update it with a **Progress note** as you go, and change its status.

Your lead and HR can add notes on your goals. A goal is archived, never deleted. There is no weighting and no score.

# Common mistakes

- **Submitting too fast.** You cannot edit it afterwards.
- **Putting client information in a comment.** Never do this.

# Try it yourself

- Open **Reviews** and look at the two sections.
- Add a goal for next month.
