# Your certificates

Click **Certificates** in the left menu. Under **My certificates** you see the training certificates recorded for you, for example HIPAA awareness, with the dates they start and end. If none are recorded, the page says **No certificates are recorded for you.**

- ELEVATE reminds you and HR 30 days and 7 days before a certificate ends, and on the end date.
- A certificate is only a name and dates. There is no file, number or score here.
- When it is time to renew, the renewal is added as a new row. The old one is not changed.
- A certificate you earn in **My training** is added for you.

![My certificates, with the dates they end.](help:your-certificates-and-equipment/01-certificates.png)

# Your equipment

Click **Assets** in the left menu. Under **My assets** you see the items assigned to you, such as a computer or headset. If nothing is assigned, it says **Nothing is assigned to you.**

- Open an item to see its tag, its condition when it was handed over and its history.
- When you get an item, you also get a notice in ELEVATE.
- When you return it, HR records the return and writes the condition.
- Assets keep items only. There are no prices or values.

![My assets: the equipment assigned to you.](help:your-certificates-and-equipment/02-assets.png)

# If something looks wrong

Tell HR. For example: a certificate that is missing, or an item listed with you that you already returned. You can use **Contact support**.

# Common mistakes

- **Waiting for a certificate to expire before renewing.** Start early. You get reminders.
- **Giving equipment back without telling HR.** The return has to be recorded in ELEVATE.

# Try it yourself

- Open **Certificates** and check the end date of each.
- Open **Assets** and look at what is with you.
