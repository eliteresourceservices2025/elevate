The **Course studio** is where HR reads and edits the training courses. It is in the **Learning** group of the menu.

# The most important rule

A published version never changes. An edit is always a **new version**, and someone other than the editor has to publish it. This keeps a clear record of what each person learned.

# What you can do

- **Read** every version of a course, module by module.
- **See what changed** between two versions with the comparison.
- **Edit** the wording and the exam questions. Your edit makes a new draft version.
- **Publish** a draft. A reviewer, who is not the editor, publishes it.

If no course is loaded yet, the page says **No course has been loaded yet.**

![Course studio: read, compare and edit the courses.](help:edit-training-courses/01-course-studio.png)

# Client add-on courses

A client can need extra training. Click **New client add-on**, choose an active client that has no add-on yet, and the studio creates a course for it. After it is published:

- People assigned to that client are assigned the add-on, with a due date 30 days after they joined the client.
- Their certificate gets its own reference.
- The client evidence report shows the add-on as well.

# What stays private

The exam answer key never reaches the training app. Leads and clients see only whether someone passed, never scores.

# Never put patient information in a course

Content is plain text and validated. Examples must be invented.

# Common mistakes

- **Expecting an edit to change a published course at once.** It makes a new version that someone else must publish.
- **Using real client or patient examples.** Invent them.
