Your payslip shows how your pay for a pay period was worked out. Only you can open your payslips. Not even a Super Admin can read yours. All names and amounts in this guide are made up.

# When it appears

A payslip appears after the whole pay run it belongs to has been paid. You get a notice that says **Your payslip is ready**. Before that, **My pay** shows **No payslips yet**.

# Steps

1. Click **My pay** in the left menu.
2. Find the **Pay period** you want. The **Net pay** is shown beside it.
3. Click **Download PDF**. It opens in a new tab. You can save or print it.

# What is on it

- The pay for the period, and any days off or late minutes taken off.
- Extra hours, allowances, reimbursements or bonuses.
- Loans or other deductions.
- **Net pay**. If you have more than one pay line, you also see a **Total net pay**.
- The dollar rate used, if you had a dollar allowance.

It does not show tax or contributions, and it does not show your bank details. Names with letters outside English may show a "?" on the PDF.

# If something looks wrong

Write to HR and say which pay period. Do not send the PDF by chat or email: it holds your pay. If **Net pay** shows **Can't be read**, tell HR; it is a problem on our side.

# Common mistakes

- **Looking for a payslip before payment is done.** It appears when everyone on the run is paid.
- **Sharing the PDF.** Keep it private.

# Try it yourself

- Open **My pay** and read the page, even if it is empty.
