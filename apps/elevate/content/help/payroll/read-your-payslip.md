Your payslip shows how your pay for a pay period was worked out. Only you can open your payslips. Not even a Super Admin can read yours. All names and amounts in this guide and its pictures are made up.

# When it appears

A payslip appears after the whole pay run it belongs to has been paid. You get a notice that says **Your payslip is ready**. Before that, **My pay** shows **No payslips yet**.

![My pay before a pay run is paid: it says there are no payslips yet.](help:read-your-payslip/01-no-payslip-yet.png)

# Steps

1. Click **My pay** under **Core HR** in the left menu. You can also click the bell at the top and open the notice **Your payslip is ready**.
2. Find the **Pay period** you want. The **Net pay** is shown beside it.
3. Click **View** to read the payslip on the page. It works in any browser, with nothing to install.
4. Click **Download PDF** to keep or print a copy. It opens in a new tab, and you can save it from there. The **Download PDF** button is also at the top of the page when you are viewing a payslip.

![My pay after the run is paid: the pay period, the net pay, View and Download PDF.](help:read-your-payslip/02-my-pay.png)

The bell shows the notice. It never shows an amount.

![The bell in the header shows the notice: Your payslip is ready.](help:read-your-payslip/04-notice.png)

# What is on it

The payslip is one page, and the PDF looks the same as the page.

- **At the top:** the payslip number, the pay period, the date you were paid and how (for example Remitly).
- **Pay for the period:** your pay for the cutoff, any extra hours with the hours and the rate, any allowance, and anything taken off for days off or late minutes. It ends with **Gross pay**.
- **Added after gross pay:** a dollar allowance (shown in pesos, with the dollars and the rate used), and any reimbursement or bonus.
- **Deductions:** loans or other deductions.
- **Net pay**, in the purple box. If you have more than one pay line, each has its own section and you also see **Total net pay**.
- **Authorized by:** the signature, name and title of the Human Resources Director who signed it, and the date. It is the person who was the signatory on the day your pay run was paid, so it never changes afterwards. If nobody was set up yet on that day, it says **Not signed**.

![A payslip on screen: the period, how it was paid, the pay line by line, the net pay and who signed it.](help:read-your-payslip/03-payslip.png)

It does not show tax or contributions, and it does not show your bank details. Names with letters outside English may show a "?" on the PDF.

# If something looks wrong

Write to HR and say which pay period. Do not send the PDF by chat or email: it holds your pay. If **Net pay** shows **Can't be read**, tell HR; it is a problem on our side.

# Common mistakes

- **Looking for a payslip before payment is done.** It appears when everyone on the run is paid.
- **Sharing the PDF.** Keep it private.

# Try it yourself

- Open **My pay** and read the page, even if it is empty.
