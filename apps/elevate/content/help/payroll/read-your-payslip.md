Your contractor payment statement shows how your pay for a pay period was worked out. (It used to be called the payslip.) Only you can open your statements. Not even a Super Admin can read yours. All names and amounts in this guide and its pictures are made up.

# When it appears

A statement appears after the whole pay run it belongs to has been paid. You get a notice that says **Your contractor payment statement is ready**. Before that, **My payment statements** shows **No statements yet**.

![My payment statements before a pay run is paid: it says there are no statements yet.](help:read-your-payslip/01-no-payslip-yet.png)

# Steps

1. Click **My payment statements** under **Core HR** in the left menu. You can also click the bell at the top and open the notice **Your contractor payment statement is ready**.
2. Find the **Pay period** you want. The **Total payable compensation** is shown beside it.
3. Click **View** to read the statement on the page. It works in any browser, with nothing to install.
4. Click **Download PDF** to keep or print a copy. It opens in a new tab, and you can save it from there. The **Download PDF** button is also at the top of the page when you are viewing a statement.

![My payment statements after the run is paid: the pay period, the total payable compensation, View and Download PDF.](help:read-your-payslip/02-my-pay.png)

The bell shows the notice. It never shows an amount.

![The bell in the header shows the notice: Your contractor payment statement is ready.](help:read-your-payslip/04-notice.png)

# What is on it

The statement is one page, and the PDF looks the same as the page. Everything is in pesos.

- **At the top:** the statement number, the pay period, the date you were paid and how (for example Remitly).
- **Pay for the period:** your pay for the cutoff, any overtime (OT) or holiday pay with the hours and the rate, each allowance under its own name (for example Health allowance), and anything taken off for absences, unpaid sick leave (SL) or late minutes. It ends with **Gross pay**.
- **Leave:** any paid bereavement or PTO days. They show the number of days and take nothing off.
- **Reimbursements, Bonuses and Adjustments:** each in its own section, each line under its own name.
- **Deductions:** loans or other deductions.
- **Total payable compensation**, in the purple box. If you have more than one pay line, each has its own section and you also see **Total payable compensation, all lines**.
- **Authorized by:** the signature, name and title of the Human Resources Director who signed it, and the date. It is the person who was the signatory on the day your pay run was paid, so it never changes afterwards. If nobody was set up yet on that day, it says **Not signed**.

![A contractor payment statement on screen: the period, how it was paid, the pay line by line, the total payable compensation and who signed it.](help:read-your-payslip/03-payslip.png)

It does not show tax or contributions, and it does not show your bank details. Names with letters outside English may show a "?" on the PDF.

# If something looks wrong

Write to HR and say which pay period. Do not send the PDF by chat or email: it holds your pay. If **Total payable compensation** shows **Can't be read**, tell HR; it is a problem on our side.

# Common mistakes

- **Looking for a statement before payment is done.** It appears when everyone on the run is paid.
- **Sharing the PDF.** Keep it private.

# Try it yourself

- Open **My payment statements** and read the page, even if it is empty.
