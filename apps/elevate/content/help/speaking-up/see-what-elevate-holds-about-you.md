# Open My data

Click **My data** in the left menu. The page says: **What ELEVATE holds about you. Only you see this page.**

![My data: everything ELEVATE holds about you, with downloads and a way to ask HR.](help:see-what-elevate-holds-about-you/01-my-data.png)

# What you will see

- **Profile**: your employee number, legal name, preferred name, work email, status, worker type, position, team, manager and dates.
- **Government IDs, bank and pay**: shown masked. To see a full value, reveal it on your profile. Each reveal is recorded.
- **Emergency contacts**, **Client assignments**, **Documents on file** and **Employment history**.
- **Announcements and policies you acknowledged**.
- **Training (ELEVATE Learn)**: dates, status, time spent and certificates. Your answers are not included.
- **Activity on your record**: what you did, and what was done to your record. Other people are not named.

# Download your data

Click **Download PDF** or **Download JSON** at the top to get a copy.

# Ask HR to correct or delete your data

1. Click **Ask HR about your data**.
2. In **What do you need?** choose what you want, and write in **Tell HR what to do**. Do not include passwords or ID numbers.
3. Send it.

HR reviews every request. Some records must be kept by law, so nothing is deleted automatically. You can send another request once the first is handled.

# Common mistakes

- **Typing an ID number or password into the request box.** Never do this.
- **Looking for other people's data.** You only see your own.

# Try it yourself

- Open **My data** and read the activity list.
- Download the PDF.
