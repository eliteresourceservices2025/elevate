**Safe Voice** is for concerns you want to raise without anyone knowing it was you. It is a separate website. It does not know who you are.

# How your privacy is protected

- It does not use cookies, does not ask who you are, and does not keep your device, browser or network address with your report.
- Times are kept only as a calendar day.
- Only a few people named as Safe Voice handlers can read reports.
- Files you attach have their hidden details, such as camera, place and author, removed before they are saved.
- A detail in your own words can still point to you, so avoid details that only you would know.

# Open Safe Voice

Click **Report a concern** at the bottom of the ELEVATE menu, under **Speak up**. It opens in a new tab.

# Send a report

1. Choose **What is this about?** The choices are harassment or bullying, discrimination, retaliation, health, safety or wellbeing, fraud, theft or ethics, manager or leadership conduct, client conduct toward a team member, or something else.
2. Write **What happened?** Say roughly when and where, and who was involved if you are comfortable saying. Up to 8,000 characters. Do not include client or patient information.
3. Add files if you want (**Attach files (optional)**). Up to three, JPG, PNG or PDF, 4 MB in total. Words and images that show on the page itself stay, so check them first.
4. Click **Send report**.

![Safe Voice: say what this is about and what happened. No name is asked.](help:report-something-anonymously/01-send-a-report.png)

# Write down your code and passphrase

After you send, the page says **Your report was sent** and shows a case code and a passphrase. **Write these two down now.** They are the only way to read replies. They are not kept anywhere you can look them up again, and closing the page forgets them.

# Read the reply

1. Open **Safe Voice** again and click **Check a case**.
2. Type your **Case code** and **Passphrase**, then click **Open my case**.
3. You see the **Status**, any **Messages** from the handler, and the **Outcome** when the case is closed.
4. You can **Write a reply or add something**, unless the case is closed. If it is closed, a handler can reopen it.

![To read a reply, type your case code and passphrase.](help:report-something-anonymously/02-check-a-case.png)

# Common mistakes

- **Losing the code.** Nobody can recover it. If you need to, send a new report.
- **Putting your name or a clear detail in the text.** That can identify you.

# Try it yourself

- Find **Report a concern** in your menu and read the page.
