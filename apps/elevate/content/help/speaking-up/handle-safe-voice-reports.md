Only people the Super Admin has named as **Safe Voice handlers** see reports. No role gives it. A Super Admin or HR Admin does not see cases unless they are also named. If you do not see **Safe Voice cases** in your menu, you are not a handler, and this guide is only for information.

# Why it works this way

Reports are anonymous. Nothing that could identify a reporter is stored or logged. Handlers see the reporter's words and nothing else, and they cannot find out who it was.

# Open the cases

Click **Safe Voice cases** in the left menu. You see the list of cases with their status. A card under the list shows counts. A category with fewer than five reports is never shown with its number.

# Read and reply

1. Open a case. It has the report, the messages and any files.
2. Write a reply. If you need an answer from the reporter, ask in the reply. The case then shows **awaiting reporter**.
3. The reporter reads your reply on the Safe Voice website with their case code and passphrase.

You cannot write to a reporter outside the case. Do not try to work out who they are.

# Change the status

A case moves from **new** to **in review** or **awaiting reporter** and finally to **closed**. To close it, choose an outcome:

- Substantiated
- Partly substantiated
- Not substantiated
- No action
- Referred
- Could not be determined

You can add a closing message. A closed case takes no more messages until a handler **reopens** it.

# Notices

Handlers get an in-app notice about new reports and replies, as counts only, about once an hour. No email is ever sent for Safe Voice.

# Privacy rules

- Never write a report's text in chat, email or the audit log. The log records only that you viewed, replied or changed the status.
- Files are cleaned of hidden details before you see them.
- Refer to a case only by its case reference.

# For the Super Admin

At least two people should be named, so one absence never leaves reports unread. Name them under **Settings**, **Roles and access**, or with an invitation. With none named, you get a daily warning.

# Common mistakes

- **Guessing who the reporter is.** Never do this.
- **Closing without an outcome.** It is required.

# Try it yourself

- If you are a handler, open **Safe Voice cases** and read the status list.
