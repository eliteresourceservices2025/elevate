If you made a privacy or security mistake, or you are worried about one, tell us as soon as you notice. It does not matter whether you are sure it is a problem. Nobody is punished for reporting in good faith.

# What to report

- You sent something to the wrong person.
- You lost a device, or it was stolen.
- You clicked a suspicious link.
- You saw information you should not have seen.

# Send a report

1. Click **Privacy incidents** in the left menu.
2. Click **Report an incident**.
3. Answer the questions:
   - **What happened?** In your own words.
   - **Day it happened (or the day you noticed)**
   - **How did you find out?**
   - **Which client is involved?** Leave it blank if none or not sure.
   - **Who else knows?** (optional)
   - **What have you already done about it?** (optional)
   - Whether it may still be happening.
4. Click **Send the report**. You see: **Thank you. Your report was sent.**

# Never include patient details

The form forbids patient names or details. Say what kind of information it was, not the information itself.

# After you send it

HR, Super Admin and the Privacy Officer read your report. Reports and notices never carry your text. You can follow it under **Your reports**, and you can answer questions from the privacy team in the messages. When it is closed, you see the status. Nobody can handle their own report, so someone else on the privacy team does.

# If you want to stay anonymous

Use Safe Voice instead. See "Report something anonymously (Safe Voice)".

# Common mistakes

- **Waiting to be sure.** Report early.
- **Copying the sensitive information into the report.** Describe it instead.
